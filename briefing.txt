🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• AirTrunk Reports "Stronger-Than-Expected" Interest from All Five US
  Hyperscalers — Google, Apple, Meta, Amazon and Microsoft All Actively
  Eyeing Australian AI Data Center Builds, Driven by Green Grid and Land
  Availability, as AI Demand Risks Consuming 13% of Australia's Total Power
  by 2035-36 [HIGH RELEVANCE]: On 7 September 2026, Bloomberg published an
  exclusive report citing AirTrunk Operating Pty Ltd — the Blackstone-owned
  data center platform — revealing it has seen stronger-than-expected interest
  in Australia over the past six to eight months from Google, Apple, Meta
  Platforms, Amazon Web Services, and Microsoft, all of which are evaluating
  Australian sites for AI data center builds; AirTrunk Associate Vice President
  for Energy and Utilities Sabooh Whitelaw cited "political stability, access
  to renewable power, available land" and proximity to major Asian population
  centres in Singapore, Indonesia, South Korea and Japan as the core attraction
  factors drawing this wave of hyperscaler attention; AI-driven data centers
  could consume approximately 13% of Australia's total electricity by 2035-36,
  up sharply from 3% today, straining a grid that regulators have warned cannot
  expand fast enough; Whitelaw simultaneously flagged that delays in grid
  connections and planning approvals risk pushing back Australian projects as
  demand outpaces construction, and community opposition has already surfaced in
  New South Wales (a parliamentary inquiry was triggered by a petition exceeding
  10,000 signatures) and Tasmania; for South Energy, this Bloomberg report
  represents the clearest and most recent direct buying-signal confirmation that
  all five major US hyperscalers are in active Australian site-evaluation mode
  simultaneously — the convergence of political stability, renewable power access
  and available land as stated hyperscaler criteria maps precisely onto South
  Australia's competitive advantage profile, and the grid-delay and community-
  pushback warnings in NSW and Tasmania directly reinforce the scarcity value of
  SA sites that already hold transmission connection agreements, approved
  renewables-firming arrangements and established community social licence.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• ECMC September 2026 Meeting This Month to Consider National Electricity
  Rule Changes Mandating Data Centre Renewable and Firming Offsets — The
  Final Regulatory Instrument Before the 2027 Legislation, Representing the
  Last Window Before NER Changes Impose Hard Obligations on New Connection
  Applications [HIGH RELEVANCE]: Pinsent Masons' legal analysis of the
  Australian data centre regulatory pipeline confirms that National Electricity
  Rule (NER) changes requiring large data centres to offset their electricity
  demand by investing in additional renewable generation and firmed capacity
  are scheduled to be formally considered by the Energy and Climate Change
  Ministerial Council (ECMC) at its September 2026 meeting — a meeting that
  falls this month; this follows the July 2026 ECMC's agreement (with
  Queensland and Northern Territory dissenting) to progress the regulatory
  arrangements, which were informed by the AEMC's August 5 formal four-part
  framework advice (REGO additionality, firming mandate, market registration,
  flexible connection) and subsequently elevated by the National Cabinet's
  unanimous endorsement on 26 August 2026; the ECMC September 2026
  consideration represents the critical pre-legislation regulatory milestone —
  NER changes adopted this month will define the specific technical and
  commercial obligations that new data centre connection applicants will face
  before the 2027 legislative framework takes full effect; for South Energy,
  any project already structured with contracted additionality-compliant
  renewables and BESS firming before the NER changes are adopted this month
  is positioned to demonstrate pre-compliance, whereas projects in the
  connection queue without those arrangements will need to retrofit compliance
  at higher cost and later timeline against a tightening rule set.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]

🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• Pinsent Masons Analysis Confirms Australia's Incoming Data Centre
  Framework Will Substantially Increase Planning, Land Access, Water and
  Environmental Due Diligence Obligations — Raising the Acquisition Premium
  for Sites That Have Already Cleared These Hurdles [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  Pinsent Masons' published analysis of "What Australia's new data centre
  rules will mean for planning and environment" confirms that the federal
  government's commitment to bring data centre location, energy, water and
  land obligations under a single national framework — the first such
  integrated national framework in the world according to the government —
  will materially increase the complexity and cost of site acquisition and
  development approval for greenfield data centre projects; specific
  implications flagged by Pinsent Masons include heightened land access due
  diligence requirements, expanded water access and efficiency obligations,
  new environmental impact assessment triggers for large-load facilities, and
  greater upfront scrutiny of the renewable and firming credentials of proposed
  sites; Prime Minister Albanese's announced intent is to ensure that data
  centre location decisions are shaped by proximity to renewable resources and
  grid capacity, not just to population centres — a principle that directly
  advantages SA's regional renewable-rich corridors over urban fringe sites
  in NSW and Victoria; for South Energy, the Pinsent Masons analysis validates
  the acquisition logic of sites where planning, environmental, water, land
  title, and grid connection due diligence have already been completed and
  where renewable firming credentials are established — the incoming national
  framework converts that pre-completed due diligence stack from a commercial
  advantage into a hard regulatory prerequisite for any buyer operating under
  the early-2027 legislative timetable.

• AirTrunk披露美國五大超大規模雲端客戶——谷歌、蘋果、Meta、亞馬遜及
  微軟——均已積極評估澳大利亞人工智能數據中心選址，買方熱情「超出預期」，
  綠色電網與土地可得性為核心吸引因素，AI需求或致2035-36年數據中心耗電量
  佔澳大利亞總用電量的13%【高度相關】：2026年9月7日，彭博新聞社刊登獨家
  報道，引述黑石集團旗下數據中心運營商AirTrunk的表述——過去六至八個月，
  AirTrunk在澳大利亞業務中來自谷歌、蘋果、Meta、亞馬遜AWS及微軟的問詢
  「超出預期」，五家企業均在積極評估澳大利亞人工智能數據中心的選址方案；
  AirTrunk能源與公用事業副總裁薩布·懷特勞（Sabooh Whitelaw）指出澳大利亞
  具備「政治穩定性、可再生能源獲取渠道和可用土地」，且鄰近新加坡、印度尼西亞、
  韓國和日本等亞洲主要人口中心，是吸引這一波超大規模雲端客戶關注的核心因素；
  AI驅動型數據中心預計至2035-36年將消耗澳大利亞約13%的電力，遠高於當前的3%，
  對監管機構已警告擴建速度不足的電網形成壓力；懷特勞同時警示，電網連接和
  規劃審批的延遲有可能拖累澳大利亞項目進度，而新南威爾士州（一份逾1萬人
  聯署的請願書已觸發議會調查）和塔斯馬尼亞州已出現社區反對聲音；對South
  Energy而言，這份彭博報道是迄今最明確、最新的買方信號確認——美國五大超大
  規模雲端客戶同時處於澳大利亞選址主動評估階段；各方明確的買方標準
  （政治穩定、可再生能源獲取、土地可用性）與南澳州的競爭優勢高度吻合，

  已具備輸電連接協議、批准的可再生能源調峰安排及既有社區認可的場址的稀缺
  性溢價。【高度相關】

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 能源與氣候變化部長理事會（ECMC）本月（2026年9月）召開會議，
  審議強制要求數據中心完成可再生能源及調峰補償的《國家電力規則》修訂案——
  這是2027年立法前的最後一個監管里程碑，也是新連接申請納入強制義務前的
  最後窗口期【高度相關】：品誠梅森律師事務所對澳大利亞數據中心監管管道的
  法律分析確認，要求大型數據中心通過投資額外的可再生發電和調峰容量來抵消
  其用電量的《國家電力規則》（NER）修訂案，已納入能源與氣候變化部長理事會
  （ECMC）2026年9月會議議程，而該會議本月即將舉行；這一進程緊接2026年7月
  ECMC會議（昆士蘭州和北領地反對，其他州通過）同意推進監管安排之後，
  該安排以AEMC於8月5日正式提交ECMC審議的四部分框架建議（REGO附加性要求、
  強制調峰要求、市場主體登記及靈活接入）為基礎，並於2026年8月26日獲全國
  內閣一致背書；ECMC 9月會議的審議是立法前的關鍵監管里程碑——本月通過的
  NER修訂案將界定新數據中心連接申請者在2027年立法框架全面生效前須履行的
  具體技術和商業義務；對South Energy而言，在本月NER修訂案獲批之前已完成
  可再生能源附加性合約安排及BESS調峰結構化的項目，將能夠展示預合規狀態；
  而連接排隊中尚未完成上述安排的項目，將不得不在規則收緊、成本更高的條件下
  事後補齊合規要件。【高度相關】

🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

• 品誠梅森分析確認澳大利亞即將出台的數據中心框架將大幅增加規劃、土地
  獲取、水務及環境盡職調查義務——大幅提升已通過上述審查的場址的收購溢價
  【高度相關】：品誠梅森就「澳大利亞數據中心新規對規劃與環境意味著什麼」
  發布的分析確認，聯邦政府承諾將數據中心選址、能源、水務和土地義務納入
  單一國家框架——根據政府所述，這將是全球首個此類綜合性國家框架——將從根本上
  提升未開發數據中心項目場址獲取和開發審批的複雜性和成本；品誠梅森重點
  提示的具體影響包括：土地獲取盡職調查要求提高、水務獲取和效率義務擴展、
  大型負荷設施的環境影響評估觸發條件增加，以及對擬議場址可再生能源和調峰
  資質的更嚴格前置審查；總理阿爾巴尼斯宣布的政策意圖是確保數據中心選址
  決策由可再生資源和電網容量的鄰近程度決定，而非僅由人口中心決定——這一
  原則直接有利於南澳州可再生資源豐富的區域廊道，而非新南威爾士州和維多利亞
  州的城市邊緣場址；對South Energy而言，品誠梅森的分析驗證了如下收購邏輯：
  對於已完成規劃、環境、水務、土地產權和電網連接盡職調查，且可再生能源調峰
  資質已確立的場址，即將出台的國家框架將把這一預先完成的盡職調查技術棧
  從商業優勢轉化為所有按照2027年初立法時間表運作的買方必須滿足的強制性
  監管前提條件。【高度相關】
