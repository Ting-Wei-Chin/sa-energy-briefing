  validating that SA grid infrastructure is actively absorbing new capacity at scale and that
  ElectraNet's transmission programme is delivering connection bandwidth for large new loads.

  Opposition Forces Withdrawal [HIGH RELEVANCE]: Australia's largest listed property developer
