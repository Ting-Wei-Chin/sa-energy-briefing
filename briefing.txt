📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 15 September 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

  No new updates today.

  [ACTION — 2 DAYS] AEMO non-network options submissions for three newly
  actionable 2026 ISP projects (Brisbane Area 275 kV Reinforcement, Central
  to North Queensland Reinforcement Stage 1, Tasmania REZ Expansion) CLOSE
  THURSDAY 17 SEPTEMBER 2026. Sites with secured SA transmission connections
  and integrated renewable firming should assess eligibility now — this
  deadline is live.

  [MONITORING] Project EnergyConnect full 800 MW bi-directional transfer
  capability between SA and NSW expected to be released by AEMO in Q4 2026.

  [MONITORING] SA's Bundey 330 kV campus (four secured feeder exits, 800 MW
  committed, Goyder Battery 454 MW / 1,814 MWh co-located) remains the only
  pre-secured, large-scale transmission node in any Australian regional market
  capable of absorbing hyperscale demand on the 2026–2028 timeline.

  [MONITORING] ElectraNet NTx public consultation open — landholders, councils,
  and co-development interests along the $3.1B Adelaide–Mid North 330 kV
  corridor should engage the consultation process to position adjacent
  projects and land holdings within the planning and approvals pathway.


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] Goyder Battery at Bundey Substation: Stage 2 contracts
  executed with Tesla (Megapack 3 / Megablock) and UGL; combined capacity
  454 MW / 1,814 MWh; site works commenced August 2026; operational 2028;
  first in world to deploy Tesla Megablock product; reduces construction
  costs up to 40%. Co-located at same Bundey 330 kV node as IREN 800 MW
  campus. Meets "new firm, clean energy" co-location requirement under
  incoming REGO offset framework.

  [MONITORING] SA $32.5 billion pipeline of large-scale renewable energy
  projects (under construction, under assessment, or development-approved as
  at mid-2026) provides deep PPA optionality for buyers meeting REGO offset
  obligations.

  [MONITORING] Gawler Ranges East (~5,200 km²) and Whyalla West (~6,500 km²)
  release areas under the Hydrogen and Renewable Energy Act 2023 — SA's
  largest renewable land release in history; tender closed 28 June 2026;
  outcomes under assessment by the SA Government.


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• Western Downs Digital Park (Zerra DC) Energy Analysis Confirms Scale of
  Unmanaged Hyperscale AI Demand — SA Grid Advantage Strengthened:
  ABC News published a detailed energy-impact analysis on 14 September 2026
  of the 2.16 GW Western Downs Digital Park proposed near Dalby, Queensland
  (Singapore-based Zerra DC); at full four-stage build-out, the development
  application before Western Downs Regional Council would consume approximately
  47 GWh per day — equivalent to 25% of Queensland's current daily electricity
  usage — on a single 725.5-hectare site; the analysis highlights that the
  project has no pre-secured transmission agreement and faces a grid impact
  assessment of unprecedented scale; the contrast with SA's pre-secured Bundey
  330 kV campus (IREN, 800 MW, four secured 330 kV feeder exits, fully
  assessed) is the core buyer-conversion argument — grid-ready capacity in SA
  vs multi-year grid-impact uncertainty in Queensland and NSW.
  Source: ABC News |
  https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-14/western-downs-digital-park-data-centre-energy-queensland/107115082

  [ACTION — TOMORROW] DATA CENTER INVESTMENT SUMMIT AUSTRALIA (DCIS),
  16 September 2026, ICC Sydney. Capital-markets summit for investors,
  hyperscalers, operators, developers and policymakers; themes: AI demand,
  power access, grid connection timelines. All announcements are immediate
  watch-list items for tech buyer signals and competitor site moves.

  [MONITORING] NVIDIA's 2 GW AI factory buildout with eight Australian
  partners (IREN, Firmus, NEXTDC, AirTrunk, CDC, Sharon AI, ResetData,
  Megaport) by 2027 — IREN's 800 MW Bundey SA campus and Firmus Project
  Southgate (Tailem Bend and Port Augusta) are SA-named sites.

  [MONITORING] AEMO ESOO 2026 data centre forecast: 5 TWh in FY2025-26 →
  34 TWh by FY2035-36; ~3% → ~13% of NEM supply. Of 225 projects with
  67 GW of combined connection requests, average grid utilisation ~27%
  in FY26 — cost-allocation exposure accelerating under enacted
  "full capacity charging" rule.

  [MONITORING] US hyperscalers (Google, Apple, Meta, AWS, Microsoft) have
  committed ~A$45 billion to Australian digital infrastructure over 22
  months. Bloomberg (7 September 2026) confirmed all five are actively
  evaluating new Australian AI data centre sites. Global hyperscaler capex
  forecast at US$630–700 billion for 2026, up ~62% on 2025.

  [MONITORING] Anthropic in active discussions for 300–500 MW of Australian
  data centre capacity; CDC front-runner; AirTrunk, NEXTDC, Firmus also in
  process. No signed lease confirmed as of this briefing.


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 regulatory package now formally issued
  (confirmed via King & Wood Mallesons 14 September analysis): (1) mandatory
  REGO-backed renewable energy and firming offset obligation for data centres;
  (2) AEMC "full capacity charging" cost recovery rule; (3) AEMC 30 MW+
  technical grid standards. Only sites with pre-secured firm transmission in
  renewable-rich locations — such as Bundey 330 kV — satisfy all three
  obligations at connection without additional capital investment.

  [MONITORING] Federal mandatory data centre energy and water standards:
  non-retrospective intent confirmed at National Cabinet (26 August 2026).
  Projects obtaining SA Coordinator General approval in 2026 may lock in
  pre-legislation status — directly increasing pipeline value for buyers
  seeking to avoid post-2027 mandatory standards. Federal draft legislation
  targeting early 2027.

  [MONITORING] NSW Electricity Infrastructure Investment Amendment Bill 2026
  consultation closed 14 September 2026. Industry submission volume now under
  analysis; political durability of NSW ministerial gatekeeping unclear.
  SA single-pathway Coordinator General fast-track remains the primary SA
  buyer-conversion argument against NSW.

  [MONITORING] AEMC proposed "full capacity charging" rule and AEMC 30 MW+
  technical standards — both now formally part of the September ECMC package.
  Implication: pre-secured, contracted connections command lower cost exposure
  vs speculative applications.


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] AER has approved ElectraNet's Stage 1b early works contingent
  project for NTx — land acquisition, environmental approvals and procurement
  for the $3.1B Adelaide–Mid North link now proceeding; NTx public
  consultation open (see Category 1). NTx land corridor is the priority
  co-location and adjacency acquisition zone.

  [MONITORING] Partners Group (Swiss PE) acquisition of GreenSquareDC
  (Sydney, Melbourne, Perth sites) confirmed — PE-backed consolidation
  benchmarks Australian data centre land asset values. SA Coordinator
  General-approved regional sites with secured grid connections command
  structural scarcity premium replicable nowhere else in Australia.

  [MONITORING] Transgrid's declaration that western Sydney grid will be
  capacity-constrained by 2033 continues to redirect hyperscale site
  selection to regional markets. SA's Bundey 800 MW node is the primary
  beneficiary. Land adjacent to or within the Bundey catchment area is
  the direct land acquisition target.

  [MONITORING] Firmus Technologies' Project Southgate (Tailem Bend and
  Port Augusta as SA AI factory sites) confirms active site-selection in
  the Murray Mallee corridor and Upper Spencer Gulf — both land acquisition
  target zones for South Energy.

  [MONITORING] Deloitte August 2026 tracker: 33 Australian data centre
  projects, A$104B combined. ~84% of ~9 GW of proposed NEM connections
  still at application stage as at June 2026 — confirming structural premium
  of completed connection agreements.

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年9月15日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

  今日無新動態。

  【行動——還有2天】AEMO就2026年ISP三項新可行動項目（布里斯班
  地區275千伏加強工程、昆士蘭中部至北部加強工程一期、塔斯馬尼亞
  可再生能源區擴建）發出非電網解決方案徵集——截止日期為2026年9月
  17日（星期四）。已獲南澳州輸電連接協議並集成可再生能源調峰的
  場址應立即評估資格——此截止期限為實時行動事項。

  【持續監測】項目EnergyConnect全800兆瓦雙向輸電能力預計由
  AEMO於2026年第四季度發布。

  【持續監測】南澳州邦迪330千伏園區（四個已確認饋線出口、
  800兆瓦承諾容量、高德爾電池454兆瓦/1,814兆瓦時同址部署）
  仍是全澳任何區域市場中唯一能夠在2026至2028年時間窗口內
  承接超大規模需求的預先確認大型輸電節點。

  【持續監測】ElectraNet北部輸電項目（NTx）正式公開諮詢進行中
  ——沿31億澳元阿德萊德至北部中部330千伏走廊持有土地的地主、
  地方議會及希望協同開發的利益相關方應參與諮詢程序，以確保
  相關項目及土地持有進入規劃審批路徑。


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】高德爾電池（邦迪變電站）：二期合同已與特斯拉
  （Megapack 3 / Megablock技術）及UGL簽署；合計容量454兆瓦/
  1,814兆瓦時；工地施工2026年8月已啟動；預計2028年投入運行；
  全球首個部署特斯拉Megablock產品的項目；可降低建設成本達40%；
  與IREN 800兆瓦園區同址接入邦迪330千伏節點；完全符合即將
  強制實施的REGO抵消框架「新增清潔、有保障電力」同址要求。

  【持續監測】南澳州325億澳元大型可再生能源項目管道（截至
  2026年中期在建、在評或已獲批）為超大規模買家履行REGO抵消
  義務提供深度PPA對接選擇。

  【持續監測】《氫能與可再生能源法2023》下高德爾山脈東部
  （約5,200平方公里）及懷阿拉西部（約6,500平方公里）可再生能源
  競標區域——南澳州歷史上最大規模可再生能源土地開放機制；
  招標於2026年6月28日截止；結果由南澳州政府評估中。


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• 西部丘陵數位園區（Zerra DC）能源影響分析揭示失控超大規模
  AI需求規模——南澳州電網優勢進一步凸顯：
  ABC新聞於2026年9月14日發布了針對昆士蘭州道比附近（距布里斯班
  約250公里）擬建中的2.16吉瓦西部丘陵數位園區（新加坡開發商
  Zerra DC）的詳細能源影響分析；按四期全面建成計算，目前正由
  西部丘陵地區議會審議的開發申請每日用電量將達約47吉瓦時——
  相當於昆士蘭州目前日均用電量的25%——僅佔地725.5公頃的
  單一場址即可產生如此規模的電網衝擊；分析指出該項目尚無預確認
  輸電連接協議，面臨前所未有規模的電網影響評估；相較之下，
  南澳州邦迪330千伏園區（IREN 800兆瓦、四個確認330千伏饋線
  出口、已完成全面評估）的優勢一目了然——南澳州提供電網就緒容量，
  而昆士蘭州及新南威爾士州面臨多年電網影響不確定性。
  資料來源：ABC新聞 |
  https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-14/western-downs-digital-park-data-centre-energy-queensland/107115082

  【行動——明天】澳大利亞數據中心投資峰會（DCIS），2026年9月
  16日，悉尼國際會議中心。面向投資者、超大規模雲端商、運營商、
  開發商及政策制定者的資本市場峰會；議題涵蓋AI需求、電力接入、
  電網連接時間表。峰會所有公告均為買家信號及競品場址動態的
  即時關注事項。

  【持續監測】英偉達與8家澳大利亞合作夥伴（IREN、Firmus、
  NEXTDC、AirTrunk、CDC、Sharon AI、ResetData、Megaport）聯合推
  進2027年前2吉瓦人工智能工廠建設——南澳州IREN邦迪800兆瓦
  園區及Firmus「南門計劃」（塔萊姆本德及阿古斯塔港）明確點名。

  【持續監測】AEMO ESOO 2026年數據中心需求預測：2025–26財年
  5太瓦時增至2035–36財年34太瓦時；225個項目合計67吉瓦連接
  申請；2025–26財年平均電網利用率僅約27%——「全容量收費」
  規則落地後成本敞口加速擴大。

  【持續監測】美國超大規模雲端商（谷歌、蘋果、Meta、AWS、
  微軟）在22個月內已向澳大利亞數字基礎設施承諾約450億澳元；
  彭博社（2026年9月7日）確認五家均在積極評估澳大利亞新人工
  智能數據中心選址。全球超大規模雲端商2026年資本開支預測達
  6,300至7,000億美元，較2025年增長約62%。

  【持續監測】Anthropic正積極洽談澳大利亞300–500兆瓦數據中心
  容量；CDC據報為首選；AirTrunk、NEXTDC、Firmus亦在評選範圍。
  截至本簡報，尚無正式租賃合同確認。


📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】ECMC 2026年9月監管一攬子規則已正式頒布
  （金杜律師事務所9月14日分析確認）：（1）數據中心強制性
  REGO抵消義務；（2）AEMC「全容量收費」費用回收規則；
  （3）AEMC 30兆瓦以上連接技術電網標準。只有在可再生能源
  豐富地區持有預確認固定輸電連接的數據中心——例如邦迪330千伏
  節點——才能在接入時不追加額外資本投入即滿足三項合規要求。

  【持續監測】聯邦強制性數據中心能源和用水標準的不追溯效力
  已獲全國內閣（2026年8月26日）確認。2026年取得南澳州總督察員
  規劃批准的項目，可能鎖定立法前身份——對尋求規避2027年後
  強制標準的買家具有直接溢價。聯邦法律目標2027年初提交議會。

  【持續監測】新南威爾士州《電力基礎設施投資修訂法案2026》
  諮詢於9月14日截止。業界提交意見現正分析中；新州部長審批
  門控的政治可持續性尚待觀察。南澳州總督察員單一通道快速審批
  路徑仍是SA項目的核心競品比較論點。

  【持續監測】AEMC「全容量收費」規則及AEMC 30兆瓦以上技術
  標準——均已正式納入ECMC 9月一攬子規則。含義：預先確認的
  合同連接較投機性申請面臨更低成本敞口。


🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測——高度相關】AER已批准ElectraNet NTx一期甲段
  早期工程條件項目申請，土地獲取、環境審批及採購工作已啟動；
  NTx公開諮詢現已開放（見第1類）。NTx線路走廊是優先同址
  選址及相鄰地塊獲取目標區域。

  【持續監測】Partners Group（瑞士私募股權）完成收購
  GreenSquareDC（悉尼、墨爾本、珀斯場址）——印證已持有開發
  審批的場址可獲一線私募買家收購溢價。南澳州擁有電網確認
  連接的總督察員審批區域場址具有全澳不可複製的結構性稀缺溢價。

  【持續監測】Transgrid正式宣布西悉尼電網將於2033年前達到
  容量上限，結構性地將超大規模選址活動從新南威爾士州引向
  區域市場。南澳州邦迪800兆瓦接入節點是這一位移效應的
  主要受益者。毗鄰邦迪輻射範圍的土地是直接對應該結構性
  轉移的土地獲取目標。

  【持續監測】Firmus科技「南門計劃」（塔萊姆本德及阿古斯塔港
  南澳州場址）確認莫雷馬利走廊和上斯賓塞灣地區存在積極選址
  活動，均為South Energy的土地獲取目標地帶。

  【持續監測】德勤2026年8月項目追蹤：33個澳大利亞數據中心項目
  合計1,040億澳元。在申請接入NEM的約9千兆瓦擬建容量中，截至
  2026年6月約84%仍處於申請階段，印證已完成輸電連接協議場址的
  結構性稀缺溢價。
