📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 19 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• AEMO's Q2 2026 Quarterly Energy Dynamics report confirms that 9GW of large-scale data centre
  projects are now queuing for national grid connection, while data centres already operating
  are lifting daytime demand and measurably flattening the solar duck curve; AEMO is
  simultaneously developing new rules to prevent sudden large-load disconnections — treating
  data centre connectivity as a systemic grid management issue for the first time; of the 11
  large-scale projects (5.4GW) actively progressing through the NEM transmission connection
  process, approximately 60% are in NSW and 40% in Victoria, with none yet listed in SA;
  South Energy should use this gap to position SA's already-transmission-connected Bundey
  and Aurora sites as the only gigawatt-scale positions not in a connection queue.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-are-already-helping-flatten-solar-duck-curve-and-there-are-9-gigawatts-more-seeking-grid-connection/
  Source: AEMO | https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/news-updates/digital-demand-surge


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• Fortescue has announced plans to build a dedicated $1 billion green grid specifically to
  power data centres — leveraging its large Australian renewable energy portfolio to offer
  competitively priced, firmed renewable supply outside the standard spot-market and PPA
  route; this signals that large-scale renewable developers are now directly competing for
  data centre offtake by packaging generation, storage, and grid infrastructure into bundled
  offers; South Energy can use this to demonstrate market-level validation of the bundled
  renewable-plus-land model it is positioning in SA, while pointing to SA's superior resource
  quality and existing FERM-backed battery storage as structural advantages over a purpose-built
  private grid. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/cheaper-and-faster-fortescue-to-spend-nearly-1-billion-on-new-green-grid-to-power-data-centres/


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• Moody's Ratings has revised its hyperscaler capex forecast upward by US$85 billion —
  now projecting US$785 billion in 2026 and tracking to approach US$1 trillion by 2027 —
  driven by strong AI compute backlogs, rising adoption, and compute shortages across
  Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Alphabet, Oracle, and CoreWeave; the upward revision (from an
  earlier $700bn estimate) indicates that buyer capital expenditure is accelerating ahead
  of prior consensus and strengthens the investment case for securing large-scale SA land
  positions ahead of peak demand. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/moodys-hyperscaler-capex-forecasts-marked-up-by-85bn-to-close-in-on-1trn-by-2027/

• A major data centre operator has publicly confirmed it is "happy with" the federal
  renewable mandate for data centres, reinforcing that Australia's BYO-renewables requirement
  is not deterring serious hyperscale buyers — it is filtering demand toward renewable-ready
  states; with Queensland and the Northern Territory now pursuing gas-backed alternatives
  (see Policy section), demand is increasingly concentrating toward SA, NSW, and Victoria
  as the compliant-state tier, further strengthening SA's structural position. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-giant-says-its-happy-with-renewable-mandate-but-queensland-and-nt-snub-canberra-and-go-for-gas/


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• NSW has launched a formal data centre fast-track policy framework requiring new data centres
  to source at least 40% of their energy from wind under a binding PPA — in return for a
  guaranteed 75-day development application assessment; the framework is deliberately wind-
  skewed to address NSW's wind under-construction problem, and while it accelerates NSW
  planning, it also introduces a prescriptive constraint that SA does not impose; South Energy
  should actively compare SA's flexible full-renewable grid (100% net renewable by 2027, with
  FERM-backed storage across all technology types) against NSW's wind-only mandate as a key
  differentiator in buyer conversations. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/nsw-launches-new-guidelines-to-fast-track-data-centres/
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/new-south-wales-to-require-new-data-centers-run-40-on-wind-energy-under-new-framework/

• Queensland and the Northern Territory have both formally refused to sign the federal
  ministerial communiqué mandating data centre investment in additional renewables, instead
  backing gas-powered alternatives: the NT government has committed 185 hectares at Weddell
  (~30km from Darwin) to Beetaloo Digital's 2GW gas-powered data centre campus (AU$40 billion),
  fuelled by shale gas from the Beetaloo Basin; separately, a 1GW Northern Territory off-grid
  data centre project ("Project Ares") — powered by 3GW of solar, a 16GWh battery, and
  900–1,100MW of backup gas — has entered the federal environmental approval queue; the
  growing gas-versus-renewables divide between states sharpens SA's structural advantage
  as the most compliant state for hyperscale buyers seeking federal regulatory priority under
  the BYO-renewables framework. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/northern-territory-commits-land-to-gas-backed-data-centre/
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/government-backs-disaster-proposal-to-power-hyperscale-data-centre-with-fracked-beetaloo-gas/
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/plan-to-power-off-grid-data-centre-with-solar-gas-and-a-16-gigawatt-hour-battery-seek-federal-green-tick/


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• Keppel's Connectivity Division has secured exclusive rights to lease a 123-hectare site
  near Morwell, Victoria (approximately 150km east of Melbourne, adjacent to the former
  Hazelwood Power Station) with a gross power capacity of up to 720MW from Australian
  energy landowner Lightwood Group — the site offers pathways to competitively priced
  renewable power, non-potable water for cooling, and low-latency fibre; this is the single
  largest Victorian land deal announced to date and deepens the eastern states pipeline
  competing for the same hyperscale buyer pool South Energy targets, reinforcing the urgency
  of securing buyer commitments for SA positions before Victoria absorbs further capacity.
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/keppel-secures-rights-to-720mw-data-center-campus-outside-melbourne-australia/

• AirTrunk (Blackstone-owned) has agreed in principle to acquire the planned 1GW, AU$5
  billion, six-building data centre campus at Kemps Creek, Western Sydney (706–752 Mamre
  Road) from developer ISPT, with construction forecast to commence in early 2026 — this
  confirms AirTrunk as an active gigawatt-scale land buyer in the Australian market and a
  priority target for South Energy's SA project pipeline; AirTrunk is one of the operators
  most likely to be evaluating a second major Australian site alongside its existing Sydney
  campus expansion, and SA's renewable and grid-readiness credentials directly address its
  ESG and energy security requirements. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/airtrunk-set-to-buy-planned-1gw-data-center-site-in-western-sydney-australia/

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年8月19日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• AEMO《2026年第二季度能源動態季報》確認，目前全國有9吉瓦大型數據中心項目正在排隊
  申請電網接入；已投入運營的數據中心正在提升白天的電力需求，明顯平緩了澳大利亞太陽
  能「鴨形曲線」（duck curve）的低谷效應；與此同時，AEMO正著手制定新規則，防止大型
  負荷突然斷連，首次將數據中心接入視為需要系統化管理的電網安全問題。在正積極推進
  NEM輸電接入流程的11個大型項目（合計5.4吉瓦）中，約60%位於新南威爾士州，40%位於
  維多利亞州，南澳州暫無項目列入；South Energy應以此為切入點，將南澳州已獲輸電接入
  的Bundey和Aurora地塊定位為市場上唯一不需要排隊等候接入的吉瓦級機會。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-are-already-helping-flatten-solar-duck-curve-and-there-are-9-gigawatts-more-seeking-grid-connection/
  資料來源：AEMO | https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/news-updates/digital-demand-surge


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• Fortescue宣布計劃投資10億澳元，專門為數據中心建設一套綠色能源專屬電網——借助其
  龐大的澳大利亞可再生能源項目組合，提供價格具競爭力的有保障可再生電力，繞過傳統現
  貨市場和標準PPA路徑；此舉標誌著大型可再生能源開發商正在直接爭奪數據中心購電合約，
  以「發電+儲能+電網基礎設施」一體化方案參與競爭。South Energy可借此驗證南澳州「可
  再生能源+土地」一體化定位的市場邏輯，同時以南澳州更優越的資源稟賦和經FERM機制認
  可的電池儲能作為差異化優勢。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/cheaper-and-faster-fortescue-to-spend-nearly-1-billion-on-new-green-grid-to-power-data-centres/


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• 穆迪評級（Moody's Ratings）將超大規模科技公司資本支出預測上調850億美元——2026年
  預測值升至7,850億美元，2027年有望逼近1萬億美元；驅動力來自AI算力積壓訂單持續增
  加、採用率加速提升，以及微軟、亞馬遜、Meta、Alphabet、甲骨文和CoreWeave等公司的
  算力短缺問題；這一高於此前7,000億美元市場共識的上調，印證了買家資本支出正在提速，
  進一步強化了在南澳州搶先鎖定大型地塊的投資邏輯。【高度相關】
  資料來源：Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/moodys-hyperscaler-capex-forecasts-marked-up-by-85bn-to-close-in-on-1trn-by-2027/

• 一家主要數據中心運營商公開表示對澳大利亞聯邦可再生能源強制要求「感到滿意」，有力
  印證了澳大利亞「自帶可再生能源」政策並未嚇退主流超大規模買家——反而正在引導需求
  向可再生能源就緒的州份集中；隨著昆士蘭州和北領地轉向天然氣路線（詳見政策板塊），
  買家需求將越來越集中於南澳州、新南威爾士州和維多利亞州構成的「合規州」陣營，進一
  步強化了南澳州的結構性競爭地位。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-giant-says-its-happy-with-renewable-mandate-but-queensland-and-nt-snub-canberra-and-go-for-gas/


📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 新南威爾士州已推出正式的數據中心快速審批政策框架，要求新建數據中心通過具約束力的
  購電協議（PPA）從風電項目採購至少40%的電力，作為回報，州政府承諾在75個工作日內完
  成開發申請評估；該框架刻意向風電傾斜，旨在解決新州風電項目在建量不足的問題；雖然
  新州審批速度加快，但也引入了南澳州不設置的技術限制。South Energy應積極以南澳州
  靈活的全可再生能源電網（2027年實現100%淨可再生電力，FERM機制支持各類儲能技術）
  對比新州僅限風電的強制要求，作為向買家溝通時的核心差異化賣點。【高度相關】
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/nsw-launches-new-guidelines-to-fast-track-data-centres/
  資料來源：Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/new-south-wales-to-require-new-data-centers-run-40-on-wind-energy-under-new-framework/

• 昆士蘭州與北領地雙雙正式拒絕在要求數據中心投資新可再生能源的聯邦部長級公報上署名，
  轉而支持天然氣驅動的替代方案：北領地政府已向Beetaloo Digital公司的2吉瓦天然氣數
  據中心園區提供達爾文附近Weddell（距達爾文約30公里）185公頃的開發用地，總投資規模
  高達400億澳元，計劃以Beetaloo盆地頁岩氣為能源；另有一個名為「Project Ares」的北
  領地1吉瓦離網數據中心項目——以3吉瓦太陽能、16吉瓦時電池儲能及900至1,100兆瓦備用
  天然氣發電為動力——已進入聯邦環境審批程序；州際間天然氣vs.可再生能源的政策鴻溝不
  斷加深，進一步鞏固了南澳州在「自帶可再生能源」聯邦框架下作為最合規州份所具備的
  結構性優勢，以及在聯邦監管審批中獲得優先處理的政策紅利。【高度相關】
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/northern-territory-commits-land-to-gas-backed-data-centre/

  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/plan-to-power-off-grid-data-centre-with-solar-gas-and-a-16-gigawatt-hour-battery-seek-federal-green-tick/


🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

• 凱德（Keppel）連接部門已獲得澳大利亞能源基礎設施地主Lightwood Group在維多利亞州
  莫爾韋爾（Morwell，距墨爾本約150公里，毗鄰前哈澤伍德電廠舊址）123公頃地塊的獨
  家租賃權，最大電力容量可達720兆瓦；該地塊具備獲取具競爭力可再生能源電力、非飲用
  冷卻水及低延遲光纖的條件；這是迄今維多利亞州宣布的最大單一數據中心用地交易，進
  一步擴大了東部各州爭奪South Energy目標買家群的供應管線，強化了南澳州加速鎖定買
  家承諾的緊迫性。
  資料來源：Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/keppel-secures-rights-to-720mw-data-center-campus-outside-melbourne-australia/

• 由黑石集團持有的AirTrunk已與開發商ISPT就悉尼西區坎普斯克里克（Kemps Creek，曼
  里路706–752號）規劃中的1吉瓦、50億澳元六棟樓數據中心園區達成初步收購協議，項目
  預計於2026年初動工——此舉確認AirTrunk是澳大利亞市場上最活躍的吉瓦級土地買家之一，
  也是South Energy應優先接觸的目標；AirTrunk極有可能正在評估悉尼現有園區擴建之外的
  第二個澳大利亞主要站點，而南澳州的可再生能源資質和電網就緒度恰好直接滿足其ESG和
  能源安全要求。【高度相關】
  資料來源：Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/airtrunk-set-to-buy-planned-1gw-data-center-site-in-western-sydney-australia/
