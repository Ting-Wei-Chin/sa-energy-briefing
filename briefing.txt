📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• Australia's Data Centre Boom Flagged to Risk 26% Increase in Household Power Bills
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]: New analysis warns that without direct cost assignment frameworks — such
  as SA's proposed "new energy for new demand" obligation — the wave of hyperscale data centre
  investment in Australia could add up to 26% to national household electricity bills by
  pushing network upgrade costs onto all consumers. The finding arrives nine days before the
  PM&C AI data centre consultation closes (9 October 2026).
  Source: Tech Insider Australia | https://tech-insider.org/au/australia-data-centre-boom-power-bill-2026/
  [HIGH RELEVANCE — SA's proposed Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act explicitly mandates
  "new energy for new demand," assigning all new network costs directly to data centre
  developers rather than socialising them across SA consumers. This policy structure is now
  nationally differentiated: SA's framework pre-empts the cost-shift concern that this
  analysis identifies as a 26% risk. For South Energy's developer and buyer clients, SA
  Coordinator-General-approved sites are currently the only Australian sites where federal
  mandatory standards, state cost-assignment obligations, and community-impact concerns are
  simultaneously addressed by the approval framework.]
