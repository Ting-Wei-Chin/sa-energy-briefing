📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• AEMC Actively Reviewing National Electricity Rule Changes for 30MW+ Data
  Centre Technical Standards — Fault Ride-Through Requirement in Focus [HIGH
  RELEVANCE]:
  National Electricity Rule change requests addressing technical standards for large
  data centres (30 MW and above) are currently under active AEMC review in September
  2026, with the mandatory fault ride-through standard identified as the central
  requirement — designed to prevent simultaneous grid disconnection events of the
  type that caused cascading blackouts internationally. Federal legislation is
  targeted for introduction to Parliament in early 2027. Sites with pre-secured
  transmission connections and compliant grid interface specifications — including
  the Bundey 330 kV campus — are already structurally aligned with the incoming
  rule, while prospective applications face redesign and reconnection cost exposure.
  Source: PV Tech | https://www.pv-tech.org/australias-data-centre-energy-rules-take-shape-as-aemc-publishes-framework-and-nsw-moves-to-control-grid-access/
  [HIGH RELEVANCE — technical standards review is live; pre-compliant connections
  command increasing premium as rule change timeline accelerates]

• Australia to Legislate Net-Generator Requirement for Large Data Centres —
  Federal Draft Legislation Confirmed Targeting Early 2027:
  Following PM Albanese's July 15 announcement, Energy Storage News and PV Tech
  confirm Australia will legislate a mandatory net-generator requirement for
  large-scale data centres — operators must put at least as much power into the
  grid as they consume, and must underwrite new firmed renewable supply. Federal
  draft legislation is confirmed to target early 2027 Parliament introduction.
  SA sites with co-located firmed renewable generation (Bundey + Goyder Battery)
  are the only currently pipeline-ready Australian sites that satisfy the
  net-generator obligation structurally at connection, without retrofitting.
  Source: Energy Storage News | https://www.energy-storage.news/australia-moves-to-legislate-net-generator-requirement-for-large-scale-data-centres/
  [HIGH RELEVANCE — direct impact on buyer site-selection criteria; SA Bundey
  co-location advantage is a statutory compliance differentiator from 2027]

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 regulatory package now formally issued
  (confirmed via King & Wood Mallesons 14 September analysis): (1) mandatory
  REGO-backed renewable energy and firming offset obligation for data centres;
  (2) AEMC "full capacity charging" cost recovery rule; (3) AEMC 30 MW+
  technical grid standards. Only sites with pre-secured firm transmission in
  renewable-rich locations — such as Bundey 330 kV — satisfy all three
  obligations at connection without additional capital investment.

  [MONITORING] Federal mandatory data centre energy and water standards:
  non-retrospective intent confirmed at National Cabinet (26 August 2026).
  Projects obtaining SA Coordinator General approval in 2026 may lock in
  pre-legislation status — directly increasing pipeline value for buyers
  seeking to avoid post-2027 mandatory standards. Federal draft legislation
  targeting early 2027.

  [MONITORING] SA Government's Proposed Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act —
  Dedicated Legislation Framework Confirmed (announced June 2026). Data centres
  to be designated essential infrastructure; Coordinator-General as single
  decision-making authority; "new energy for new demand" requirement; currently
  in consultation and drafting stage.

• AEMC正積極審查針對30兆瓦以上數據中心的國家電力規則修改——故障穿越
  技術標準為核心焦點 【高度相關】：
  解決30兆瓦及以上大型數據中心技術標準的國家電力規則變更申請，目前正由
  AEMC於2026年9月主動審查，其中強制性電網故障穿越標準（Low Voltage Ride
  Through，LVRT）為核心要求——旨在防止大量數據中心同步脫網引發與國際案例
  類似的連鎖停電事故。聯邦立法預計2027年初提交議會。持有預確認輸電連接
  且符合電網接口技術規範的場址——包括邦迪330千伏園區——已在結構上符合
  即將頒布的規則，而投機性申請場址面臨重新設計及額外接入成本敞口。
  來源：PV Tech | https://www.pv-tech.org/australias-data-centre-energy-rules-take-shape-as-aemc-publishes-framework-and-nsw-moves-to-control-grid-access/
  【高度相關——技術標準審查正在進行；隨著規則修改時間表加快，
  預先合規接入場址的溢價優勢持續擴大】

• 澳大利亞將立法要求大型數據中心履行「淨發電商」義務——聯邦立法草案
  確認目標2027年初提交議會：
  繼聯邦總理阿爾巴內塞7月15日宣布後，Energy Storage News及PV Tech確認
  澳大利亞將正式立法，強制要求大型數據中心成為「淨發電商」——運營商
  必須向電網輸入不少於其消耗量的電力，並須自行承擔新增有保障可再生
  能源供應。聯邦立法草案確認目標2027年初提交議會審議。在同址可再生
  能源方面，目前管道中唯一能在接入時無需額外改造即可在結構上滿足
  「淨發電商」義務的澳大利亞場址，是邦迪加上高德爾電池的組合。
  來源：Energy Storage News | https://www.energy-storage.news/australia-moves-to-legislate-net-generator-requirement-for-large-scale-data-centres/
  【高度相關——直接影響買家選址標準；南澳州邦迪同址優勢將從2027年起
  成為法定合規差異化優勢】

  【持續監測】ECMC 2026年9月監管一攬子規則已正式頒布（金杜律師事務所
  9月14日分析確認）：（1）數據中心強制性REGO抵消義務；（2）AEMC「全容量
  收費」費用回收規則；（3）AEMC 30兆瓦以上連接技術電網標準。只有在可再生
  能源豐富地區持有預確認固定輸電連接的數據中心——例如邦迪330千伏節點——
  才能在接入時不追加額外資本投入即滿足三項合規要求。

  【持續監測】聯邦強制性數據中心能源和用水標準的不追溯效力已獲全國內閣
  （2026年8月26日）確認。2026年取得南澳州總督察員規劃批准的項目，可能
  鎖定立法前身份。聯邦法律目標2027年初提交議會。

  【持續監測】南澳州政府擬議《數據中心與AI基礎設施法》——專項立法框架確認
  （2026年6月宣布）。數據中心將被指定為基本基礎設施；總督察員為重大項目
  單一決策機構；「新需求配新能源」要求；目前處於諮詢及起草階段。
