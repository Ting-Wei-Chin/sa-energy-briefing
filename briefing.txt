📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 23 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• Project EnergyConnect — the 900km NSW-SA interconnector — has reached its full 800MW
  transfer capability milestone targeted for mid-2026, following completion of Stage 2
  construction by Transgrid and successful interstate testing; this doubles the
  interconnection capacity that South Australian generators can access into NSW demand
  centres, materially improving the firming economics for any SA-based project (including
  South Energy sites) that relies on interstate arbitrage to guarantee supply to large loads
  during low-renewable periods. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Kalkine / Project EnergyConnect | https://kalkine.com.au/news/general-news/energyconnect-nears-the-finish-line-as-data-centres-push-australias-grid-to-its-limits
  Source: Project EnergyConnect | https://www.projectenergyconnect.com.au/article.php?id=78

• AEMC released publicly on 5 August 2026 its framework advice to the Energy and Climate
  Change Ministerial Council (delivered 28 July), proposing a firm 30MW threshold as a
  legally binding NER standard for large inverter-based loads connecting to distribution or
  transmission systems — requiring data centres to meet disturbance ride-through and power
  recovery standards; the ECMC will consider the rule change requests in September 2026,
  with national legislation targeting early 2027; for South Energy, projects that already
  include integrated generation and BESS assets are structurally advantaged over standalone
  data-centre connections, as they inherently satisfy the technical performance requirements
  the new standards impose. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: AEMC | https://www.aemc.gov.au/news-centre/media-releases/aemc-proposes-new-grid-standards-data-centre-connections
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/aemc-proposes-new-grid-standards-for-data-centres/
  Source: PV Tech | https://www.pv-tech.org/australias-data-centre-energy-rules-take-shape-as-aemc-publishes-framework-and-nsw-moves-to-control-grid-access/


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• The SA Government released two new renewable energy licence areas in January 2026 —
  Gawler Ranges East (approx. 5,200 km² on the Upper Eyre Peninsula) and Whyalla West
  (approx. 6,500 km² in the Upper Spencer Gulf region) — with feasibility licence
  applications open from 30 March 2026 and closed on 28 June 2026; results of the tender
  process are now pending, and the winning developers will gain exclusive feasibility rights
  over areas that overlap with South Energy's core portfolio corridors; the tender adds
  potential new generation partners in the region, and any awarded licences will signal
  which developers are building critical mass in the same zones South Energy is actively
  targeting — requiring close monitoring for partnership and competition implications.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: SA Government Energy & Mining | https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/south-australia-powers-up-vast-new-renewable-energy-opportunities
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/south-australia-opens-new-areas-for-renewables-investment/

• AEMO's battery storage data shows Australia's grid-connected battery fleet tripled its
  daily load-shifting contribution following 4.4GW of new capacity coming online since
  Q1 2025; in South Australia, grid-forming BESS assets are now a priority system
  strength mechanism following the commencement of Grid Reference Transitional Services
  from 1 July 2026, reinforcing the value of co-locating storage with data-centre loads
  as both a grid service and a cost-offset mechanism — directly applicable to South
  Energy's integrated project model.
  Source: Energy Storage News | https://www.energy-storage.news/australias-battery-storage-fleet-triples-daily-load-shifting-as-4-4gw-comes-online-in-q1-2026-says-aemo/

🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• AEMO's June 2026 assessment disclosed that 5.4GW of data centre capacity is currently
  progressing through the national transmission connection process across the NEM, with
  approximately 40% of that capacity located in Victoria and 60% in NSW; South Australia
  is not yet a major component of the formal connection pipeline by volume, which creates
  a first-mover and capacity-access advantage for projects that have already secured
  transmission connection agreements (such as IREN Bundey) or are in advanced planning
  stages — reinforcing the strategic value of South Energy's sites in a state with
  significantly less connection queue congestion than the eastern seaboard. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Australia Data Centre Index / CertifiedStrategic | https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/aemo-5-4gw-australian-data-centre-pipeline-june-2026
  Source: AEMO | https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/news-updates/digital-demand-surge

• No new hyperscaler announcements specific to South Australia were identified today. The
  broader backdrop of $630B in global Big Four capex for 2026 and approximately A$45B in
  confirmed Australian digital infrastructure commitments remains unchanged from prior
  briefings, as does SA's position as the only fully renewable-firmed NEM jurisdiction.


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• The AEMC's August 5 framework advice to the ECMC — which will be formally considered
  at the September 2026 ministerial council meeting — requires data centres to: (1) offset
  electricity consumption using certificates linked exclusively to NEW renewable generation
  via the REGO scheme (not existing capacity), closing the "green washing" arbitrage
  loophole; and (2) contract for firming capacity alongside renewable certificates; federal
  legislation introducing these requirements is targeted for Parliament in early 2027;
  critically, this framework structurally advantages integrated energy-plus-compute projects
  — where new firmed renewable generation is co-developed with the data centre load — over
  simple buy certificates from the grid approaches, directly validating South Energy's
  integrated project development model. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: AEMC | https://www.aemc.gov.au/news-centre/media-releases/aemc-proposes-new-grid-standards-data-centre-connections
  Source: Energy Storage News | https://www.energy-storage.news/australia-advances-data-centre-energy-policy-on-three-fronts-as-battery-storage-emerges-as-key-compliance-tool/
  Source: Mallesons | https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/big-day-for-data-centres.html

• The National Cabinet consideration of the Albanese Government's mandatory AI data centre
  standards (announced 15 July 2026) is scheduled for August 2026 — no formal National
  Cabinet decision has been publicly confirmed as of today (23 August); the framework
  requires large-scale AI data centres to match energy demand with new supply and fund
  their own grid connections, with legislation targeting early 2027; once a National
  Cabinet decision is announced, expect an acceleration of buyer due diligence activity
  for sites that already demonstrate compliance with new energy and water requirements.
  Source: MLQ AI News | https://mlq.ai/news/australia-mandates-ai-data-centers-be-net-power-generators-sets-water-efficiency-rules/
  Source: Mycelium 365 | https://mycelium365.com.au/blog/australia-new-ai-standards-15-july-2026-announcement


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• The SA Government announced on 23 June 2026 a proposed Data Centre and AI
  Infrastructure Act that would designate data centres as essential state infrastructure,
  empower the Coordinator-General to assume decision-making authority for major projects,
  create a dedicated expedited approvals pathway, and require developers to confirm SA
  Water supply availability and Technical Regulator power-system compliance before approval;
  the act has not yet been drafted or tabled in Parliament, but its policy intent —
  streamlined approvals for energy-matched, water-confirmed projects — maps precisely onto
  South Energy's integrated project structure, and developers who front-load SA Water and
  Technical Regulator engagement now will be best placed for fast-tracked decisions once
  the act is enacted. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Capital Brief | https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/new-energy-for-new-demand-sa-plans-data-centre-and-ai-infrastructure-act-47dfa4af-f755-494c-981a-121fed28eb67/
  Source: W.Media | https://w.media/south-australia-proposes-dedicated-data-centre-legislation/
  Source: InDaily | https://www.indailysa.com.au/news/just-in/2026/06/23/we-need-data-centres-government-all-in-on-ai-revolution

• The ElectraNet 2026 TAPR also identifies two transmission upgrades beyond NTx that
  were not highlighted in previous briefings: the Eyre Peninsula Upgrade (expanding the
  Cultana–Yadnarie lines serving the Upper Spencer Gulf/Eyre Peninsula industrial corridor)
  and the South East Expansion — both of which expand transmission headroom in regions
  where South Energy's land portfolio is concentrated; monitoring AEMO's Project Assessment
  process for these projects will be important for timing site development applications to
  align with confirmed grid capacity timelines. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: PV Magazine Australia | https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• 「EnergyConnect項目」——全長900公里的新南威爾士州至南澳州跨州輸電聯絡線——已於2026年
  年中實現全額800兆瓦輸電能力里程碑，由Transgrid完成第二期建設並通過跨州聯調測試後正式達
  成；這一里程碑使南澳州發電商接入新南威爾士州需求中心的聯絡線容量翻倍，從根本上改善了
  南澳州一體化項目（包括South Energy地塊）在可再生能源出力低谷期依靠跨州套利為大型負荷
  保供的經濟可行性。【高度相關】

• 澳大利亞能源市場委員會（AEMC）於2026年8月5日公開發布其向能源與氣候變化部長理事會
  （ECMC）提交的框架建議（建議於7月28日遞交），提議在《國家電力規則》（NER）中設定30兆
  瓦的強制門檻，作為大型逆變器負荷接入配電或輸電系統的法定標準——要求數據中心滿足擾動穿
  越及功率恢復技術規範；ECMC擬於2026年9月審議相關規則修改申請，全國立法目標為2027年初；
  對South Energy而言，已整合發電及電池儲能資產的項目在結構上優於單純接入電網的數據中心，
  因前者天然符合新標準所要求的技術性能規範。【高度相關】

  East，約5,200平方公里，位於上艾爾半島）及懷阿拉西區（Whyalla West，約6,500平方公里，
  位於上斯賓塞灣地區）——可行性授權許可申請於2026年3月30日開放，截至2026年6月28日截止；
  招標結果目前仍在審議中，中標開發商將獲得這兩個區域的排他性可行性研究權，而上述地區正與
  South Energy土地組合的核心廊道高度重疊；招標結果一旦公布，將揭示哪些開發商正在South
  Energy重點布局的相同地帶積累資源——需密切關注合作及競爭動向。【高度相關】

🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• AEMO 2026年6月評估顯示，目前全國輸電接入流程中共有5.4吉瓦的數據中心容量正在推進，其
  中約60%位於新南威爾士州，40%位於維多利亞州；南澳州尚未成為全國正式接入管線的主要組成
  部分，這反而為已取得輸電接入協議（如IREN班迪項目）或處於高級規劃階段的項目創造了「先
  行者優勢」與「容量接入優勢」——相比東部沿岸接入隊列嚴重積壓的州份，南澳州的戰略價值
  由此得到進一步強化。【高度相關】

  元、澳大利亞確認承諾約450億澳元數字基礎設施投資的整體背景，以及南澳州作為NEM唯一完全
  可再生能源保障供電管轄區的地位，均與前期簡報一致，無新變化。


📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• AEMC於8月5日向ECMC提交的框架建議——擬於2026年9月部長理事會正式審議——要求數據中心：
  （1）通過《可再生電力保證來源》（REGO）計劃，以來源與【新增】可再生發電掛鉤的憑證抵
  消用電量（不得購買現有存量電力的憑證），封堵「洗綠」套利漏洞；（2）在採購可再生電力憑
  證的同時配套簽訂保供容量合同；相關要求立法目標為2027年初進入國會；至關重要的是，該框
  架從結構上有利於能源與算力一體化項目——即新增有保障可再生發電與數據中心負荷協同開發的
  模式——相較於單純通過市場購買憑證的路徑，直接驗證了South Energy一體化項目開發模式的
  商業邏輯。【高度相關】

🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

• 南澳州政府於2026年6月23日宣布擬立《數據中心與AI基礎設施法》（Data Centre and AI
  Infrastructure Act），將數據中心列為州級重要基礎設施，授權「總協調人」（Coordinator-
  General）對重大項目行使審批決策權，建立專項快速審批通道，並規定開發商須在獲批前取得

  構高度契合；提前完成SA Water及技術監管局接洽工作的開發商，將在法案生效後佔據最有利的
  快速審批地位。【高度相關】
