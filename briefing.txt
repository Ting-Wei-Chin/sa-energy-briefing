📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 18 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• IREN (formerly Iris Energy) has signed a transmission connection agreement with ElectraNet
  for an 800MW data centre campus at the Bundey substation, approximately 78 miles north-east
  of Adelaide — securing four 330kV feeder exits capable of delivering up to 800MW without
  requiring any network upgrades, with energisation on track from 2028; this is the largest
  announced data centre in the Asia-Pacific region and confirms that SA's existing high-voltage
  network can accommodate gigawatt-scale loads without costly grid augmentation — a core value
  proposition South Energy should be leading with in buyer conversations. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center News Asia | https://datacenternews.asia/story/iren-signs-deal-for-800mw-south-australia-data-centre
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/iren-targets-australia-for-800mw-data-center-campus/
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/us-ai-firm-to-build-australias-biggest-data-centre-in-sa/
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/

• ElectraNet's 2026 Transmission Annual Planning Report (TAPR) reveals that interest to
  connect to South Australia's transmission network is at its highest level in decades; peak
  demand is forecast to double from 3.3GW today to more than 6.5GW within 15 years; Project
  EnergyConnect Stage 2 (adding 800MW of bi-directional interstate capacity) is on schedule
  for completion in Q4 2026; and the Northern Transmission Project (NTx) — extending the
  network into northern and eastern SA — is in active planning with a Project Assessment
  Conclusions Report expected by December 2026, alongside the Eyre Peninsula Upgrade
  anticipated by 2027/28 — all expanding the pool of transmission-connected land parcels
  South Energy can present to buyers. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ElectraNet | https://electranet.com.au/resource/2026-tapr-transmission-annual-planning-report/
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/

• AEMO has introduced a standalone demand forecast category specifically for data centres —
  separating them from general commercial load for the first time — giving South Energy a
  structured, regulator-endorsed framework to quantify the grid capacity that SA data centre
  land positions can absorb and present to buyers with greater credibility.
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/australian-energy-market-operator-creates-standalone-forecast-category-for-data-centers/


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• The South Australian Government has opened new land areas for renewables investment under
  its Renewable Energy Zone (REZ) framework, expanding the supply of development-ready
  renewable land available for co-location or PPA structuring alongside South Energy's data
  centre project pipeline; SA's total large-scale renewable energy investment pipeline has
  grown to $32.5 billion — up from $19 billion when the Hydrogen and Renewable Energy Act
  was introduced in 2023 — significantly deepening the firmed renewable supply a hyperscale
  buyer can anchor a long-term PPA against. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/south-australia-opens-new-areas-for-renewables-investment/
  Source: SA Government | https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/south-australia-powers-up-vast-new-renewable-energy-opportunities

• South Australia's first round of Firm Energy Reliability Mechanism (FERM) tender has
  awarded agreements to six battery energy storage projects totalling 517MW / 4,136MWh of
  dispatchable energy, directly strengthening the firmed renewable supply stack underpinning
  24/7 clean energy commitments that hyperscale buyers require; this reduces the LDES policy
  risk flagged in earlier briefings as a buyer concern.
  Source: SA Government | https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/industry/hydrogen-and-renewable-energy/leading-the-green-economy


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• Meta has formally established a new division called "Meta Compute" and announced plans to
  build tens of gigawatts of AI data centre capacity this decade — including multiple
  gigawatt-plus scale campus developments — with CEO Mark Zuckerberg framing the investment
  as building toward "superintelligence" and spending "hundreds of billions of dollars" on
  compute; Meta's capex for 2026 is guided at US$115–135 billion, significantly above 2025,
  making Meta one of the most active gigawatt-scale buyers South Energy should be pursuing
  with an SA land and energy proposal. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/meta-establishes-meta-compute-plans-multiple-gigawatt-plus-scale-ai-data-centers/

• CDC Data Centres has signed what it describes as the largest data centre deal in Australian
  history — a 555MW, 30-year contract (extendable by 20 years) with an undisclosed US
  investment-grade client, taking CDC's contracted capacity above 1GW — confirming that
  US hyperscale buyers are signing decade-long Australian capacity commitments and that the
  deal scale and tenure now exceeds anything previously seen in this market.
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/cdc-signs-largest-ever-aussie-data-center-deal-to-provide-555mw-for-us-client/

• Anthropic is actively seeking data centre leasing arrangements in Europe and Australia —
  adding the leading AI research company to the list of hyperscale-class buyers now in the
  Australian market seeking to secure capacity; South Energy should proactively position SA
  land projects to Anthropic's infrastructure procurement team given the company's explicit
  preference for renewable-powered infrastructure. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/anthropic-seeks-data-center-leasing-deals-in-europe-and-australia/


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• The Australian Government has published formal national "expectations" for data centres and
  AI infrastructure — a five-point framework covering national interest, energy transition
  (including underwriting new renewables and paying the full cost of grid connectivity),
  water sustainability, skills and jobs, and research/innovation; development proposals meeting
  these expectations will be prioritised under federal regulatory assessments, creating a
  meaningful approval-speed advantage for SA projects that already align with the framework.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/australian-govt-publishes-expectations-for-data-centers-and-ai-infrastructure-projects/
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/australia-has-set-new-expectations-for-ai-data-centres/
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/byo-renewables-new-national-principles-set-bar-for-data-centre-energy-and-water-use/

• The Prime Minister has stated that data centres will face a "legal obligation" to
  underwrite new renewable energy supply to offset their electricity demand — going further
  than the principles framework — and legislation is now being foreshadowed; Microsoft
  and Anthropic have both signed MoUs in April 2026 committing to the expectations, while
  Queensland is withholding support for the national framework, deepening SA's structural
  advantage as the state most naturally aligned with the BYO-renewables requirement. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-will-have-legal-obligation-to-byo-renewables-says-pm-under-proposed-national-ai-standards/
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centre-power-struggle-as-hold-out-states-oppose-federal-push-for-byo-renewables/

• AEMO has published its 2026 Integrated System Plan (ISP), reaffirming that renewable energy
  — connected by new transmission, firmed by storage, and backed up by gas — is Australia's
  least-cost path forward through to 2050; the ISP underpins major transmission investment
  decisions and confirms the long-term grid infrastructure SA data centre projects will rely on.
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• 1414 Degrees, through its subsidiary SiliconAurora, has signed a non-binding Heads of
  Agreement with an unnamed data centre partner for the development of up to 1GW of AI data
  centre infrastructure at the Aurora Energy Precinct, approximately 20km north of Port
  Augusta — offering a 1,580-hectare site with up to 900MW of solar generation potential, an
  approved 140MW/280MWh BESS, existing high-voltage connection infrastructure, and fibre and
  water access; the phased development begins with a 17MW campus on the existing 33kV
  connection, expanding to 200MW via a 275kV upgrade, then toward gigawatt scale — this is a
  direct comparable transaction for South Energy's SA pipeline and signals that unnamed
  hyperscale-class operators are now actively transacting on large-scale SA land positions
  with co-located renewable energy. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/1414-degrees-signs-deal-for-up-to-1gw-ai-campus-at-aurora-energy-precinct-in-south-australia/
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/1414-degrees-inks-major-data-centre-deal-in-south-australia/
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/thermal-battery-company-chasing-1-gw-data-centre-deal-for-site-once-earmarked-for-giant-solar-towers/

  plans for a 1GW campus and a separate 202MW campus — by developers competing for the same
  hyperscale buyer pool South Energy targets; this eastern-states pipeline is growing rapidly
  and reinforces the urgency of South Energy differentiating its SA positions on grid
  readiness, renewable credentials, and connection certainty rather than competing on proximity
  to Sydney.

☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• 南澳州政府在其可再生能源區（REZ）框架下開放了新的土地區域供可再生能源投資，擴大了
  可與South Energy數據中心項目管線配套開發或用於購電協議（PPA）結構設計的開發就緒型
  可再生能源用地供應；南澳州大型可再生能源投資管線總規模已增至325億澳元——較2023年
  《氫能與可再生能源法》出台時的190億澳元大幅提升——顯著深化了超大規模買家可依托長
  期PPA錨定的有保障可再生能源供應體量。【高度相關】
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/south-australia-opens-new-areas-for-renewables-investment/
  資料來源：南澳州政府 | https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/south-australia-powers-up-vast-new-renewable-energy-opportunities

• 南澳州固定能源可靠性機制（FERM）首輪招標已向六個電池儲能項目授予協議，合計容量達
