🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• US AI infrastructure company IREN has signed a transmission connection agreement with
  ElectraNet for an 800MW data centre campus at Bundey, approximately 75km north-east of
  Adelaide — securing four 330kV feeder exits at the Bundey substation without requiring any
  network upgrades, with energisation targeted from 2028; the ~$10 billion project is the
  largest data centre announced in Australia and the Asia-Pacific region, and critically
  demonstrates that specific SA sub-transmission nodes already have headline connection
  capacity available to absorb hyperscale load without the costly network augmentation that
  makes eastern-state projects economically difficult. South Energy should immediately map
  other ElectraNet substations with comparable spare capacity and proximity to renewable
  zones to identify the next acquisition targets before comparable connection agreements
  are signed. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/us-ai-firm-to-build-australias-biggest-data-centre-in-sa/
  Source: Data Center News Asia | https://datacenternews.asia/story/iren-signs-deal-for-800mw-south-australia-data-centre
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/

• Nationally, 9GW of data centre projects are currently in the transmission connection
  queue, and SA-based projects are already demonstrating a grid-stabilising effect by
  helping flatten the state's solar duck curve, making them operationally attractive to
  AEMO and more likely to receive expedited approvals; ElectraNet is separately consulting
  on a proposal to charge data centres for their full contracted connection capacity whether
  or not it is used — meaning early connection agreements at favourable legacy tariffs are
  increasingly time-sensitive and strategically valuable. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-are-already-helping-flatten-solar-duck-curve-and-there-are-9-gigawatts-more-seeking-grid-connection/
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/network-seeks-to-charge-data-centres-for-full-grid-connection-capacity-whether-they-use-it-or-not/

• ElectraNet's 2026 Transmission Annual Planning Report (TAPR) identifies the Northern
  Transmission Project (NTx) — a potential $3.1 billion investment linking Adelaide to
  SA's wind- and solar-rich northern regions — as the critical enabling infrastructure for
  the next wave of large-load industrial development; the NTx Project Assessment Draft
  Report (PADR) is now open for stakeholder input, and the route corridor will directly
  determine which northern SA land parcels gain premium grid-connected status over the
  coming planning horizon. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/
  Source: ElectraNet NTx PADR | https://electranet.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/NTx-PADR-fact-sheet.pdf
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/south-australia-eyes-new-transmission-line-to-support-industrial-demand-drawn-to-100-pct-renewables/

  availability of direct high-voltage transmission access as the decisive factors; the
  project's ~$10 billion capex and 800MW scale establish SA as the benchmark location for
  future hyperscale AI builds in Australia and represent the most direct buying signal yet
  that tech firms will pay a substantial premium for SA renewable-grid sites with secured
  transmission access. [HIGH RELEVANCE]

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• The Australian Government's Department of Industry, Science, and Resources has published
  a formal set of "expectations" for data centre developers under the National AI Plan —
  the five expectations are: (1) prioritise Australia's national interest; (2) support the
  energy transition (BYO firmed renewables, net energy generation into the grid);
  (3) use water sustainably; (4) invest in Australian skills and jobs; and (5) strengthen
  research, innovation, and local capability; projects that closely align will receive
  prioritised regulatory approvals while non-compliant projects will not receive
  preferential treatment — formally institutionalising the two-speed regulatory regime in
  which SA's renewable-compliant projects hold a structural approval advantage, reinforcing
  South Energy's value proposition to tech company buyers. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/australia-has-set-new-expectations-for-ai-data-centres/
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/australian-govt-publishes-expectations-for-data-centers-and-ai-infrastructure-projects/

• AEMO has published the final 2026 Integrated System Plan (ISP), the 25-year roadmap for
  the National Electricity Market, explicitly identifying data centre demand growth as one
  of the dominant forces reshaping the SA and national grid to 2050 — the ISP provides the
  definitive long-term grid capacity and transmission investment schedule against which SA
  project connection timelines should be benchmarked.
  Source: AEMO | https://www.aemo.com.au/-/media/files/major-publications/isp/2026/2026-integrated-system-plan-isp.pdf


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• The IREN Bundey campus defines the template that hyperscale buyers are willing to pay
  for in SA: a site ~75km north-east of Adelaide with direct 330kV transmission access,
  proximity to multiple operating wind farms, and submarine fibre links to Singapore,
  Indonesia, South Korea, and Japan — the combination of renewable colocation,
  high-voltage transmission, and intercontinental fibre connectivity is now the de facto
  reference specification for hyperscale data centre site selection in Australia, and South
  Energy should use this template to evaluate and market its own SA pipeline sites. [HIGH RELEVANCE]

  centre infrastructure anchored by firmed renewable storage — establishes Aurora as a
  direct competitor/comparable for South Energy's project pipeline; understanding the
  precinct's location, grid connection terms, and renewable supply structure is recommended
  to sharpen South Energy's competitive positioning and deal terms with potential buyers.

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• 美國AI基礎設施企業IREN已與南澳州輸電公司ElectraNet簽署輸電接入協議，
  擬在阿德萊德東北約75公里的邦迪（Bundey）建設一座800兆瓦數據中心園區——
  確保在無需任何電網升級的前提下使用邦迪變電站四條330千伏饋線出口，
  目標於2028年起通電；估計投資額約100億澳元，該項目是澳大利亞及亞太地區
  迄今宣佈的最大規模數據中心。此案充分證明，南澳州特定次輸電節點已具備
  承接超大規模負荷的接入容量，且無需代價高昂的電網增強工程——
  這正是東部各州項目在經濟上難以立足的主因。South Energy應立即盤點
  其他ElectraNet具有類似剩餘容量且毗鄰可再生能源資源的變電站，
  在類似接入協議相繼簽署之前，搶先鎖定下一批收購目標。【高度相關】

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 澳大利亞聯邦工業、科學與資源部已在《國家AI計劃》框架下，正式發佈
  針對數據中心開發商的一套「期望標準」——五項核心期望為：

  符合標準的項目將在監管審批中獲得優先安排，不符合者不予優待——
