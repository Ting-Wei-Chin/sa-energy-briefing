🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• POST-NATIONAL CABINET — King & Wood Mallesons: "Big Day for Data Centres" — New
  Renewable Energy, Grid Connection & Cost Recovery Rules Now in Final Form [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  Following the August 27-28 National Cabinet meeting, King & Wood Mallesons published a
  comprehensive legal briefing explaining the three-part regulatory framework now finalised
  for Australian data centres: (1) mandatory renewable energy and power requirements to be
  implemented via AEMC rule changes; (2) updated technical grid connection standards for
  large loads connecting to the NEM; and (3) cost recovery rules establishing which parties
  pay for transmission network augmentations triggered by new large loads. Critically, for
  South Energy, the cost recovery framework means that a project connecting adjacent to
  Bundey — where network augmentation has already been triggered and largely committed via
  the IREN campus — may avoid or significantly reduce the cost recovery burden that would
  apply to a greenfield connection requiring a new substation or new transmission line; this
  is a concrete commercial advantage of Bundey-corridor sites over any competing SA location
  that requires fresh transmission investment. South Energy should ensure all buyer materials
  address each of these three regulatory pillars explicitly.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: King & Wood Mallesons (August 2026) |
  https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/big-day-for-data-centres.html

• ElectraNet 2026 TAPR: $3.5 Billion Northern Transmission Project Identified — Bundey-to-
  Adelaide and Bundey-to-Cultana Corridors Would Unlock Second-Wave Capacity for Data Centres
  and Defence Industry [HIGH RELEVANCE]: ElectraNet's 2026 Transmission Annual Planning
  Report (TAPR) identifies three major new transmission projects driven explicitly by data
  centre demand and the AI boom in the Greater Adelaide and Mid North regions; the headline
  project is the $3.5 billion Northern Transmission Project — currently in early planning —
  which would expand transfer capacity between Bundey substation (where IREN has anchored
  800MW) and both the Adelaide metropolitan load centre and the anticipated Cultana load
  centre to the northwest; the TAPR also proposes the Eyre Peninsula Upgrade (expanding
  the Cultana–Yadnarie 132kV lines) and a South East Expansion; for South Energy, the TAPR
  is the clearest official signal yet that the Bundey corridor is not a one-cycle opportunity —
  ElectraNet is planning billions of dollars of transmission investment centred on Bundey in
  direct response to data centre demand, meaning land adjacent to Bundey will benefit from
  long-term network investment regardless of which specific hyperscalers connect; any South
  Energy site positioned along the Bundey-to-Adelaide or Bundey-to-Cultana transmission
  corridor is therefore aligned with the long-term direction of SA network investment.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• SA Technical Regulator Guidelines (February 2026): Data Centres ≥100MW Must Obtain
  SA-Specific Certification Covering Power System Reliability, Security and Stability
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]: South Australia's Technical Regulator published guidelines in February
  2026 requiring data centres of 100MW or more to obtain SA-specific certification covering:
  (i) power system reliability; (ii) security and stability; (iii) mandatory backup energy
  systems; and (iv) compliance with directions during system stress events; this SA-specific
  regulatory layer sits alongside the federal AEMC grid ride-through standards and the
  post-National Cabinet cost recovery rules, creating a three-tiered compliance structure
  that any large SA data centre project must navigate; for South Energy, this means every
  project site targeting a hyperscaler tenant must build SA Technical Regulator certification
  requirements into the project engineering specifications and development approval
  documentation from the earliest stage — buyers conducting technical and regulatory due
  diligence will require evidence of this compliance pathway, and South Energy should ensure
  its information memorandums address the SA Technical Regulator framework explicitly; sites
  already in the Bundey corridor benefit from IREN's presence as a proof-of-compliance
  precedent.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]

  電網故障穿越標準及全國內閣後的成本回收規則共同構成三層合規架構，任何大型南澳州
  數據中心項目均須逐層通過；對South Energy而言，這意味著每個以超大規模客戶為目標
  的項目選址，都必須從最早期就將南澳州技術監管機構的認證要求嵌入項目工程規格和開發
  審批文件——進行技術及監管盡職調查的買家將要求提供這一合規路徑的書面證據；班迪走廊
  的現有選址受益於IREN的合規先例效應，可作為參照依據。【高度相關】
