☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• Australia's NEM Breaks 80% Renewables Barrier for First Time — Saturday 19 September 2026
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  Renewable energy met more than 80 per cent of total NEM grid demand for the first time in
  history on Saturday 19 September 2026, reaching 80.5% at 12:25 pm — surpassing the
  previous record of 79.5% set just the day before (Friday 18 September). The milestone was
  achieved despite heavy curtailment: by 1:50 pm, approximately 54.8 GWh of wind and solar
  had been throttled, with flexible loads absorbing around 22.5 GWh (including ~17.3 GWh
  into batteries and ~5.2 GWh into pumped hydro). South Australia, consistently the NEM's
  most renewable state, was a significant contributor. The back-to-back record-breaking days
  confirm the structural velocity of Australia's clean-energy transition and validate the
  net-generator compliance case for SA-sited hyperscaler campuses.

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 regulatory package (King & Wood Mallesons, 14 September
  analysis): (1) mandatory REGO-backed renewable energy and firming offset obligation for
  data centres; (2) AEMC full capacity charging cost recovery rule; (3) AEMC 30 MW+
  technical grid standards. Only sites with pre-secured firm transmission in renewable-rich
  locations — such as Bundey 330 kV — satisfy all three obligations at connection without
  additional capital investment.

  [MONITORING] Australia to legislate mandatory net-generator requirement for large data
  centres — operators must inject at least as much power as they consume and underwrite new
  firmed renewable supply. Federal draft legislation targeting early 2027 Parliament
  introduction. SA Bundey + Goyder Battery is the only currently pipeline-ready site
  satisfying this obligation structurally without retrofitting.

  [MONITORING] Non-retrospective intent for federal mandatory standards confirmed at National
  Cabinet (26 August 2026). Projects obtaining SA Coordinator-General approval in 2026 may
  lock in pre-legislation status — directly increasing pipeline value for buyers seeking to
  avoid post-2027 mandatory standards.

  [MONITORING] SA Government's proposed Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act (announced June
  2026): data centres to be designated essential infrastructure; Coordinator-General as single
  decision-making authority; "new energy for new demand" requirement; currently in
  consultation and drafting stage.

  [MONITORING] AEMC actively reviewing National Electricity Rule changes for 30 MW+ data
  centres (fault ride-through/LVRT requirement); federal legislation targeted for early 2027.
  Sites with pre-secured grid connections and compliant grid interface specifications are
  structurally aligned with the incoming rule while prospective applicants face redesign costs.

  【持續監測】澳大利亞擬立法強制要求大型數據中心履行「淨發電商」義務——運營商須向
  電網輸入不少於其消耗量的電力，並須自行承擔新增有保障可再生能源供應。聯邦立法草案
  目標2027年初提交議會。邦迪加高德爾電池是目前管道中唯一能在接入時無需額外改造即可
  在結構上滿足此義務的澳大利亞場址。

  【持續監測】全國內閣（2026年8月26日）確認聯邦強制性標準的不追溯效力。2026年取得
  南澳州總督察員規劃批准的項目，可能鎖定立法前身份，直接提升對尋求規避2027年後
  強制標準之買家的管道價值。

  【持續監測】南澳州政府擬議《數據中心與AI基礎設施法》（2026年6月宣布）：數據中心
  將被指定為基本基礎設施；總督察員為重大項目單一決策機構；「新需求配新能源」要求；
  目前處於諮詢及起草階段。

  【持續監測】AEMC正積極審查針對30兆瓦以上數據中心的國家電力規則修改（故障穿越/LVRT
  技術標準為核心），聯邦立法目標2027年初提交議會。持有預確認電網連接且符合電網接口
  技術規範的場址已在結構上符合即將頒布的規則，而投機性申請場址面臨重新設計及額外
  接入成本敞口。
