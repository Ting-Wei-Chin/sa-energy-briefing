🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• [NEW] AEMO Launches World-First Trial: Grid Operation with Zero Synchronous Generation [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  AEMO has commenced a world-first trial using grid-scale battery inverters to sustain stable
  operation of Australia's interconnected grid with no synchronous generation online. If validated
  at commercial scale, this directly removes the system strength barrier that currently requires
  synchronous gas plant to remain online — a structural constraint limiting how fast large new
  loads can connect without triggering system strength obligations or incurring synchronous
  condenser costs. South Australia, with the NEM's highest renewable penetration and the Bundey
  330 kV anchor node, is the primary proving ground for this operating mode. A successful outcome
  materially reduces the per-MW compliance cost for new data centre connections in the NTx
  corridor.
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/aemo-turns-to-battery-inverters-for-world-first-trial-of-running-big-grids-with-no-synchronous-generation/

• [UPDATED COUNTDOWN — 14 DAYS] AEMC Final Determination on Data Centre Grid Standards:
  29 October 2026 [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  One day closer: the AEMC binding technical standard for 30 MW+ data centres (disturbance
  ride-through, system strength self-remediation or charge, alignment with Texas/Ireland/Finland
  benchmarks) is now 14 days away. Pre-secured firm transmission agreements at Bundey and the NTx
  corridor are specifically structured to satisfy these standards from day one of the
  determination's commencement. Projects still in the connection queue face the highest compliance
  exposure post-determination.
  Sources: AEMC | https://www.aemc.gov.au/rule-changes/improving-nem-access-standards-package-2
  WattClarity | https://wattclarity.com.au/articles/2026/04/05april-aemc-draft-datacentre/

• [NEW] AMPYR Australia Receives AEMO Section 5.3.4 Connection Letter for 270 MW Grid-Forming
  Battery at Port Augusta [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  AMPYR Australia has received its AEMO section 5.3.4 connection enquiry response letter for its
  270 MW grid-forming battery energy storage system at Port Augusta — the first formal AEMO
