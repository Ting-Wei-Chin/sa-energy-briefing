📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• Victoria's Sustainable Data Centre Action Plan: All New Data Centres Must Supply Own
  Renewable Energy and Storage — SA Competitive Position Materially Strengthened [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  Victoria released its Sustainable Data Centre Action Plan on 22 September 2026, imposing four
  binding requirements on all new data centre developments: (1) match new electricity demand
  with new generation from 100% renewable sources; (2) meet own grid connection and network
  upgrade costs; (3) use recycled or non-potable water for cooling; and (4) locate outside
  residential zones and areas near schools. The rules apply to new developments and new DA
  applications only — no retrospective effect. Victoria had been Australia's second-largest data
  centre market after NSW, with 44 facilities and 11.4 GW in the NSW pipeline as context for
  scale.
  Sources: ABC News | Data Center Dynamics | pv magazine Australia | ESS News
  https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/victoria-australia-proposes-rules-requiring-data-centers-to-secure-own-renewable-energy-supply/
  https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/09/23/victoria-mandates-renewables-for-new-data-centres/
  [HIGH RELEVANCE — Victoria's mandatory 100% own-generation + own-connection-cost requirement
  mirrors SA's proposed "new energy for new demand" principle but adds incremental cost burdens
  (network upgrade liability + water infrastructure) that SA's current framework does not impose.
  This permanently widens the cost gap between SA Coordinator-General-approved sites and
  comparable Victorian projects, and eliminates Melbourne as a cost-competitive alternative to
  SA for hyperscalers evaluating greenfield AI inference campuses. South Energy's land parcels
  adjacent to committed transmission nodes (Bundey, Tailem Bend, Stirling North) are now the
  lowest all-in-cost greenfield option in the two most attractive eastern-seaboard states.]

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 維多利亞州《可持續數據中心行動計劃》：所有新建數據中心須自備可再生能源發電及儲能
  ——南澳州競爭優勢大幅強化【高度相關】：
  維多利亞州於2026年9月22日發布《可持續數據中心行動計劃》，對所有新建數據中心項目
  設定四項強制要求：（1）新增用電負荷須100%由自備可再生能源發電匹配；（2）自行承擔
  電網接入費用及網絡升級成本；（3）冷卻系統須使用再生水或非飲用水；（4）選址禁止
  設於住宅區或學校附近。上述規則僅適用於新開發項目及新提交的規劃申請，不具追溯效力。
