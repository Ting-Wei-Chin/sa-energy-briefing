  [MONITORING] AEMC Final Rule "Package 2" — 30 MW Large-Load Threshold and Combined Impact
  Assessment Now in Force (12 November 2026) [HIGH RELEVANCE]: The AEMC published its final rule
  "Improving the NEM Access Standards – Package 2" on 12 November 2026, establishing a formal
  30 MW threshold that classifies data centres and other large inverter-based loads (IBLs) as a
  distinct connection category subject to mandatory technical standards. Key obligations: (1) network
  businesses must assess the COMBINED impact of multiple nearby large-load connections; (2) AEMO
  must develop and maintain modelling guidelines for IBLs; (3) existing applicants already advanced
  receive a 6-month grace period. South Energy's pre-secured Bundey 330 kV firm transmission
  positions satisfy both the technical (Package 2) and commercial (ECMC) requirements simultaneously.
  Sources: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/aemc-sets-new-rules-for-data-centre-grid-connections/
  AEMC | https://www.aemc.gov.au/news-centre/media-releases/aemc-determination-strengthens-framework-address-anticipated-data-centre-impacts-national
