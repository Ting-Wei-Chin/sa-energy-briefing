• Australia's NEM Sets New All-Time Renewable Energy Record: 79.5% on Friday 18 September 2026
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  The National Electricity Market hit a new all-time renewable energy record of 79.5% at 1:30pm
  on Friday September 18, 2026 — surpassing the previous record of 78.6% set on October 11,

🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• Anthropic Signs A$32 Billion Queensland Data Centre Lease — 2.16 GW Campus Near Dalby
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  Anthropic signed its first Australian data centre lease agreement on September 15–16, 2026,
  for a 2.16 GW AI inference campus at Western Downs, approximately 250 km west of Brisbane,
  Queensland, developed by Singapore-based Zerra DC (A$30–32B construction cost). The facility
  — which would rank among the largest data centres globally — will power Anthropic's Claude AI
  inference operations using a closed-loop air-cooled system; it is subject to Foreign
  Investment Review Board (FIRB) approval and local council planning sign-off, with operations
  targeted from 2027 and a 4–6 year full build-out. Critically, the Western Downs site has
  attracted significant criticism for its reliance on Queensland's coal-heavy grid — a
  comparison that directly accentuates SA's fully-renewable grid advantage and net-generator
  compliance position, and which has already generated national media coverage contrasting
  Queensland's grid exposure with SA's clean-energy leadership.
  Source: ABC News / The Next Web | https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-16/queensland-data-centre-anthropic-dalby/107160640
  [HIGH RELEVANCE — Australia's largest-ever data centre lease establishes 2 GW+ as the new
  benchmark for mega-campus AI inference demand in Australia; Queensland's coal-grid exposure
  creates an immediate, publicly-visible compliance contrast favouring SA; creates urgency
  for SA site acquisition as hyperscaler-grade supply compresses nationally]

• Amazon Receives AUD 2.17B Planning Approval for 235 MW Western Sydney Data Centre
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]:

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 regulatory package (King & Wood Mallesons, 14 September
  analysis): (1) mandatory REGO-backed renewable energy and firming offset obligation for
  data centres; (2) AEMC full capacity charging cost recovery rule; (3) AEMC 30 MW+
  technical grid standards. Only sites with pre-secured firm transmission in renewable-rich
  locations — such as Bundey 330 kV — satisfy all three obligations at connection without
  additional capital investment.

  [MONITORING] Australia to legislate mandatory net-generator requirement for large data
  centres — operators must inject at least as much power as they consume and underwrite new
  firmed renewable supply. Federal draft legislation targeting early 2027 Parliament
  introduction. SA Bundey + Goyder Battery is the only currently pipeline-ready site
  satisfying this obligation structurally without retrofitting.

  [MONITORING] Non-retrospective intent for federal mandatory standards confirmed at National
  Cabinet (26 August 2026). Projects obtaining SA Coordinator-General approval in 2026 may
  lock in pre-legislation status — directly increasing pipeline value for buyers seeking to
  avoid post-2027 mandatory standards.

  [MONITORING] SA Government's proposed Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act (announced June
  2026): data centres to be designated essential infrastructure; Coordinator-General as single
  decision-making authority; "new energy for new demand" requirement; currently in
  consultation and drafting stage.

  [MONITORING] AEMC actively reviewing National Electricity Rule changes for 30 MW+ data
  centres (fault ride-through/LVRT requirement); federal legislation targeted for early 2027.
  Sites with pre-secured grid connections and compliant grid interface specifications are
  structurally aligned with the incoming rule while prospective applicants face redesign costs.

  REGO可再生能源及調峰抵消義務；（2）AEMC「全容量收費」費用回收規則；（3）AEMC 30兆瓦

  目標2027年初提交議會。邦迪加高德爾電池是目前管道中唯一能在接入時無需額外改造即可

  強制標準之買家的管道價值。
