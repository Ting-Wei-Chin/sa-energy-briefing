📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 10 September 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 meeting on NER changes mandating data
  centre renewable and firming offsets — outcome not yet published as of
  10 September. Queensland and the Northern Territory dissented at the
  28 July ECMC meeting where the AEMC delivered its advice; the remaining
  six jurisdictions backed the nationally consistent framework. Federal
  legislation is targeted for introduction to Parliament in early 2027.
  Watch for the official communiqué from energy.gov.au.

  [MONITORING] AEMO's call for non-network options for three newly
  actionable 2026 ISP projects remains open — submissions close Thursday
  17 September 2026. Sites with secured transmission connections and
  integrated renewable firming should assess eligibility.

  [MONITORING] ElectraNet's 2026 TAPR forecasts increased connection
  demand from Greater Adelaide, the Mid North, and South East SA driven
  by data centres, AI growth, and defence industry expansion; three new
  transmission projects are proposed, including the $3.5B Northern
  Transmission Project spanning northern and eastern SA.


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] Goyder Battery Stage 1 (200 MW / 800 MWh) construction
  commenced at ElectraNet's Bundey substation in August 2026, co-located
  with IREN's 800 MW data centre campus transmission connection (four
  330 kV feeder exits). Co-location of large-scale BESS firming with
  the highest-capacity grid connection node in regional SA continues to
  be the leading site-quality signal for hyperscale buyer criteria.


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• NVIDIA Announces Up to 2 GW AI Factory Buildout in Australia by 2027
  — IREN's Bundey Campus (SA) and Firmus Project Southgate Sites (Tailem
  Bend and Port Augusta, SA) Explicitly Named [HIGH RELEVANCE]: NVIDIA
  announced on 9 September 2026 a collaboration with Australian NVIDIA
  Cloud Partners and AI infrastructure operators — including IREN, Firmus,
  NEXTDC, AirTrunk, CDC, Sharon AI, ResetData, and Megaport — to expand
  land, power, and shell capacity designed to host multiple generations of
  NVIDIA DSX AI factory infrastructure, targeting a buildout of up to
  2 gigawatts by 2027; IREN is applying the NVIDIA DSX blueprint across
  its development portfolio, including its 800 MW Bundey campus in South
  Australia; Firmus is expanding its Project Southgate initiative —
  developing NVIDIA-powered AI factories designed to support hyperscale
  and AI-native demand across Australia and Asia-Pacific — with sites
  near Tailem Bend and Port Augusta confirmed as part of that initiative;
  for South Energy, this announcement materially expands the active SA
  buyer and partner universe beyond direct hyperscalers: IREN's existing
  Bundey campus is already the reference anchor for SA grid-connection
  benchmarking, and Firmus's confirmed interest in Upper Spencer Gulf
  and Tailem Bend sites signals demand for additional SA locations
  meeting equivalent grid-connection, renewable-firming, and planning
  criteria — precisely the project profile South Energy is developing;
  the NVIDIA DSX designation also signals that the infrastructure layer
  hyperscalers are purchasing has standardised around power-dense,
  renewable-firmed AI factory specifications, further validating
  South Energy's project model.

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] South Australia released its dedicated data centre strategy
  and announced the Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act on 8 September
  2026; crown sponsorship pathway and Coordinator General designation
  powers are now active, compressing planning timelines and eliminating
  council-level approval risk; the "new energy for new demand" statutory
  obligation validates South Energy's renewable-firmed project model.
  (Covered in detail in the 9 September briefing.)

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 NER decision (data centre renewable
  and firming mandatory offset requirement) — formal communiqué pending.
  Federal draft legislation targeting early 2027. QLD and NT dissented at
  the 28 July ECMC meeting; six remaining jurisdictions backed the
  nationally consistent framework. Minister Bowen confirmed "no
  carve-outs" on 28 August 2026. National Cabinet (26 August 2026)
  endorsed developing nationally consistent mandatory standards covering
  energy, water, land-use, and skills/training.


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] Firmus Technologies' Project Southgate (confirmed active
  as of the NVIDIA 9 September announcement) names Tailem Bend and Port
  Augusta as SA AI factory sites, indicating active site-selection
  activity in both the Murray Mallee corridor and Upper Spencer Gulf.
  For South Energy, this introduces a second confirmed tier of SA
  site-acquisition demand, supplementing the IREN Bundey benchmark with
  demand signals from a different geographic corridor and a different
  buyer profile (platform developer vs. direct campus owner).

  [MONITORING] Key background context: Deloitte's project tracker
  (August 2026) counted 33 Australian data centre projects worth a
  combined A$104 billion. Of the approximately 9 GW of proposed data
  centre capacity seeking transmission connections on Australia's main
  grid, approximately 84% remained at the application stage as of
  June 2026, confirming a structural bottleneck that favours sites with
  completed transmission connection agreements. With SA now introducing
  a dedicated Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act (Coordinator General
  fast-track active), SA projects are further differentiated from NSW and
  VIC where no equivalent state-level mechanism exists.

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年9月10日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】能源與氣候變化部長理事會（ECMC）2026年9月會議
  關於強制要求數據中心完成可再生能源及調峰補償的《國家電力規則》
  修訂案——截至9月10日尚未發布正式公報。昆士蘭州和北領地在
  7月28日ECMC會議上持異議立場，其餘六個司法管轄區支持全國
  統一框架。聯邦立法目標於2027年初提交議會。
  請關注energy.gov.au發布的官方公報。

  【持續監測】AEMO就2026年綜合系統規劃三項新可行動項目發出
  非電網解決方案徵集——截止日期仍為2026年9月17日（星期四）。
  已獲輸電連接協議並集成可再生能源調峰的場址應評估申請資格。

  【持續監測】ElectraNet 2026年輸電充足性規劃報告（TAPR）預測，
  受數據中心、人工智能增長及國防產業擴張驅動，大阿德萊德、
  北部中部及南澳州東南部的電網接入需求將顯著上升；報告提議
  三項新輸電項目，包括橫跨南澳北部及東部的35億澳元北部輸電項目。


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】Neoen高德爾電池儲能項目一期（200兆瓦/800兆瓦時）
  已於2026年8月在ElectraNet邦迪變電站啟動建設，與IREN 800兆瓦
  數據中心園區的輸電連接協議（四個330千伏饋線出口）同址共建。
  大規模電池儲能調峰基礎設施與南澳州區域最高容量電網接入節點
  的同址集中，持續作為超大規模雲端買家選址標準的領先質量信號。


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• 英偉達宣布澳大利亞最高2吉瓦人工智能工廠建設計劃至2027年——
  南澳州IREN邦迪園區及Firmus「南門計劃」場址（塔萊姆本德和
  阿德萊德北部阿古斯塔港）明確被點名【高度相關】：英偉達
  （NVIDIA）於2026年9月9日宣布與澳大利亞英偉達雲端合作夥伴
  及人工智能基礎設施運營商展開合作——合作方包括IREN、Firmus、
  NEXTDC、AirTrunk、CDC、Sharon AI、ResetData及Megaport——
  共同擴大土地、電力及機房容量，以承載多代NVIDIA DSX人工智能
  工廠基礎設施，目標於2027年前建設最高達2吉瓦的容量；IREN
  正將NVIDIA DSX藍圖應用於其整個開發組合，包括位於南澳州的
  800兆瓦邦迪園區；Firmus正在擴展其「南門計劃」（Project
  Southgate）——開發由英偉達技術驅動的人工智能工廠，服務澳大利亞
  及亞太地區的超大規模客戶和人工智能原生需求——塔萊姆本德及
  阿古斯塔港附近場址已確認納入該計劃；對South Energy而言，
  此次公告實質性擴展了南澳州的活躍買家及合作夥伴群體，超越了
  直接超大規模雲端客戶的範疇：IREN現有的邦迪園區已是南澳州
  電網連接基準的參考錨點，Firmus在上斯賓塞灣及塔萊姆本德場址
  的確認興趣，則表明對符合同等電網連接、可再生能源調峰及規劃
  標準的南澳州額外場址的需求——這正是South Energy正在開發的
  項目特徵；NVIDIA DSX的指定規格還表明，超大規模客戶所採購的
  基礎設施層已圍繞高功率密度、可再生能源調峰的人工智能工廠
  規格形成標準化，進一步驗證了South Energy的項目模型。

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】南澳州於2026年9月8日發布專項數據中心戰略並宣布
  《數據中心與人工智能基礎設施法》計劃；皇家擔保規劃通道及總
  督察員指定授權已正式啟動，大幅壓縮規劃審批時間表、消除地方
  議會審批風險；「新需求配新能源」法定義務驗證了South Energy
  項目模型中的可再生能源及電池儲能調峰架構。
  （詳見9月9日簡報。）

  【持續監測】ECMC 2026年9月《國家電力規則》決策（數據中心
  可再生能源及調峰強制補償要求）——正式公報尚未發布。聯邦法律
  草案目標於2027年初提交議會。昆士蘭州和北領地在7月28日ECMC
  會議上持異議立場，其餘六個司法管轄區支持全國統一框架。
  博文部長於2026年8月28日確認「無豁免條款」。全國內閣
  （2026年8月26日）已背書制定覆蓋能源、用水、土地使用及
  技能/培訓的全國一致性強制標準。


🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】Firmus科技「南門計劃」（根據英偉達9月9日公告
  確認仍在積極推進）點名塔萊姆本德及阿古斯塔港作為南澳州
  人工智能工廠場址，表明在莫雷馬利走廊和上斯賓塞灣地區均存在
  積極的場址選擇活動。對South Energy而言，這引入了南澳州場址
  收購需求的第二個確認梯隊，以不同的地理走廊和不同的買家畫像
  （平台開發商vs.直接園區業主）補充了IREN邦迪基準，拓寬了
  South Energy土地獲取項目的潛在退出通道。

  【持續監測】關鍵背景：德勤項目追蹤數據（2026年8月）顯示，
  澳大利亞已有33個數據中心項目、總投資額達1,040億澳元。在申請
  接入澳大利亞主幹電網的約9千兆瓦擬建數據中心容量中，截至
  2026年6月約84%仍處於申請階段，印證了「已完成輸電連接協議
  場址」的結構性瓶頸溢價邏輯。隨著南澳州即將推出《數據中心
  與人工智能基礎設施法》（總督察員快速審批通道已啟動），南澳州
  項目與目前尚無同等州級機制的新南威爾士州和維多利亞州之間的
  差異化優勢進一步拉大。
