🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• AEMO 2026 Electricity Statement of Opportunities Confirms 40GW Pipeline and 9.1GW
  Commissioned in FY2026 — Simultaneously Flags Data Centre Load Connections as One
  of Four Priority National Grid Risks, Raising the Scarcity Value of SA Sites Already
  Advanced in the ElectraNet Connection Queue [HIGH RELEVANCE]: The Australian Energy
  Market Operator published its 2026 Electricity Statement of Opportunities (ESOO) on
  25 August 2026, confirming a pipeline of approximately 40GW of committed and anticipated
  generation and storage projects across the National Electricity Market and finding no
  forecast reliability gap until 2030-31, with 9.1GW of new generation and storage
  commissioned in FY2026 alone — described as the clearest reliability outlook the annual
  report has carried in several years, conditional on projects arriving on time; critically
  for large-load developers, AEMO simultaneously released the 2026 General Power System
  Risk Review, which identified increasing large-load connections from data centres as one
  of four priority grid risks nationally, alongside non-credible system-strength risks from
  synchronous-machine retirements, large non-credible generation and network contingency
  events, and voltage-control risks; operating data centres across the NEM had approximately
  2.2GW of maximum connection capacity in Q1 2026 with actual demand representing only
  approximately 27% of that capacity — meaning the 5.4GW pipeline (confirmed in the 2026
  ISP) is largely uncommissioned and its load profile remains uncertain; for South Energy,
  AEMO's formal classification of data centre load connections as a priority grid risk
  formalises what was previously a planning challenge — it means AEMO is now actively
  modelling and managing large-load connection risk at the system level, which in practice
  signals that connection conditions, approval processes and cost recovery for new projects
  are likely to tighten progressively over 2026-2027; SA sites that already hold firm
  transmission connection agreements with ElectraNet, or are advanced in the ElectraNet
  connection queue, therefore carry measurable option value over greenfield sites that will
  face tighter conditions as AEMO implements system-level risk mitigations.

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• National Cabinet Unanimously Endorses Mandatory National Standards for Large Data
  Centres Requiring 100% Renewable Energy — 26 August 2026, Commonwealth Legislation
  Committed for Early 2027, "No Exceptions and No Carve-Outs," Elevating the Compliance
  Value of SA's Pre-Assembled Renewables-Firming Stack from Competitive Advantage to
  Regulatory Necessity [HIGH RELEVANCE]: Australia's National Cabinet met on 26 August 2026
  and unanimously endorsed a plan to develop mandatory national standards for large data
  centres, committing all nine federal, state and territory governments to a single compliance
  rulebook covering energy (100% renewable generation requirement), water efficiency, land
  use and skills and training, with Commonwealth legislation committed for early 2027; Energy
  and Climate Change Minister Chris Bowen stated explicitly that there would be "no exceptions
  and no carve-outs" under the 100% renewable requirement, and the net-generator requirement
  — requiring data centres to put at least as much power into the grid as they draw from it
  — was confirmed as part of the legislated framework; the National Cabinet decision elevates
  and politically solidifies the PM's July 15 mandatory net-generator announcement and the
  AEMC's August 5 formal four-part framework advice (REGO additionality, firming mandate,
  market registration, flexible connection) that entered ECMC consideration in September 2026
  — all nine governments having endorsed the direction means the legislative pathway is
  cleared and the compliance timetable is now a political commitment rather than a regulatory
  recommendation; critically, the National Cabinet unanimous endorsement resolves the residual
  sovereign risk question that sophisticated buyers will have been running against Australian
  data centre investment cases: the mandatory renewables-firming compliance framework is no
  longer an AEMC advice or a PM speech — it is a nine-government political commitment with a
  firm legislative timetable of early 2027, eliminating the scenario where a change in
  federal government or state-level resistance could delay or weaken implementation; for
  South Energy, this is the most important policy development since the AEMC framework
  entered the ECMC legislative pipeline: SA sites that already carry contracted new renewable
  supply, BESS firming arrangements and approved grid connections are the only projects in
  Australia demonstrably compliant in advance of legislation, converting their scarcity value
  from a commercial differentiator into an absolute compliance differentiator.

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年9月6日

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• AEMO 2026年《電力機遇聲明》確認40吉瓦管道及2026財年新增9.1吉瓦並網裝機
  ——同步將數據中心大負荷接入列為全國四大電網優先風險之一，進一步強化
  南澳州已進入ElectraNet接入排隊的場址的稀缺性溢價【高度相關】：澳大利亞
  能源市場運營商（AEMO）於2026年8月25日發布2026年《電力機遇聲明》（ESOO），
  確認全國電力市場（NEM）已承諾及預期中的發電和儲能項目管道合計約40吉瓦，
  並預計至2030-31財年之前不存在可靠性缺口；2026財年已有9.1吉瓦新發電和儲能
  裝機投入運行——被描述為該年度報告多年來所呈現的最樂觀可靠性展望，但前提
  是相關項目必須按時落地；對大型負荷開發商而言更為關鍵的是：AEMO同步發布的
  2026年《電力系統總體風險審查》，將數據中心大負荷接入連接增加列為全國四大
  電網優先風險之一，其他三項分別為同步發電機退役導致的系統強度非可信事件
  風險、大型非可信發電及電網突發事件風險，以及電壓控制風險；2026年第一季度
  NEM在運數據中心的最大連接容量約為2.2吉瓦，但實際需求僅佔該容量的約27%，
  意味著2026年綜合系統規劃（ISP）確認的5.4吉瓦管道大部分仍處於未投運狀態；
  對South Energy而言，AEMO正式將數據中心負荷連接列為電網優先風險，標誌著此前
  僅屬規劃層面的挑戰已上升為系統層面的主動管控議題，這在實踐中意味著電網連接
  條件、審批流程及新項目成本回收機制很可能在2026至2027年間持續收緊；已與
  ElectraNet簽訂固定輸電連接協議或已進入ElectraNet接入排隊的南澳州場址，因此
  相對於尚未取得連接的未開發場址，具備可量化的期權價值。【高度相關】

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 全國內閣一致通過對大型數據中心強制推行「100%可再生能源」國家標準——
  2026年8月26日，聯邦立法承諾於2027年初出台，「無例外、無豁免」，南澳州
  預建可再生能源調峰技術棧的合規價值從競爭優勢躍升為監管必要條件【高度相關】：
  澳大利亞全國內閣於2026年8月26日一致通過為大型數據中心制定強制性國家標準的
  計劃，承諾由聯邦、各州及各地區政府共同執行一份統一合規規則手冊，涵蓋能源
  （100%可再生電力要求）、水效率、土地使用及技能培訓等方面，聯邦立法已承諾
  於2027年初落地；能源與氣候變化部長克里斯·鮑文明確表示，100%可再生能源要求
  「無例外、無豁免」；淨發電要求——要求數據中心向電網輸入的電力不低於其從
  電網抽取的電力量——已確認納入立法框架；全國內閣決定在政治層面強化並確定了
  總理7月15日的強制淨發電公告，以及AEMC於8月5日提交ECMC審議的四部分正式
  框架建議（REGO附加性要求、強制調峰要求、市場主體登記及靈活接入）——九個
  政府的一致背書意味著立法路徑已疏通，合規時間表已從監管建議升格為政治承諾；
  最關鍵的是，全國內閣一致表態消除了精明買家一直針對澳大利亞數據中心投資案例
  的剩餘主權風險疑慮：強制可再生能源調峰合規框架已不再僅是AEMC建議或總理演講
  ——它是九個政府的政治承諾，具備2027年初的明確立法時間表，消除了因聯邦政府
  更迭或州級阻力而導致實施延遲或弱化的情景；對South Energy而言，這是繼AEMC
  框架進入ECMC立法管道以來最重要的政策進展：已具備已合約化新可再生能源供應、
  BESS調峰安排及批准電網連接的南澳州場址，是澳大利亞唯一可在立法前即達標的
  項目，其稀缺性已從商業差異化因素升格為絕對的合規差異化因素。【高度相關】
