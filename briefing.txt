📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 16 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• Project EnergyConnect — Australia's biggest transmission project (900km, budget blown from $2.1B to
  $3.8B after delays on the NSW/Transgrid section) — has been energised; the SA section was delivered
  on time and on budget by ElectraNet, and Stage 2 release of up to 800MW of bi-directional SA–NSW
  capacity is now expected in Q4 2026, pending test conditions. This milestone materially strengthens
  SA's grid security and its ability to export surplus renewable generation or import balancing power
  — both critical to hyperscale data centre uptime guarantees — and closes the last gap in SA's
  east-coast interconnection spine. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/not-easy-australias-biggest-transmission-project-energised-after-delays-and-cost-overruns/
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/energyconnect-reaches-completion-after-delays-cost-blowouts/

• ElectraNet is planning to grow SA's network capacity to 25GW, with peak demand forecast to more than
  double — from 3.3GW currently to over 6.5GW over the next 15 years — driven by data centres, AI,
  defence (Osborne Naval Shipyard) and industrial loads; connection enquiries are at their highest
  level in decades; this planning horizon confirms that grid-connected SA land will command a
  structural scarcity premium as capacity tightens, and that South Energy should prioritise locking in
  connection terms before the queue lengthens further. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/australian-energy-week-2026/
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/peak-load-is-expected-to-double-as-industries-flock-to-australias-world-first-100-pct-renewables-grid/

• AEMO has updated its demand forecasting methodology to create a standalone category for data centres
  — split into commercial-scale (≤10MW, forecast by historical trends) and industrial-scale (>10MW,
  tracked via direct operator data and network intelligence) — first implemented in the 2026 WEM
  Electricity Statement of Opportunities; this institutionalises data centres as a distinct planning
  class and signals that AEMO will apply deeper regulatory scrutiny to their grid integration
  requirements, making early and well-structured connections more valuable.
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/australian-energy-market-operator-creates-standalone-forecast-category-for-data-centers/
  Source: AEMO | https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/news-updates/aemos-updated-forecasting-methodology-targets-rapidly-growing-electricity-loads


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• The federal government has locked in a Renewable Energy Transformation Agreement with South
  Australia, underwriting a minimum 1GW of new wind and solar generation plus 400MW/1,600MWh of
  storage, to guarantee SA's 100% net renewables target by 2027 — making SA the first non-hydro
  gigawatt-scale grid in the world to achieve this milestone; this federal backstop eliminates
  residual investor risk that SA's renewable transition might stall, and directly elevates the premium
  that energy-sensitive buyers (hyperscalers, AI companies) will pay for SA-located data centre
  projects anchored to a federally underwritten renewable supply. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/south-australia-locks-in-federal-funds-to-become-first-grid-in-world-to-reach-100-per-cent-net-wind-and-solar/

• SA's grid has recently recorded a consecutive 100%-net-renewables operational run exceeding 10 days,
  providing empirical proof to prospective data centre buyers that the state's renewable credentials
  are operational reality and not a future aspiration — a critical due-diligence data point for
  technology companies evaluating long-term PPAs anchored to SA sites. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/south-australias-remarkable-100-per-cent-renewables-run-extends-to-over-10-days/


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• Microsoft has announced a landmark AU$25 billion (US$18bn) commitment to expand its cloud and AI
  infrastructure across Australia by end 2029 — the largest single hyperscaler commitment to Australia
  ever made, building on a 2023 AU$5B pledge and expanding Azure capacity by over 140% — and has
  simultaneously signed an MOU with the Australian Government committing to meet the national
  "expectations for data centres," including covering transmission and infrastructure costs and
  offering Australian organisations favourable terms; SA's 100%-renewable grid and the pre-existing
  hyperscale connection template (IREN Bundey at 330kV, 800MW) position South Energy's pipeline sites
  as the natural landing ground for the greenfield hyperscale portion of this record commitment.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/microsoft-pledges-18bn-to-expanding-cloud-and-ai-capacity-in-australia/

• At Australian Energy Week 2026, data centre developers argued publicly that they should be permitted
  to run diesel backup generator fleets while receiving renewable energy credits — a position in direct
  tension with SA's clean-energy governance framework and the federal "expectations"; South Energy's
  sites, underpinned by firmed renewable generation and battery storage, offer buyers a structurally
  cleaner solution that avoids this emerging regulatory and reputational exposure, and provide a
  marketable point of differentiation.
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-says-they-will-drive-down-network-part-of-bills-and-want-us-to-embrace-their-diesel-generators/


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• Queensland and the Northern Territory have formally opposed the federal government's push to
  legislate a mandatory BYO-renewables obligation for data centres, creating a two-speed Australian
  data centre policy landscape — SA, which already operates under voluntary BYO-renewable deal
  structures (IREN Bundey), is "ahead of the law" and can be marketed to buyers as de-risked from the
  legislative transition costs that projects in hold-out states will face when legislation eventually
  passes; this state-level resistance accelerates the relative advantage of SA-compliant projects.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centre-power-struggle-as-hold-out-states-oppose-federal-push-for-byo-renewables/

• The Australian Energy Market Commission (AEMC) has ruled that data centres must bring their own
  clean, firmed energy rather than drawing from the shared grid pool; with AEMO, the federal
  government's five "expectations," and now the AEMC all aligned on mandatory BYO clean energy, SA
  projects that already embed firmed renewable generation are structurally compliant with the
  multi-regulator consensus while projects in other states face rising retrofit risk. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/aemc-says-data-centres-must-bring-their-own-clean-firmed-energy/


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• ESD News analysis confirms that the data centre construction boom will not translate into higher
  power prices for SA consumers if stalled renewable projects continue to be unlocked — directly
  validating the central economic logic of South Energy's combined land-plus-renewable-integration
  model as the politically sustainable and market-acceptable form of data centre development in SA,
  and reinforcing that projects which package land with renewable co-location can be presented to
  government as consumer-positive. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/data-centre-boom-wont-mean-higher-prices-if-we-unlock-stalled-renewables/

• RenewEconomy analysis ("100 pct renewable, 74 pct of the time") documents SA's strategic plan to
  become an energy export powerhouse using its world-leading renewable grid, with ElectraNet
  explicitly targeting 25GW network capacity and SA's transmission network attracting the highest
  connection enquiry volume in decades; the analysis reinforces that direct high-voltage access to
  SA's transmission backbone is the primary scarcity being bid for — ahead of land area or geographic
  location — and that South Energy's site evaluation should weight substation proximity as the
  decisive acquisition criterion.
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/100-pct-renewable-74-pct-of-the-time-how-australias-most-advanced-grid-plans-to-be-an-energy-powerhouse/

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年8月16日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• 澳大利亞規模最大的輸電工程「能源互聯項目」（Project EnergyConnect，全長900公里，
  因NSW段承建方Transgrid延誤及嚴重超支，預算從21億澳元膨脹至38億澳元）已正式通電；
  南澳州段由ElectraNet按時按預算竣工，第二期可釋放最高800兆瓦南澳州至新南威爾士雙向
  輸電容量，預計在測試條件允許的情況下於2026年第四季投入商業運行。此里程碑大幅提升
  南澳州的電網安全憑證，及出口剩餘可再生電力或輸入調峰電力的能力——兩者均是超大規模
  數據中心正常運行時間保障不可或缺的條件——並填補了南澳州通往東部沿岸電網骨幹的
  最後一環。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/not-easy-australias-biggest-transmission-project-energised-after-delays-and-cost-overruns/
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/energyconnect-reaches-completion-after-delays-cost-blowouts/

• ElectraNet正制定計劃，將南澳州電網容量擴展至25吉瓦；最高需求目前為3.3吉瓦，
  未來15年預計將增至逾6.5吉瓦（增幅超過一倍）——主要由數據中心、人工智能、國防
  （Osborne海軍造船廠）及工業負荷帶動；電網接入諮詢量現已達到數十年來最高水平。
  這一規劃週期印證South Energy的長期投資論據：隨著容量限制日趨收緊，具備南澳州電網
  接入條件的土地將享有結構性稀缺溢價，South Energy應優先在接入排隊進一步延長之前
  鎖定接入條款。【高度相關】
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/australian-energy-week-2026/
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/peak-load-is-expected-to-double-as-industries-flock-to-australias-world-first-100-pct-renewables-grid/

• AEMO已更新能源需求預測方法，將數據中心列為獨立預測類別——分為商業規模（不超過10兆瓦，
  依歷史需求趨勢預測）及工業規模（超過10兆瓦，通過直接從運營商獲取數據及電網信息
  等方式追蹤）——首次在《2026年WEM電力市場機遇報告》中實施；此舉在機構層面正式確立
  數據中心為獨立規劃類別，並預示監管機構將對其電網整合要求施加更嚴格的審查，
  令早期且結構完善的接入協議愈加珍貴。
  資料來源：Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/australian-energy-market-operator-creates-standalone-forecast-category-for-data-centers/
  資料來源：AEMO | https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/news-updates/aemos-updated-forecasting-methodology-targets-rapidly-growing-electricity-loads


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• 聯邦政府已與南澳州正式簽署「可再生能源轉型協議」（Renewable Energy Transformation
  Agreement），承諾保底支持最少1吉瓦新增風能和太陽能發電，以及400兆瓦/1,600兆瓦時的
  儲能，確保南澳州到2027年實現100%淨可再生能源目標——令南澳州成為全球首個達成此
  里程碑的非水電吉瓦級電網；這一聯邦政府底牌消除了南澳州可再生能源轉型可能中途
  受阻的殘餘投資者風險，並直接提升了對能源敏感買家（超大規模服務商、AI企業）而言
  以聯邦保底可再生能源供應為錨定的南澳州數據中心項目之溢價空間。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/south-australia-locks-in-federal-funds-to-become-first-grid-in-world-to-reach-100-per-cent-net-wind-and-solar/

• 南澳州電網近期曾持續以100%淨可再生能源運行超過連續10天，以實際運行數據向潛在
  數據中心買家證明，州內的可再生能源認證是已實現的運行事實而非未來願景——這是科技
  企業在評估以南澳州地塊為錨定的長期購電協議時盡職調查的關鍵參考點。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/south-australias-remarkable-100-per-cent-renewables-run-extends-to-over-10-days/


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• 微軟宣佈創紀錄的250億澳元（約180億美元）承諾，將在2029年底前大幅擴展在澳大利亞的
  雲計算和人工智能基礎設施——這是迄今超大規模服務商對澳大利亞所作出的最大單一承諾，
  在2023年50億澳元承諾基礎上進一步加碼，Azure容量將擴大逾140%；微軟同時與澳大利亞
  政府簽署合作備忘錄，承諾遵守國家「數據中心期望標準」，包括承擔輸電及基礎設施費用
  並向澳大利亞機構提供優惠條款；南澳州的100%可再生電網地位及現有超大規模接入範本
  （IREN邦迪，330千伏，800兆瓦）使South Energy的管線地塊成為這筆創紀錄承諾中
  用於新建超大規模設施的天然落地首選。【高度相關】
  資料來源：Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/microsoft-pledges-18bn-to-expanding-cloud-and-ai-capacity-in-australia/

• 在2026年澳大利亞能源周上，數據中心開發商公開主張，在獲得可再生能源信用的同時，
  亦應被允許運行柴油備用發電機組以保障正常運行時間——此立場與南澳州的清潔能源治理
  框架及聯邦「期望標準」存在直接矛盾；South Energy以有保障可再生能源及電池儲能為
  基礎的地塊，為買家提供從結構上更乾淨的解決方案，規避了這一新興監管和聲譽風險，
  並構成可市場化的差異化賣點。
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-says-they-will-drive-down-network-part-of-bills-and-want-us-to-embrace-their-diesel-generators/


📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 昆士蘭州和北部地區（NT）已正式反對聯邦政府將數據中心「自備可再生能源」義務立法化
  的計劃，在澳大利亞形成兩速並行的數據中心政策格局——南澳州已在自願基礎上採用
  「自備可再生能源」交易結構（如IREN邦迪項目），在立法層面「領先於法律」，
  可向買家展示其免受「持異見州份」項目在法規最終通過後所需承擔合規轉型成本的
  結構性優勢；各州的抵制恰恰加速了南澳州合規項目的相對競爭優勢。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centre-power-struggle-as-hold-out-states-oppose-federal-push-for-byo-renewables/

• 澳大利亞能源市場委員會（AEMC）裁定數據中心必須自行提供清潔、有保障的能源，
  而非從共享電網中取用；在AEMO、聯邦政府五項「期望標準」及AEMC三方監管機構
  一致要求自備清潔能源的背景下，已嵌入有保障可再生能源的南澳州項目在結構上
  完全符合這一新興多監管機構共識，而其他州份的項目則面臨日益上升的改造風險。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/aemc-says-data-centres-must-bring-their-own-clean-firmed-energy/


🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

• ESD News分析確認，只要南澳州能持續解鎖停滯中的可再生能源項目，數據中心建設浪潮
  並不會推高消費者電費——直接驗證了South Energy「土地＋可再生能源整合」模式的核心
  經濟邏輯：這既是政治上可持續的，也是市場接受的南澳州數據中心開發路徑，
  且使打包了可再生能源協同配置的項目可在政府面前呈現為消費者利好。【高度相關】
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/data-centre-boom-wont-mean-higher-prices-if-we-unlock-stalled-renewables/

• RenewEconomy分析文章（「100%可再生，74%的時間」）詳述南澳州藉其世界領先可再生
  電網打造能源出口強州的戰略藍圖，ElectraNet明確以25吉瓦電網容量為目標，南澳州輸電
  網絡正吸引數十年來最高水平的接入咨詢量；分析強調，具備直接高壓接入南澳州骨幹
  輸電網條件的土地，才是市場競相爭奪的核心稀缺資源——其優先性超越土地面積或地理
  位置本身——South Energy的地塊評估應將變電站毗鄰度列為決定性收購標準。
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/100-pct-renewable-74-pct-of-the-time-how-australias-most-advanced-grid-plans-to-be-an-energy-powerhouse/
