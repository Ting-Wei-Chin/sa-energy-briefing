📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 25 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• BREAKING (24–25 Aug 2026): Bloomberg and ABC News report that AEMO has sharply revised
  upward its data centre power demand forecast — Australian computing facilities are now
  expected to consume 34 TWh per year by 2035–36, a sevenfold increase from current levels,
  lifting data centres' share of National Electricity Market consumption from 3% to 13%;
  this revision directly increases the urgency and scale of grid investment required in South
  Australia, and signals that early-mover grid connection positions — of the type South Energy
  is securing — will attract significant scarcity premiums as the grid tightens. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Bloomberg | https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/australia-data-center-power-use-seen-jumping-sevenfold-by-2036
  Source: ABC News | https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-25/data-centre-power-hungry-demand-2036/107070852

• IREN Limited (NASDAQ: IREN), a US-listed AI infrastructure operator, signed a transmission
  connection agreement on 3 June 2026 to support an 800MW data centre campus at Bundey,
  South Australia — securing four 330kV feeder exits at ElectraNet's Bundey substation,
  the same 330kV node that is the focal point of ElectraNet's $3.5 billion Northern
  Transmission Project; the agreement is structured to support up to 800MW without requiring
  network upgrades, with staged energisation from 2028 and a total investment of approximately
  A$10 billion; this is the largest single announced data centre project in South Australia
  to date, directly validates South Energy's thesis that Bundey-corridor sites are
  high-value data-centre-ready land, and is a concrete buying signal from a listed
  hyperscale-class operator active in the same geography. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: GlobeNewsWire / IREN | https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305925/0/en/IREN-Announces-First-Australian-Data-Center-Campus-800MW-in-South-Australia.html
  Source: Data Centre Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/iren-targets-australia-for-800mw-data-center-campus/
  Source: Data Centre News Asia | https://datacenternews.asia/story/iren-signs-deal-for-800mw-south-australia-data-centre


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• AEMO's June 2026 Electricity Statement of Opportunities has disclosed an Australian data
  centre pipeline of 5.4 GW — the first time AEMO has publicly quantified the scale of the
  national data centre load pipeline in the NEM — confirming the structural shift in South
  Australia's grid investment and planning environment; a material share of this pipeline
  is expected to seek connection in South Australia given the state's 100% net renewable
  energy target (on track for 2027) and superior co-incident wind-solar resource profile
  relative to other NEM jurisdictions.
  Source: Australia Data Centre Index / AEMO | https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/aemo-5-4gw-australian-data-centre-pipeline-june-2026

• IREN explicitly cited South Australia's status as Australia's first grid targeting 100%
  net renewable energy by 2027 as a primary site selection criterion for its Bundey campus,
  alongside submarine fibre connectivity to Singapore, Indonesia, South Korea, and Japan;
  this confirms that South Australia's renewable energy credentials are now a commercially
  decisive factor — not merely a sustainability aspiration — for large-scale data centre
  operators choosing between Australian states. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• IREN's 800MW Bundey campus (announced 3 June 2026) is the single most significant buyer
  signal for SA data centre land to date: a listed operator with a mandate to build AI
  infrastructure at hyperscale has selected South Australia specifically, committed A$10
  billion, and contracted grid capacity at the Bundey 330kV node — confirming that
  well-located SA land with confirmed grid access and renewable credentials is actively being
  acquired by global data centre capital; South Energy should monitor IREN's future land
  acquisition activity for adjacent sites as the campus expands through its staged build-out
  to 800MW, with the project expected to create 500+ construction jobs and 200+ ongoing
  full-time positions. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: IREN / Nasdaq | https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/iren-announces-first-australian-data-center-campus-800mw-south-australia-2026-06-03
  Source: w.media | https://w.media/iren-targets-bundey-transmission-hub-for-800mw-australian-data-centre-campus/

• The August 24–25 Bloomberg/ABC data centre power demand revision (34 TWh by 2035–36,
  sevenfold increase) is generating significant attention from Australian electricity market
  participants and is expected to accelerate due diligence timelines for operators seeking
  SA land positions before grid headroom at Bundey and other 330kV nodes tightens; AWS
  and Microsoft have collectively committed approximately A$45 billion to Australian digital
  infrastructure, with potential SA-specific site announcements expected as the Bundey
  corridor is validated by the IREN transaction.
  Source: Bloomberg | https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/australia-data-center-power-use-seen-jumping-sevenfold-by-2036


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• KEY UPDATE: The AEMC (Australian Energy Market Commission) delivered its advice to the
  Albanese Government on 28 July 2026 and released its findings publicly on 5 August 2026,
  in response to the Prime Minister's 15 July announcement requiring large-scale data centres
  to become net renewable generators; the AEMC's first recommendation requires data centres
  to offset their electricity consumption using certificates (REGOs) linked to NEW renewable
  energy generation — not legacy certificates — establishing a "net generation" obligation
  directly aligned with South Energy's integrated model of co-located generation plus data
  centre load; proposals that demonstrate underwriting of new renewable supply will be
  prioritised through Commonwealth regulatory processes, with full legislative implementation
  expected in early 2027. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: PV Tech | https://www.pv-tech.org/australias-data-centre-energy-rules-take-shape-as-aemc-publishes-framework-and-nsw-moves-to-control-grid-access/
  Source: Mallesons | https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/big-day-for-data-centres.html
  Source: White & Case | https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/australian-ai-update-pms-ai-and-data-centre-speech-key-takeaways-data-centre

• The Australian Government's March 23, 2026 "National Expectations for Data Centres and AI
  Infrastructure" framework — covering energy underwriting, grid cost recovery, and
  innovation access obligations — underpins the AEMC advice above; under this framework,
  data centre operators are expected to pay their full share of new grid connectivity costs,
  which directly advantages South Energy's integrated project model (where grid costs are
  embedded in the project structure rather than borne separately by the data centre buyer)
  and should be emphasised in South Energy's buyer pitch materials as a cost advantage
  versus stand-alone data centre procurement. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Dept of Industry | https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/expectations-data-centres-and-ai-infrastructure-developers
  Source: Norton Rose Fulbright | https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/746b2689/new-expectations-for-data-centre-operators-and-ai-infrastructure-developers


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• The IREN Bundey campus directly establishes a market comparable for SA data centre land
  values: a $10 billion, 800MW project anchored to the Bundey 330kV substation, with
  energisation from 2028, implies a land-and-grid-access package being valued at a
  significant premium by listed international capital; the Bundey substation is the same
  infrastructure node highlighted in ElectraNet's TAPR as the centre of the $3.5 billion
  Northern Transmission Project — the geographic and infrastructure overlap with South
  Energy's site portfolio in the Spencer Gulf / Mid North corridor makes this directly
  comparable transaction data. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: DataCenterMap | https://www.datacentermap.com/australia/adelaide/iren-bundey/

• Australia's colocation data centre portfolio now comprises 146 existing facilities and 58
  upcoming data centres (per the 2026 national portfolio analysis), with primary market
  supply constraints in Sydney and Melbourne driving operators to evaluate regional and
  alternative-state options; South Australia — with confirmed grid access, a renewable
  energy target, and the IREN validation — is increasingly positioned as the viable
  third-market alternative for operators priced out of or underserved by eastern seaboard
  primary markets.
  Source: Yahoo Finance / Research Report | https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/australia-colocation-data-center-portfolio-080500611.html

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年8月25日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• 【最新消息 — 8月24至25日】Bloomberg及澳大利亞廣播公司（ABC News）報道，AEMO已大幅上調其數
  據中心電力需求預測——澳大利亞計算設施的年用電量預計在2035至2036年度達到34太瓦時，較目前水平
  增長7倍，數據中心在全國電力市場（NEM）總消費中的佔比將從3%提升至13%；此次預測修正直接加大了南
  澳州電網投資的緊迫性和規模，並表明像South Energy正在積累的早期電網接入頭寸將隨著電網容量趨緊而
  獲得顯著稀缺溢價。【高度相關】
  資料來源：Bloomberg | https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/australia-data-center-power-use-seen-jumping-sevenfold-by-2036
  資料來源：ABC News | https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-25/data-centre-power-hungry-demand-2036/107070852

• IREN Limited（納斯達克股票代碼：IREN）——美國上市AI基礎設施運營商——於2026年6月3日簽署輸電接
  入協議，計劃在南澳州班迪（Bundey）建設一個800兆瓦數據中心園區；協議在ElectraNet班迪330千伏變
  電站確保了四條330kV饋線出口——正是ElectraNet 35億澳元「北部輸電項目」的核心樞紐節點；協議結
  構支持在無需網絡升級的前提下接入高達800兆瓦負荷，分階段投運從2028年起，總投資約100億澳元。這
  是迄今南澳州宣布的最大單體數據中心項目，直接驗證了South Energy關於「班迪走廊地塊是高價值數據中
  心就緒型土地」的核心論斷，並為上市超大規模級運營商在同一地理區域的活躍採購行為提供了具體依據。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：GlobeNewsWire / IREN | https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305925/0/en/IREN-Announces-First-Australian-Data-Center-Campus-800MW-in-South-Australia.html
  資料來源：Data Centre Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/iren-targets-australia-for-800mw-data-center-campus/
  資料來源：Data Centre News Asia | https://datacenternews.asia/story/iren-signs-deal-for-800mw-south-australia-data-centre


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• AEMO於2026年6月發布的《電力供應機會聲明》首次公開量化了全國數據中心負荷管線規模——澳大利亞
  數據中心管線總量達5.4吉瓦（GW），確認了南澳州電網投資與規劃環境的結構性轉變；考慮到南澳州在
  NEM各司法管轄區中具備100%淨可再生能源目標（預計2027年實現）和最優風光協同資源稟賦，預計這一
  管線中有相當大比例將在南澳州尋求電網接入。
  資料來源：澳大利亞數據中心指數 / AEMO | https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/aemo-5-4gw-australian-data-centre-pipeline-june-2026

• IREN明確將南澳州「2027年成為澳大利亞首個實現100%淨可再生能源電網」作為選址班迪園區的首要標準
  之一，同時考量了其通往新加坡、印度尼西亞、韓國及日本主要需求中心的海底光纜接入優勢；這一表態
  確認南澳州的可再生能源資質已成為大型數據中心運營商在澳大利亞各州之間進行選址決策時的商業核心
  因素，而非僅是可持續發展願景。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• IREN 800兆瓦班迪園區（2026年6月3日宣布）是迄今南澳州數據中心土地領域最重要的買家信號：一家
  肩負超大規模AI基礎設施建設使命的上市運營商已明確選擇南澳州、承諾約100億澳元投資，並在班迪
  330千伏節點鎖定電網容量——印證了「具備確認電網接入和可再生能源資質的南澳州優質地塊正在被全球
  數據中心資本積極採購」的市場論斷；South Energy應持續追蹤IREN未來在相鄰地塊的土地獲取動向，其
  分階段擴容至800兆瓦的建設計劃預計將帶動連鎖採購活動，並創造500個以上建設期就業崗位及200個以
  上長期全職崗位。【高度相關】
  資料來源：IREN / Nasdaq | https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/iren-announces-first-australian-data-center-campus-800mw-south-australia-2026-06-03
  資料來源：w.media | https://w.media/iren-targets-bundey-transmission-hub-for-800mw-australian-data-centre-campus/

• 8月24至25日Bloomberg / ABC關於數據中心電力需求修正（2035–36年達34太瓦時，增長7倍）的報道在
  澳大利亞電力市場引發廣泛關注，預計將加速各運營商在班迪及其他330千伏節點電網容量趨緊前的南澳州
  土地頭寸盡職調查；AWS與微軟合計已承諾約450億澳元的澳大利亞數字基礎設施投資，隨著IREN交易驗
  證班迪走廊價值，南澳州相關地塊公告有望在後續加速落地。

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 【關鍵更新】澳大利亞能源市場委員會（AEMC）於2026年7月28日向阿爾巴尼斯政府提交建議，並於8月
  5日公開發布調查結果，回應總理7月15日宣布的「要求大型數據中心成為可再生能源淨發電方」政策；
  AEMC首要建議要求數據中心通過與「新建」可再生能源項目掛鉤的REGO（可再生電力保證原產地）證書
  抵消電力消耗——明確排除存量舊證書的使用，確立了「淨發電義務」——該框架與South Energy「發電+
  負荷」一體化項目模式高度契合；能夠證明承銷新建可再生能源供應的項目申請將在聯邦政府監管審批流
  程中獲得優先處理，全面立法預計於2027年初完成。【高度相關】

  承銷、電網費用分攤及創新生態系統接入義務——為上述AEMC建議提供了政策基礎；在此框架下，數據中
  心運營商需承擔新建電網接入的全額費用，這使得South Energy的一體化項目結構（電網費用已內嵌於項
  目設計，無需買家單獨承擔）相較市場上獨立的數據中心採購具有顯著成本優勢，應在South Energy的
  買家推介材料中予以重點強調。【高度相關】
