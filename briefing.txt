  Victoria [HIGH RELEVANCE]: Australian data centre developer HyperDC Pty Ltd lodged a

  Sustainable Data Centre Action Plan BYO-renewables, own-connection-cost and community-
  siting requirements.
  Source: Data Center Dynamics (29 September 2026) |
  https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/plans-filed-for-400mw-data-center-campus-outside-melbourne-australia/
  [HIGH RELEVANCE — The HyperDC Moorabool filing is the first large-scale greenfield data
  centre planning application lodged in Victoria since the Action Plan was gazetted (22
  September 2026). At 39 hectares and 400 MW, the project is structurally comparable in
  footprint to South Energy's industrial corridor targets — yet its developer must now self-
  fund 100% renewable match, bear own grid connection and network upgrade costs, and
  demonstrate compliance with water recycling requirements. Those obligations do not apply to
  SA Coordinator-General-approved sites, which connect directly to a fully-renewable grid
  under a single-authority decision pathway. The Moorabool filing confirms that greenfield
  capacity demand is real and developers will absorb material compliance costs when SA sites
  are unavailable — validating the scarcity premium on South Energy's existing SA land options.]
