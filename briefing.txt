🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• AMPYR Australia Receives AEMO 5.3.4 Technical Approval for 270MW Grid-Forming
  Battery at Port Augusta — Provides Critical Firming Capacity in the Bundey
  Data Centre Corridor [HIGH RELEVANCE]: AMPYR Australia has received a 5.3.4
  letter from the Australian Energy Market Operator (AEMO) for its 270MW Northern
  Battery project near Port Augusta, South Australia — the formal technical
  performance standards confirmation required before a generator can connect to
  the National Electricity Market; the project is a grid-forming battery energy
  storage system (BESS), meaning it provides synthetic inertia and system strength
  in addition to energy storage, directly addressing the minimum system strength
  requirements that AEMO must manage as the SA grid transitions to high renewable
  penetration; for South Energy, the Port Augusta 270MW BESS sits within the same
  transmission corridor as IREN's planned 800MW Bundey data centre campus and the
  ElectraNet $3.5 billion Northern Transmission Project upgrade — its AEMO
  approval is independent confirmation that the corridor can support additional
  large BESS connections, and that grid-forming firming capacity is now being
  installed at scale in the exact geographic region underpinning South Energy's
  target development sites; buyers evaluating SA project engineering can reference
  this approval as evidence that the firming and system-strength architecture
  required to support continuous 100MW+ data centre loads is being constructed
  commercially, not just planned.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Energy Storage News (energy-storage.news) |
  https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/

• AEMO Formally Discloses 5.4GW Australian Data Centre Grid Connection Pipeline
  as of June 2026; Pipeline Now Exceeds Total Current Installed Data Centre
  Capacity by a Factor of More Than Five [HIGH RELEVANCE]: AEMO has formally
  disclosed that its current grid connection pipeline for Australian data centres
  stands at 5.4GW as of June 2026 — representing the formal queue of data centre
  projects that have engaged AEMO and the relevant network service providers to
  begin connection assessment, and the most authoritative official quantification
  of committed near-term data centre infrastructure investment; this 5.4GW
  formal connection pipeline is separate from announced but unqueued projects and
  provides AEMO's own view of credible development activity rather than market
  commentary; for South Energy, AEMO's 5.4GW pipeline disclosure is a direct
  signal that sophisticated buyers are already in the formal connection queue at
  scale — meaning delay in presenting a shovel-ready SA project costs positioning
  against sites already in that queue, and confirming that any SA project with
  an existing or reserved connection agreement is now a materially more valuable
  asset relative to projects that must enter a congested 5.4GW queue from scratch.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]

🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• Deloitte August 2026 Project Tracker Records 33 Committed Australian Data Centre
  Projects Worth A$104 Billion; CommBank Projects A$150 Billion in National Data
  Centre Construction by 2030; Market Now Flagging 26% Power Bill Risk Without
  Renewable Firming [HIGH RELEVANCE]: Deloitte's August 2026 project tracker has
  identified 33 Australian data centre projects representing a combined A$104
  billion in committed capital — the most comprehensive August snapshot of the
  national committed pipeline and a significant escalation from earlier 2026
  figures; separately, Commonwealth Bank of Australia projects the entire national
  data centre build-out will reach A$150 billion of construction activity by 2030,
  framing data centres as the single largest infrastructure investment category in
  Australia's near-term capital programme; concurrent analysis flags that without
  adequate renewable firming and grid investment, Australia's data centre expansion
  risks triggering a 26% spike in power bills for consumers and businesses, creating
  direct political pressure on state and federal governments to fast-track projects
  that come grid-ready with renewable supply already contracted; for South Energy,
  the Deloitte A$104 billion figure and CommBank A$150 billion projection confirm
  that the buyer and investor universe for SA project exits is operating at a
  capital commitment scale that materially exceeds the SA development pipeline
  value, and the 26% power bill risk narrative increases urgency for governments
  to approve and facilitate data centre developments that demonstrably reduce
  rather than increase systemic power cost pressure — precisely the profile of a
  Bundey or Port Augusta project with co-located renewable supply and BESS firming
  already in place.

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• Norton Rose Fulbright Publishes Formal Client Guidance on Australian
  Government's New Data Centre Developer Expectations — Second Major Law Firm
  in 48 Hours to Issue Compliance Analysis, Confirming Regulatory Framework
  is Now Fully Actionable [HIGH RELEVANCE]: Global law firm Norton Rose Fulbright
  has published a formal client alert titled "New expectations for data centre
  operators and AI infrastructure developers," providing legal analysis of the
  Australian Government's expectations framework for data centre developers —
  including requirements to underwrite new renewable energy supply, bear the full
  cost of new grid connectivity so charges are not passed to consumers, and
  support demand flexibility mechanisms; this publication follows King & Wood
  Mallesons' "Big day for data centres" publication (covered in yesterday's
  briefing) and confirms that a second major global law firm has now issued
  formal client compliance guidance on the same regulatory package within the
  same 48-hour window; the publication of parallel substantive guidance by both
  KWM and Norton Rose Fulbright is itself a market signal — it means sophisticated
  buyers are receiving legal advice from multiple counsel simultaneously, and that
  the legal due diligence workstream for SA project acquisitions is now formally
  underway in the market; for South Energy, Norton Rose Fulbright's analysis
  reinforces that any SA project with existing renewable supply agreements, grid
  connection protocols, and planning approvals already in place arrives at a
  buyer's legal due diligence fully configured against both firms' compliance
  checklists — eliminating the procurement, negotiation, and approval timelines
  that NRF specifically identifies as risk factors for developers starting fresh
  in NSW or Victoria.

  development pipeline is now considered institutional-grade infrastructure by
  global PE capital allocators; the GreenSquareDC transaction establishes a
  precedent valuation and deal structure for Australian data centre developer
  acquisitions, including land options as core assets; for South Energy, the
  Partners Group/GreenSquareDC transaction is a direct validation that a
  development-ready SA project with secured land, grid connection, and planning
  approvals represents the same asset class that global institutional capital is
  now acquiring directly — and that South Energy's project pipeline is a credible
  exit target for institutions of Partners Group's scale and sophistication,
  including PE platforms that may not have previously been identified as target
  buyers in the context of hyperscaler bilateral transactions.

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年9月3日

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• AMPYR澳大利亞奧古斯塔港270兆瓦網格成形儲能電池獲AEMO技術認可——
  為班迪數據中心走廊提供關鍵調峰容量【高度相關】：AMPYR澳大利亞已獲得
  澳大利亞能源市場運營商（AEMO）針對其位於南澳州奧古斯塔港附近的270兆瓦
  「北部電池」（Northern Battery）項目出具的5.3.4認可函，這是發電商接入
  國家電力市場（NEM）前必須取得的正式技術性能標準確認文件；該項目為網格
  成形儲能電池系統（BESS），可在提供儲能的同時輸出合成慣量和系統強度，
  直接解決AEMO在南澳州電網向高可再生能源滲透率過渡期間必須管理的最低系
  統強度要求；對South Energy而言，奧古斯塔港270兆瓦BESS與IREN計劃中的
  800兆瓦班迪數據中心園區，以及ElectraNet 35億澳元北部輸電項目升級工程
  處於同一輸電走廊之內——AEMO的認可批准是對該走廊可支持更多大型BESS
  接入的獨立確認，且在南Energy目標開發場地所在地理區域，網格成形調峰容
  量正在以商業規模安裝；評估南澳州項目工程技術條件的買方，可將此次批准
  作為支撐100兆瓦以上數據中心連續運行所需調峰和系統強度架構已在商業上
  落實建設的有力佐證。【高度相關】
  資料來源：Energy Storage News（energy-storage.news）|
  https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/

• AEMO正式披露截至2026年6月澳大利亞數據中心電網接入申請管線達5.4吉瓦，
  規模超過現有全國數據中心裝機容量的五倍以上【高度相關】：澳大利亞能源
  市場運營商（AEMO）正式披露，截至2026年6月，其受理的澳大利亞數據中心
  電網接入申請管線達5.4吉瓦——代表已啟動接入評估流程的數據中心項目的正
  式排隊規模，是目前最具權威性的官方近期數據中心基礎設施投資量化數據；
  這一5.4吉瓦正式接入管線有別於已公告但未提交申請的項目，體現的是AEMO
  自身對有效開發活動的判斷，而非市場評論；對South Energy而言，AEMO
  5.4吉瓦管線披露直接表明，精明的買方已以龐大規模進入正式接入排隊流程
  ——這意味著延誤提交一個「可即時啟動」南澳州項目，將喪失相對已在隊列
  中的場地的先發優勢，同時確認任何持有現有或已預留接入協議的南澳州項目，
  相對於必須從頭進入擁擠的5.4吉瓦隊列的項目，已具備實質性更高的資產
  價值。【高度相關】

🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• 德勤2026年8月項目追蹤報告錄得33個澳大利亞數據中心項目，合計承諾資本
  1040億澳元；澳大利亞聯邦銀行預測2030年前全國數據中心建設規模達1500億
  澳元；市場警示若無可再生調峰，電費恐大漲26%【高度相關】：德勤2026年
  8月項目追蹤器已識別33個澳大利亞數據中心項目，承諾資本合計1040億澳元
  ——這是迄今最全面的全國承諾管線8月快照，較2026年早期數據顯著升級；另
  據澳大利亞聯邦銀行預測，全國數據中心建設活動到2030年將達1500億澳元，
  將數據中心定位為澳大利亞近期最大單一基礎設施投資品類；同期分析警示，
  若缺乏充足的可再生調峰和電網投資，澳大利亞的數據中心擴張可能導致用電
  客戶和企業電費上漲26%，由此直接形成對各州和聯邦政府加快審批已具備電
  網就緒條件及已簽訂可再生能源供應合約項目的政治壓力；對South Energy而
  言，德勤1040億澳元數據及聯邦銀行1500億澳元預測，確認南澳州項目退出的
  買家和投資者圈子所運作的資本承諾規模，已實質性超過南澳州開發管線總值，
  而26%電費上漲風險的輿論壓力，進一步凸顯政府批准和推動那些可明確降低
  而非加劇系統性電力成本壓力的數據中心開發項目的緊迫性——這正是配套共
  址可再生能源供應和BESS調峰的班迪或奧古斯塔港項目的核心差異化優勢所在。

  正式客戶指引——48小時內第二家頂級律師事務所發布合規分析，確認監管框架
  已完全可操作【高度相關】：全球律師事務所富而德（Norton Rose Fulbright）
  發布題為「數據中心運營商及AI基礎設施開發商的新期望」的正式客戶簡訊，
  就澳大利亞政府對數據中心開發商的期望框架提供法律解析——包括承擔新可

  響應機制的要求；此次發布緊隨昨日簡報所述金杜（King & Wood Mallesons）
