📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 08 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] IREN secured a transmission connection agreement at ElectraNet's
  new Bundey substation (SA's first 330kV facility) for its planned 800MW AI data
  center campus — four 330kV feeder exits committed, capable of supporting up to 800MW
  without requiring further network upgrades.
  Source: GlobeNewswire / IREN – June 3, 2026
  https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305925/0/en/IREN-Announces-First-Australian-Data-Center-Campus-800MW-in-South-Australia.html

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] ElectraNet's 2026 Transmission Annual Planning Report (TAPR)
  unveiled a $3.5 billion Northern Transmission Project (early planning stage) to increase
  transfer capacity between Bundey and the Adelaide metro load centre, and Bundey to the
  anticipated Cultana load centre — driven by data centre and defence demand growth. The
  report also flags a $350M Eyre Peninsula Upgrade (Yadnarie North substation, 275/132kV)
  targeted for 2027-28.
  Source: ESD News / ElectraNet TAPR – March 31, 2026
  https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/
  https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/

• Project EnergyConnect (SA-NSW interconnector, 900km) is approaching full 800MW
  transfer capability mid-2026 following Stage 1 commissioning (180MW) and pending
  Stage 2 testing; ElectraNet's new Bundey substation on this route is the primary
  connection hub for large new loads in the Mid North region.
  Source: Project EnergyConnect / Utility Magazine
  https://utilitymagazine.com.au/project-energyconnect-successfully-delivering-electranets-largest-transmission-project/

• AEMO disclosed that approximately 5.4 GW of Australian data centre capacity is now
  moving through the transmission connection queue as of June 2026, highlighting
  unprecedented demand and early-mover advantage for projects already in queue.
  Source: Australia Data Centre Index – June 2026
  https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/aemo-5-4gw-australian-data-centre-pipeline-june-2026

• ElectraNet published an updated Customer Connection Guide (v1, 30 March 2026),
  streamlining the process for large new loads seeking connection to the SA transmission
  network — useful reference for project developers planning grid-tied data centre sites.
  Source: ElectraNet.com.au – March 30, 2026
  https://electranet.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/New-Customer-Connection-Guide-V1.pdf


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] South Australia opened more than 11,000 sq km of new Crown land
  for renewable energy development across two release areas: Gawler Ranges East
  (~5,200 sq km, Upper Eyre Peninsula) and Whyalla West (~6,500 sq km, Upper Spencer
  Gulf). Both areas feature some of SA's highest co-incident wind and solar resources
  and could power 500,000+ homes — prime co-location candidates for data centre
  renewable PPAs. Tender applications closed June 28, 2026.
  Source: SA Government / ESD News / PV Magazine – April 7-8, 2026
  https://esdnews.com.au/south-australia-opens-new-areas-for-renewables-investment/
  https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/south-australia-open-for-more-renewable-energy-investment
  https://solarquarter.com/2026/04/08/south-australia-opens-11000-sq-km-land-for-large-scale-renewable-energy-investment/

• SA is averaging 75% net variable renewable energy annually and regularly achieves
  100% instantaneous renewable generation; the state grid is on track to reach 100%
  net renewables by end of 2027 — a key selling point for hyperscalers with net-zero
  targets.
  Source: SA Government energymining.sa.gov.au
  https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/industry/hydrogen-and-renewable-energy/leading-the-green-economy

• AEMO's 2026 Integrated System Plan confirmed that Australia's least-cost energy
  future is renewable energy supported by storage and transmission — reinforcing SA's
  competitive advantage as a low-cost, high-renewable site for data centre operations.
  Source: ESD News / AEMO ISP 2026
  https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/

• AMPYR Australia received a 5.3.4 confirmation letter from AEMO for a 270MW
  grid-forming BESS near Port Augusta, SA, targeting financial close and construction
  in Q4 2026 — strengthening SA grid stability for large new data centre loads.
  Source: Energy Storage News – 2026
  https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/

• No significant new renewable energy project announcements specific to SA in the last
  48 hours — the Gawler Ranges East / Whyalla West tender outcomes remain the most
  recent material development.


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] IREN Limited (NASDAQ: IREN) announced its first Australian data
  centre campus: an 800MW AI facility in Bundey, SA, citing SA's 100% net renewable
  grid, new 330kV transmission infrastructure and subsea fibre connectivity to
  Singapore, Indonesia, South Korea and Japan. IREN projects 200+ skilled local jobs.
  This is the single largest announced data centre in Australian history and demonstrates
  that hyperscale buyers are actively evaluating SA.
  Source: GlobeNewswire / RenewEconomy – June 3, 2026
  https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305925/0/en/IREN-Announces-First-Australian-Data-Center-Campus-800MW-in-South-Australia.html
  https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/

• Global hyperscalers collectively plan US$725 billion in CapEx in 2026 (Amazon $200B,
  Google $175-185B, Meta $115-135B, Microsoft $110-120B) — a 77% increase over 2025
  — with Australia having secured approximately A$45B in committed hyperscaler digital
  infrastructure investment over a 22-month window (AWS and Microsoft confirmed).
  Source: Data Center Knowledge / ValueAdd VC – 2026
  https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/hyperscalers/hyperscalers-in-2026-what-s-next-for-the-world-s-largest-data-center-operators-

• Private equity firm Partners Group acquired Australian data centre provider
  GreenSquareDC, which holds brownfield sites and adjoining landbanks in Sydney capable
  of expansion to 96MW — indicating PE capital is actively pursuing Australian data
  centre land assets.
  Source: Data Center Dynamics
  https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/partners-group-acquires-australian-data-center-firm-greensquaredc/

• Australian Energy Week 2026 (Melbourne, June 2026) saw 1,200+ energy industry
  leaders convene, with data centres and network costs dominating discussion —
  underscoring the sector's centrality to Australia's energy market evolution.
  Source: ESD News
  https://esdnews.com.au/australian-energy-week-2026/

• No new hyperscaler site announcements specific to SA in the last 48 hours; IREN's
  Bundey announcement remains the benchmark active deal in the state.


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] The Australian Prime Minister announced on 15 July 2026 that
  large-scale AI data centres will face mandatory Australian Standards requiring
  operators to "put at least as much energy into our grid as they take out." National
  Cabinet is expected to consider the legislative framework in August 2026, with
  legislation expected in early 2027. A new Office of AI within the Department of
  PM&C will fast-track approvals for compliant projects.
  Source: White & Case / GT Law Australia – July 2026
  https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/australian-ai-update-pms-ai-and-data-centre-speech-key-takeaways-data-centre
  https://www.gtlaw.com.au/insights/australian-government-announces-mandatory-ai-standards-for-large-scale-data-centres-and-new-office-of-ai

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] The Australian Government published its National Expectations for
  Data Centres and AI Infrastructure (23 March 2026) — five voluntary expectations that
  guide Commonwealth regulatory prioritisation. Projects closely aligned with these
  expectations will be fast-tracked through federal approval processes. Key areas:
  energy efficiency, renewable energy integration, supply chain security and community
  benefit.
  Source: Dept. of Industry, Science & Resources / HSF Kramer / Clayton Utz – March 2026
  https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/expectations-data-centres-and-ai-infrastructure-developers
  https://www.hsfkramer.com/insights/2026-03/national-expectations-for-the-development-of-data-centres-and-ai-infrastructure-have-been-released-what-you-need-to-know
  https://www.claytonutz.com/insights/2026/march/great-expectations-what-the-australian-government-requires-of-data-centres-and-ai-infrastructure

• South Australia introduced Technical Regulator Guidelines (February 2026) requiring
  data centres of 100MW or more to obtain certification covering power system
  reliability, security and stability, including mandatory backup energy systems and
  compliance with directions during system stress events.
  Source: Ashurst Australian Electricity & Gas Markets Update – Feb/Apr 2026
  https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-april-2026-update/

• South Australia will implement the Managing Moderate Outages (MMO) market mechanism
  from July 2026, with industry co-design of contract terms underway; outcomes to be
  formalised in the National Electricity Law and Rules over 2026.
  Source: Ashurst / AER – 2026
  https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/energy-alert-australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-february-2026-update/

• AEMO's reliability assessment closed a previously identified minor SA reliability gap
  (25MW) for December 2026-February 2027 as of October 2025 — SA grid is forecast to
  be reliable through the upcoming summer period.
  Source: AER – T-3 Reliability Update
  https://www.aer.gov.au/industry/retail/reliability-obligation/south-australia-december-2026-february-2027/t-3

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• 【高度相關】IREN公司已與ElectraNet就南澳州首個330千伏變電站（Bundey變電站）
  簽署輸電接入協議，獲批四條330kV饋線出口，可支持高達800MW的數據中心用電需求，
  且無需進行網絡升級。
  資料來源：GlobeNewswire / IREN – 2026年6月3日
  https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305925/0/en/IREN-Announces-First-Australian-Data-Center-Campus-800MW-in-South-Australia.html

• 【高度相關】ElectraNet在其2026年輸電年度規劃報告（TAPR）中宣布，計劃投資35億澳元
  推進「北部輸電項目」（Northern Transmission Project，目前處於早期規劃階段），旨在
  提升Bundey至阿德萊德都市負荷中心及Bundey至預期Cultana負荷中心的輸電容量；
  此外還規劃「艾爾半島升級項目」（Eyre Peninsula Upgrade，3.5億澳元），預計2027至
  2028年完成，包括建設新Yadnarie North變電站（275/132kV）。上述投資主要由數據中心
  及國防工業需求增長驅動。
  資料來源：ESD News / ElectraNet TAPR – 2026年3月31日
  https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/
  https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/

• EnergyConnect項目（南澳—新南威爾士州900公里輸電聯絡線）預計2026年中達到完整
  800MW輸電能力，第一階段已完成調試（180MW），待第二階段測試完成後全面投入使用；
  Bundey新變電站是中北部地區大型新增負荷的主要接入樞紐。
  資料來源：Project EnergyConnect / Utility Magazine
  https://utilitymagazine.com.au/project-energyconnect-successfully-delivering-electranets-largest-transmission-project/

• 根據AEMO於2026年6月披露的數據，目前約5.4吉瓦（GW）的澳大利亞數據中心容量
  正在輸電接入排隊等候，市場需求空前旺盛，優先進入排隊的項目具有明顯先發優勢。
  資料來源：Australia Data Centre Index – 2026年6月
  https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/aemo-5-4gw-australian-data-centre-pipeline-june-2026

• ElectraNet於2026年3月30日發布最新版《客戶接入指南》（第1版），為尋求接入南澳
  輸電網絡的大型新增負荷提供更清晰的流程指引，可作為項目開發商規劃電網接入場址
  的重要參考。
  資料來源：ElectraNet.com.au – 2026年3月30日
  https://electranet.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/New-Customer-Connection-Guide-V1.pdf


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• 【高度相關】南澳州政府於2026年4月開放逾11,000平方公里的皇家土地用於可再生能源
  開發，涵蓋兩個招標區域：Gawler Ranges East（約5,200平方公里，上艾爾半島）及
  Whyalla West（約6,500平方公里，上斯賓塞灣）。兩個區域均擁有南澳州最高的
  風光互補資源，理論上可滿足逾50萬戶家庭的用電需求，是數據中心配套可再生能源
  購電協議（PPA）的理想選址。招標申請已於2026年6月28日截止。
  資料來源：南澳州政府 / ESD News / PV Magazine – 2026年4月7至8日
  https://esdnews.com.au/south-australia-opens-new-areas-for-renewables-investment/
  https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/south-australia-open-for-more-renewable-energy-investment
  https://solarquarter.com/2026/04/08/south-australia-opens-11000-sq-km-land-for-large-scale-renewable-energy-investment/

• 南澳州目前年均75%的電力來自凈可變可再生能源，並頻繁達到100%即時可再生能源
  發電比例，目標是在2027年底前實現100%凈可再生能源供電——對有淨零排放承諾的
  超大規模雲計算企業具有極強吸引力。
  資料來源：南澳州政府 energymining.sa.gov.au
  https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/industry/hydrogen-and-renewable-energy/leading-the-green-economy

• AEMO發布的2026年《綜合系統規劃》（ISP）確認，澳大利亞最低成本的能源未來路徑
  是可再生能源 + 儲能 + 輸電，進一步鞏固南澳州作為低成本、高可再生能源數據中心
  運營地的競爭優勢。
  資料來源：ESD News / AEMO ISP 2026
  https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/

• AMPYR Australia已獲得AEMO出具的5.3.4確認函，批准其位於南澳州Port Augusta附近
  的270MW電網成型電池儲能系統（Northern Battery），計劃於2026年第四季度完成融資
  及開工建設，將進一步提升南澳電網穩定性，有利於大型新增數據中心負荷接入。
  資料來源：Energy Storage News – 2026
  https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/

• 過去48小時內未有南澳州新可再生能源項目的重大公告；Gawler Ranges East /
  Whyalla West招標為近期最重要的實質性進展。


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• 【高度相關】IREN Limited（納斯達克：IREN）於2026年6月3日宣布澳大利亞首個數據中心
  園區計劃：在南澳州Bundey建設800MW人工智能數據中心，主要原因包括南澳州100%凈
