📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 26 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• BREAKING (Aug 2026): ElectraNet's 2026 Transmission Annual Planning Report (TAPR) reveals
  that South Australia's peak electricity demand is forecast to nearly double from 3.3 GW
  today to more than 6.5 GW — driven primarily by data centre growth and defence industry
  development — identifying three major transmission projects required to serve this load:
  (i) the $3.5 billion Northern Transmission Project (Bundey corridor); (ii) the $350 million
  Eyre Peninsula Upgrade, expected by 2027–28, involving a new 275/132 kV Yadnarie North
  substation and conversion of the Cultana–Yadnarie lines from 132 kV to 275 kV — directly
  serving the Upper Spencer Gulf and Eyre Peninsula region where both Firmus Technologies and
  the SA government's 11,000 sq km renewable land release are concentrated; and
  (iii) a South East Expansion. This "once-in-a-generation" demand growth directly validates
  the sites South Energy is acquiring in the Mid North / Spencer Gulf corridor. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ElectraNet 2026 TAPR | https://electranet.com.au/resource/2026-tapr-transmission-annual-planning-report/
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/
  Source: PV Magazine Australia | https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/
  Source: EcoGeneration | https://www.ecogeneration.com.au/power-demand-in-sa-is-set-to-double

• AEMO has issued a 5.3.4 grid connection approval letter to AMPYR Australia for the 270 MW
  Northern Battery — a grid-forming BESS project to be built on the site of the former
  Northern Power Station coal facility in Port Augusta, South Australia, targeting financial
  close and construction commencement in Q4 2026; the project (270 MW / 1,080 MWh, 8-hour
  duration) will connect via pre-existing substation infrastructure to ElectraNet's
  transmission backbone at the northern end of the SA grid, reinforcing the same transmission
  node that serves the Stirling North / Port Augusta region where Firmus Technologies is
  planning a 1.3 GW AI factory campus; battery partners include Sungrow and APD Global, with
  Green Gold Energy as co-developer; the 5.3.4 letter marks the final major regulatory
  hurdle before construction, making this one of the most advanced large-scale storage
  projects in South Australia. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Energy Storage News | https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/ampyr-acquires-battery-project-from-green-gold-energy/
  Source: IndexBox | https://www.indexbox.io/blog/ampyrs-270mw-northern-battery-secures-key-aemo-grid-connection-approval/

• South Australia's grid reliability position has improved materially: the South Australian
  Government and AGL Energy reached a funding agreement in late 2025 to extend the operation
  of three units of Torrens Island B Power Station (600 MW combined gas capacity) to
  July 2028, eliminating the 390 MW reliability gap previously forecast by AEMO for 2026–27
  following the planned retirement of Torrens Island B; the extension, funded in part by the
  SA Government, removes a near-term grid stability risk and provides the reliability runway
  needed for new renewable and storage projects to come online — including Firmus and AMPYR
  — before gas backup is fully retired. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/south-australia-and-agl-reach-funding-deal-on-torrens-island-gas-extension-due-to-transmission-delays/
  Source: AEMO Media Release | https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/media-release/reliability-outlook-improves-timely-investment-delivery-essential

• Project EnergyConnect — the 900 km NSW-SA interconnector and ElectraNet's largest-ever
  transmission project — is approaching its full 800 MW transfer capability, with Stage 1
  (180 MW) already commissioned and full 800 MW capacity targeted for mid-2026 pending
  Transgrid's completion of Stage 2 on the NSW side; the interconnector includes South
  Australia's first 330 kV substation at Bundey, the same node at which IREN has contracted
  four feeder exits for its 800 MW campus, and its full activation materially increases the
  volume of renewable energy that can be exported from SA to eastern markets — supporting
  the economics of large-scale renewable projects co-located with data centre loads.
  Source: Utility Magazine | https://utilitymagazine.com.au/project-energyconnect-successfully-delivering-electranets-largest-transmission-project/
  Source: Kalkine | https://kalkine.com.au/news/general-news/energyconnect-nears-the-finish-line-as-data-centres-push-australias-grid-to-its-limits


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• KEY OPPORTUNITY (April 2026): The South Australian Government opened more than 11,000 sq km
  of Crown land for large-scale renewable energy investment across two release areas: Gawler
  Ranges East (~5,200 sq km on the Upper Eyre Peninsula, home to some of SA's highest
  co-incident wind and solar resources) and Whyalla West (~6,500 sq km in the Upper Spencer
  Gulf region); the SA Government called for investors to propose large-scale solar, wind, and
  storage projects with the tender period closing 28 June 2026; the two release areas sit
  within or immediately adjacent to the same Spencer Gulf / Upper Eyre Peninsula geography
  where South Energy is acquiring land, and within the catchment area of the proposed $350M
  Eyre Peninsula Upgrade transmission project — making renewable energy underwriting of data
  centre loads in this corridor structurally more feasible than in any other SA region;
  SA's total renewable energy project pipeline now stands at $32.5 billion. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: PV Magazine International | https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/04/07/south-australia-opens-up-new-areas-for-renewable-energy-opportunities/
  Source: SA Government (Energy & Mining) | https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/south-australia-open-for-more-renewable-energy-investment
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/south-australia-opens-new-areas-for-renewables-investment/
  Source: SolarQuarter | https://solarquarter.com/2026/04/08/south-australia-opens-11000-sq-km-land-for-large-scale-renewable-energy-investment/

• AEMO released its 2026 Integrated System Plan (ISP), confirming that Australia's lowest-cost
  energy transition pathway is renewable energy supported by storage, connected by
  transmission, and backed up by gas — directly aligning with the project model South Energy
  is structuring for its SA sites (renewable generation + storage + grid connection + data
  centre load); the ISP is the foundational document that shapes ElectraNet's capital
  investment priorities, including the Northern Transmission Project and Eyre Peninsula
  Upgrade that will determine which SA sites achieve the strongest grid connectivity.
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/

  April 2026): Firmus has confirmed two AI factory campus sites in South Australia under its
  Project Southgate initiative, representing the second multi-gigawatt land acquisition in
  the state this year after IREN's Bundey campus:
  — TAILEM BEND: 328-hectare site north of Tailem Bend (acquired by a Firmus subsidiary in
    late April 2026) planned for approximately 1.0 GW of critical IT load at full build-out,
    supported by ~1.2 GW of planned power connection capacity.
  — PORT AUGUSTA / STIRLING NORTH: Second site planned for approximately 1.3 GW of critical
    IT load at full build-out, supported by ~1.5 GW of planned power connection capacity.
  Total Project Southgate South Australia capacity: ~2.3 GW IT load / ~2.7 GW power, making
  Firmus the largest announced data centre developer in Australia by planned SA capacity.
  Firmus has also signed a 600 MW renewable energy supply agreement to power the first phase
  of the SA campuses, and has indicated renewable energy plans would be released within weeks.
  Construction timelines and development applications are now the critical gating items.
  South Energy should regard Firmus as an active potential buyer of additional SA sites with
  confirmed grid access and renewable underwriting, given the scale of their SA ambitions.

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• SA STATE POLICY — HIGH RELEVANCE: Premier Peter Malinauskas announced in June 2026 a
  dedicated Data Centre Strategy for South Australia, centred on the proposed Data Centre and
  AI Infrastructure Act — standalone legislation that would designate data centres as
  "essential infrastructure" in South Australia for the first time; key features include:
  (i) a dedicated planning pathway and streamlined approvals for data centre and AI facility
  developments; (ii) empowering the Coordinator-General to take on decision-making functions
  for major data centre projects (equivalent powers to those used for major resource and
  infrastructure projects); (iii) amendments to the Planning, Development and Infrastructure
  Act; and (iv) explicit support for renewable-energy-powered developments and partnerships
  with AI research institutions. This legislative framework directly reduces approval risk and
  timeline for projects South Energy intends to sell to data centre buyers — the Coordinator-
  General fast-track pathway is a material competitive advantage for SA over NSW and VIC for
  data centre development. [HIGH RELEVANCE]

  Source: Capital Brief | https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/new-energy-for-new-demand-sa-plans-data-centre-and-ai-infrastructure-act-47dfa4af-f755-494c-981a-121fed28eb67/

🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• DIRECT LAND COMPARABLES — Firmus Technologies has made the two most significant greenfield
  data centre land acquisitions in South Australia in 2026 after IREN's Bundey position:
  — Tailem Bend: 328 hectares acquired April 2026, planned for 1.0 GW IT load; located

  renewable energy supply in the Mid North / Spencer Gulf corridor are being transacted.

☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• 【關鍵機遇 — 2026年4月】南澳州政府開放逾1.1萬平方公里Crown land（皇家土地）供大規模可再生
  能源投資，分設兩個招標片區：戈勒山脈東部（Gawler Ranges East，約5,200平方公里，位於上伊爾半

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 【南澳州重磅政策 — 高度相關】2026年6月，南澳州州長彼得·馬利諾斯卡斯（Peter Malinauskas）
  宣佈南澳州數據中心戰略，核心是擬議出臺的《數據中心與AI基礎設施法案》（Data Centre and AI
  Infrastructure Act）——這將是澳大利亞首部針對數據中心的專項立法，主要內容包括：
  （一）為數據中心和AI設施開發提供專屬規劃審批通道和簡化審批流程；
  （二）授權「總協調人」（Coordinator-General）對重大數據中心項目行使決策職能（相當於重大資源
  和基礎設施項目享有的快速審批授權）；
  （三）修訂《規劃、發展與基礎設施法》，將數據中心列為「基本基礎設施」；
  （四）明確支持以可再生能源供電的數據中心開發及與AI研究機構的合作。
  該立法框架直接降低了South Energy擬出售給數據中心買家的項目的審批風險和周期——「總協調人快速
  審批通道」是南澳州對比新州和維州在數據中心開發方面的重要制度性優勢。【高度相關】
