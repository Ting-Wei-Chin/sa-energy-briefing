📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 24 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• AMPYR Australia has received an AEMO Section 5.3.4 technical compliance letter for its
  270MW Northern Battery project near Port Augusta, South Australia, confirming the project
  meets the technical performance standards required to connect to the National Electricity
  Market; AMPYR is targeting financial close and construction commencement in Q4 2026, adding
  a significant new grid-forming BESS asset in the upper Spencer Gulf/Eyre Peninsula
  transmission corridor — the same region where South Energy's land portfolio is
  concentrated, and where co-located storage is a prerequisite for viable large-load grid
  connections. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Energy Storage News | https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/

• ElectraNet's 2026 TAPR discloses a third major transmission investment — the Northern
  Transmission Project — not highlighted in yesterday's briefing: an estimated A$3.5 billion
  initiative, currently in early planning, that would increase transfer capacity between the
  Bundey substation (South Australia's first 330kV substation, commissioned as part of
  Project EnergyConnect) and both the Adelaide metropolitan load centre and the anticipated
  Cultana load centre; this project directly addresses the bottleneck between the renewable-
  rich Mid North corridor and Adelaide demand, and its progression through AEMO's Project
  Assessment process will be a key timing signal for South Energy's sites that feed into
  the Bundey-to-Adelaide corridor. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: PV Magazine Australia | https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• AEMO has published its 2026 Integrated System Plan (ISP), confirming that Australia's
  least-cost pathway to a reliable, secure electricity system is renewable energy supported
  by storage, connected by transmission and distribution, and backed up by gas; for South
  Australia specifically, the ISP reinforces the investment case for co-located generation
  and storage assets adjacent to large data-centre loads — directly validating South Energy's
  integrated project model — and signals continued AEMO prioritisation of SA transmission
  augmentation to support the state's role as the NEM's leading renewable energy jurisdiction.
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/
  Source: REGlobal | https://reglobal.org/australias-energy-shift-aemo-publishes-draft-2026-integrated-system-plan/

• No new renewable energy licence or land release items for South Australia today beyond
  the Gawler Ranges East and Whyalla West tender results (pending), covered in yesterday's
  briefing. The SA pipeline of A$32.5 billion in large-scale renewable projects under
  construction, assessment, or approved remains the operative backdrop.


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• No new hyperscaler or large-tech-company announcements specific to South Australia were
  published today. The National Cabinet formal decision on the Albanese Government's
  mandatory AI data centre standards (announced 15 July 2026 and scheduled for August
  ministerial consideration) has not yet been publicly confirmed — monitoring continues.
  Once announced, this decision is expected to sharply accelerate buyer due diligence
  activity for SA sites already demonstrating energy and water compliance.

• Market context: Australia's data centre colocation market is forecast to reach USD 2.32
  billion in 2026 (up from USD 1.97 billion in 2025), growing to USD 4.28 billion by 2031,
  reflecting the structural demand tailwind for data-centre-ready land and energy-matched
  projects of the type South Energy is developing.
  Source: Australia Data Center Colocation Data Report 2026 via Yahoo Finance |
  https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/australia-data-center-colocation-data-093200287.html


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• AEMO's latest Reliability Outlook confirms that Australia's reliability position has
  improved overall, with timely delivery of committed investments essential to maintaining
  this trajectory; a minor reliability gap of 25MW is identified in South Australia in
  2026-27 under the Government Schemes and Actionable Developments assessment — a small
  gap that nonetheless signals that any large new load connection (such as a data centre)
  entering the SA market in this window will need to demonstrate firmed supply, reinforcing
  the structural premium on South Energy's integrated generation-plus-load project model
  relative to simple grid-connection approaches. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: AEMO | https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/media-release/reliability-outlook-improves-timely-investment-delivery-essential

• South Australia is proceeding with implementation of the Mandatory Market Obligations
  (MMO) gas market framework from July 2026, with an industry-led contract co-design
  process underway and outcomes to be formalised in the National Electricity Law and Rules
  over 2026; while directly a gas-market reform, the MMO establishes new gas firming
  obligations relevant to any project seeking to use gas backup for energy security — a
  consideration for South Energy projects that include dispatchable firming alongside
  renewables.
  Source: Ashurst | https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-april-2026-update/


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• Partners Group, the global private markets asset manager, has acquired GreenSquareDC,
  an Australian data centre firm, in a deal that includes land options across strategic
  sites in Sydney, Melbourne, and Perth; while not South Australia-specific, the transaction
  is a significant market signal of international institutional capital actively acquiring
  Australian data centre land positions — the same investment thesis South Energy is
  executing in SA — and confirms that data-centre-ready land with confirmed energy and
  planning approvals is trading at a meaningful premium to unimproved land. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Centre Dynamics |
  https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/partners-group-acquires-australian-data-center-firm-greensquaredc/

• No new SA-specific zoning, planning approval, or land transaction news was published
  today. The proposed SA Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act (announced 23 June 2026)
  remains in pre-drafting stage. South Energy should continue front-loading SA Water and
  Technical Regulator engagement to be positioned for fast-tracked approvals once the
  Act is tabled.

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年8月24日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• AMPYR Australia已獲得AEMO第5.3.4條技術合規函（即「5.3.4函」），確認其位於南澳州奧古斯塔
  港（Port Augusta）附近的270兆瓦「北部電池儲能」項目符合接入全國電力市場所需的技術性能標準；
  AMPYR目標於2026年第四季度完成融資關閉並啟動建設，將在斯賓塞灣上游/艾爾半島輸電走廊新增一
  座重要的電網成型型電池儲能系統資產——這一地帶正是South Energy土地組合的核心集中區域。對於
  大型負荷的可行電網接入而言，配套儲能是前提條件，該項目的推進直接強化了本地區協同儲能的戰略
  價值。【高度相關】
  資料來源：Energy Storage News |
  https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/

• ElectraNet 2026年《輸電年度規劃報告》（TAPR）披露了第三個重大輸電投資項目——「北部輸電
  項目」（Northern Transmission Project）——此前昨日簡報未有涉及：該項目預計投資約35億澳元，
  目前處於早期規劃階段，旨在提升班迪（Bundey）變電站（南澳州首座330千伏變電站，作為
  EnergyConnect項目的組成部分已投入使用）與阿德萊德都市用電中心以及預期中的庫爾塔納
  （Cultana）用電中心之間的輸電容量；該項目直接針對Mid North可再生能源富集走廊通往阿德萊德
  需求中心的輸電瓶頸，其在AEMO項目評估流程中的推進進度，將成為South Energy班迪至阿德萊德走
  廊沿線地塊制定開發申請時間表的關鍵信號。【高度相關】
  資料來源：PV Magazine Australia |
  https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/
  資料來源：ESD News |
  https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• AEMO已發布2026年《一體化系統計劃》（Integrated System Plan，ISP），確認澳大利亞實現可靠、
  安全電力系統的最低成本路徑為：可再生能源發電為基礎，儲能提供支撐，依托輸配電網絡互聯，並以
  天然氣作為兜底保障；就南澳州而言，ISP進一步強化了在大型數據中心負荷側協同布局發電與儲能資
  產的投資邏輯——直接驗證了South Energy一體化項目開發模式的正確性——並表明AEMO將持續優先
  推進南澳州輸電擴容，以支持該州作為NEM領先可再生能源司法管轄區的核心地位。
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/
  資料來源：REGlobal |
  https://reglobal.org/australias-energy-shift-aemo-publishes-draft-2026-integrated-system-plan/

• 今日南澳州無新增可再生能源地塊或土地發布信息，蓋勒山脈東區及懷阿拉西區招標結果（詳見昨日
  簡報）仍在審議中。南澳州現有325億澳元大型可再生能源項目管線（含在建、審批中及已獲批項目）
  仍為當前可再生能源開發的總體背景。


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• 今日未發現針對南澳州的超大規模雲服務商或大型科技公司新公告。阿爾巴尼斯政府於2026年7月15日
  宣布的強制性AI數據中心標準提交全國內閣審議（原計劃於8月進行），截至今日仍無正式決定公開確認
  ——持續監測中。一旦決定公布，預計買家對已具備能源和供水合規條件地塊的盡職調查活動將顯著加速。

• 市場背景：澳大利亞數據中心主機代管市場預計於2026年達到23.2億美元（較2025年的19.7億美元上
  升），並有望在2031年增長至42.8億美元，體現了對South Energy正在開發的數據中心就緒型土地及能
  源匹配項目的結構性需求支撐。
  資料來源：2026年澳大利亞數據中心主機代管數據報告（Yahoo Finance）|
  https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/australia-data-center-colocation-data-093200287.html


📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• AEMO最新《可靠性展望》確認澳大利亞整體可靠性形勢有所改善，但強調已承諾投資項目的按時交付
  至關重要；南澳州在「政府計劃與可落地開發」評估口徑下，2026-27年被識別出25兆瓦的小幅可靠性
  缺口——雖然缺口量不大，但這一信號意味著在此窗口期進入南澳州市場的任何大型新增負荷（如數據
  中心）均需具備有保障的電力供應，進一步凸顯了South Energy「發電+負荷」一體化項目模式相對於
  單純電網接入路徑的結構性溢價。【高度相關】
  資料來源：AEMO |
  https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/media-release/reliability-outlook-improves-timely-investment-delivery-essential

• 南澳州正按計劃於2026年7月起推進天然氣強制市場義務（Mandatory Market Obligations，MMO）框架
  落地，行業主導的合同聯合設計流程已啟動，相關成果將在2026年內正式納入《國家電力法》及《國家
  電力規則》；雖然MMO直接針對天然氣市場改革，但它為任何擬將天然氣備用作為能源安全保障的項目
  設立了新的氣體保供義務——對South Energy項目中可再生能源配套氣體調峰安排的設計具有直接參考
  意義。
  資料來源：Ashurst |
  https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-april-2026-update/


🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

• 全球私募市場資產管理機構Partners Group已完成對澳大利亞數據中心企業GreenSquareDC的收購，
  交易附帶悉尼、墨爾本及珀斯戰略性地塊的土地認購期權；雖然本次交易並非針對南澳州，但卻是一個
  重要的市場信號——國際機構資本正在積極在澳大利亞佈局數據中心土地頭寸，這與South Energy正在
  南澳州執行的投資邏輯完全一致——並印證了已獲得能源及規劃審批的數據中心就緒型土地正以顯著溢價
  成交。【高度相關】
  資料來源：Data Centre Dynamics |
  https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/partners-group-acquires-australian-data-center-firm-greensquaredc/

• 今日無新增南澳州土地交易、分區或規劃審批動態。擬議中的《南澳州數據中心與AI基礎設施法》
  （2026年6月23日宣布）仍處於起草前期。South Energy應持續提前推進SA Water及技術監管局的溝通
  對接，以便在法案生效後第一時間進入快速審批通道。
