  Weather, SA Government Questions $300M Upgrade "Failure" (20–21 November 2026) [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  Three ElectraNet transmission towers were brought down by an extreme weather event, triggering a
  major blackout across the entire Eyre Peninsula — the first significant unplanned outage to affect
  the region since the state's $300M network upgrade was completed. The SA government has opened
  scrutiny of ElectraNet's response, questioning whether the upgrade constituted adequate resilience

  connection strategy: (1) it exposes the physical fragility of the Eyre Peninsula corridor serving

  reinforces that inland 330 kV transmission connections such as the Bundey–Adelaide NTx corridor
  offer superior resilience versus coastal Eyre Peninsula feeder circuits; and (3) it is likely to
  accelerate AER scrutiny and potential upward revision of ElectraNet's pending Eyre Peninsula
  Upgrade capex application, adding to the A$5B grid augmentation cost and timeline picture.

  [MONITORING] AEMC Final Rule "Package 2" — 30 MW Large-Load Threshold and Combined Impact
  Assessment Now in Force (12 November 2026) [HIGH RELEVANCE]: The AEMC published its final rule
  "Improving the NEM Access Standards – Package 2" on 12 November 2026, establishing a formal
  30 MW threshold that classifies data centres and other large inverter-based loads (IBLs) as a
  distinct connection category subject to mandatory technical standards. Key obligations: (1) network
  businesses must assess the COMBINED impact of multiple nearby large-load connections; (2) AEMO
  must develop and maintain modelling guidelines for IBLs; (3) existing applicants already advanced
  receive a 6-month grace period. South Energy's pre-secured Bundey 330 kV firm transmission
  positions satisfy both the technical (Package 2) and commercial (ECMC) requirements simultaneously.

  — 5 Days to Poll (23 November 2026) [HIGH RELEVANCE]: With five days to the 28 November
  Victorian state election, the competing policy platforms are now fully articulated. Labor (incumbent,
  Premier Ben Carroll) confirms it will maintain the BYO-renewables mandate — new data centres must

  (opposition, Jess Wilson) will allow gas to power data centres and has explicitly declined to match

  approvals queue holding A$5B+ in frozen DAs, the election outcome on 28 November will
  materially determine whether Melbourne re-enters direct competition with SA's Bundey corridor for
  tech company buyers. South Energy's pre-contracted Bundey corridor satisfies any BYO-renewables
  obligation and is structurally insulated from the outcome of the Victorian vote.
