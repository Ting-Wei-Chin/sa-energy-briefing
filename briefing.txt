📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 21 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• The Australian Energy Market Commission (AEMC) has proposed dedicated new grid connection
  standards specifically for data centres — the first time the NEM's technical connection
  framework has been tailored to this load class; on 5 August 2026, Minister Bowen lodged
  two formal NER rule change requests with the AEMC to reform how data centre grid costs
  are managed, with NER amendments scheduled for the AEMC's September 2026 meeting; the
  incoming standards will define what data centre developers must demonstrate to secure a
  connection, making it essential for South Energy to position its Bundey and Mid North
  sites as already compliant with or ahead of the new requirements. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: AEMC | https://www.aemc.gov.au/news-centre/media-releases/aemc-proposes-new-grid-standards-data-centre-connections
  Source: King & Wood Mallesons (5 August 2026) | https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/big-day-for-data-centres.html

• AEMO's June 2026 data discloses that 5.4 gigawatts of data centre capacity is currently
  progressing through the NEM's transmission connection queue nationally — making the queue
  itself a systemic bottleneck for operators in NSW and Victoria; South Energy's SA sites,
  anchored to the Bundey 330kV substation created by EnergyConnect, sit outside this
  national queue congestion and represent pre-cleared, ready-to-commit connection capacity
  in a market where queue position is increasingly a deal-deciding factor for buyers.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Certified Strategic / Australia Data Centre Index (June 2026) | https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/aemo-5-4gw-australian-data-centre-pipeline-june-2026


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• The AEMC's 5 August 2026 package includes formal regulatory advice establishing a
  pathway to require data centres to fully offset their electricity demand by investing in
  new renewable generation and demonstrating firmed capacity — a mandatory obligation
  materially stronger than the advisory "National Expectations" framework of March 2026;
  when enacted through NER changes in September, this rule will make data centres active
  underwriters of new renewable supply rather than passive offtakers, directly increasing
  the value of SA sites that can co-locate generation and storage and making South Energy's
  integrated site-plus-generation model a direct legal compliance solution for buyers.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: King & Wood Mallesons (5 August 2026) | https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/big-day-for-data-centres.html
  Source: Pinsent Masons | https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/australia-new-data-centre-rules-mean-planning-environment

• Australia's national battery storage fleet has tripled its daily load-shifting capacity,
  with 4.4GW of new battery storage coming online since Q1 2026 per AEMO — a system-level
  transformation in grid firming capability that reinforces South Australia's per-capita
  battery leadership and makes grid-firmed renewable supply increasingly reliable and
  cost-competitive for the always-on AI compute loads South Energy's sites are designed
  to serve.
  Source: Energy Storage News / AEMO (2026) | https://www.energy-storage.news/australias-battery-storage-fleet-triples-daily-load-shifting-as-4-4gw-comes-online-in-q1-2026-says-aemo/


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• Commonwealth Bank of Australia has published an August 2026 analysis sizing Australia's
  data centre investment opportunity at $150 billion — the most authoritative domestic
  market sizing to date and the first from a major financial institution rather than an
  industry body; the CommBank report specifically names South Australia's cooler climate,
  high renewable penetration, and lower land and network costs as structural advantages
  driving capital toward SA as tight supply in Sydney and Melbourne redirects buyer
  attention; South Energy should use this CBA analysis as an independent third-party
  reference in buyer pitch decks and investment committee materials. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: CommBank Newsroom (August 2026) | https://www.commbank.com.au/articles/newsroom/2026/08/australias-data-centre-boom.html

• Global hyperscaler AI capital expenditure is tracking at approximately $725 billion in
  2026 — Amazon ($200B), Alphabet/Google ($185B), Meta ($125B), Microsoft ($120B) —
  a 77% year-on-year increase from 2025; AWS and Microsoft alone have committed
  approximately A$45 billion to Australian digital infrastructure, confirming buyer
  financial capacity for large-scale site acquisitions; this spending urgency favours
  ready-to-develop SA sites over greenfield land search processes in congested markets.
  Source: Data Center Knowledge / Futurum (2026) | https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/hyperscalers/hyperscalers-in-2026-what-s-next-for-the-world-s-largest-data-center-operators-


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• South Australia has unveiled plans for dedicated data centre legislation — making it the
  first Australian state to pursue a sector-specific regulatory framework for AI data
  centres; dedicated legislation would streamline planning approvals, clarify grid
  connection obligations, and provide developers and buyers with sovereign commitment to
  the sector; for South Energy, this is the most significant SA-specific policy
  development since the Hydrogen and Renewable Energy Act and could materially reduce
  project holding-period risk by providing planning and approvals certainty on a statutory
  timeline. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: W.Media | https://w.media/south-australia-proposes-dedicated-data-centre-legislation/

• The AEMC's 5 August 2026 package is the formal implementation machinery converting
  PM Albanese's July 15 voluntary framework into binding law: Minister Bowen lodged
  two NER rule change requests — one on mandatory renewable offset obligations, one on
  transmission connection cost recovery — with the AEMC agreeing to progress both and
  scheduling NER consideration for September 2026; separately, Frontier Economics
  published a detailed cost recovery primer through the Australian Data Centres
  Association (12 June 2026) that is directly informing AEMC rule-making on how
  infrastructure costs are allocated to operators rather than consumers; the voluntary
  National Expectations of March 2026 are being replaced by binding rules on an
  approximately 60–90 day regulatory timeline.
  Source: King & Wood Mallesons | https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/big-day-for-data-centres.html
  Source: Frontier Economics / ADCA (12 June 2026) | https://datacentres.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-06_Frontier-Electricity-Cost-Recovery-from-Data-Centres.pdf


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• ABC News (19 June 2026) reported that a specific proposed data centre in South Australia
  is dividing local communities — residents support the jobs but raise concerns over water
  demand; this is the first documented public-facing community opposition to a named SA
  data centre site and signals that water use and local amenity will be recurring themes
  in SA planning approval processes; South Energy's site development and buyer briefings
  should proactively address water management strategies (closed-loop cooling, rainwater
  harvesting, no draw on municipal supply) as a planning risk-mitigation and social
  licence differentiator going into any development application. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ABC News (19 June 2026) | https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-06-19/proposed-data-centre-in-south-australia-dividing-locals/106810776

• RenewMap's SA data centre pipeline tracker (August 2026) shows South Australia currently
  has 7 projects in development and 12 operating, with the largest operational facility
  at only 10MW (ADL02) — the Bundey 800MW campus (IREN) represents an 80x step-change
  from any existing operational SA site; this data underscores the scarcity of SA-specific
  execution precedent and positions South Energy's project preparation expertise as a
  meaningful buyer-facing differentiator in a market where operators have limited
  comparable references for large-scale SA delivery.
  Source: RenewMap (August 2026) | https://renewmap.com.au/resources/projects/sa/datacentre/

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年8月21日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• 澳大利亞能源市場委員會（AEMC）已就數據中心電網接入提出專項新標準——這是NEM技術接入
  框架首次針對此類用電主體進行專門設計；2026年8月5日，能源部長鮑文（Chris Bowen）就
  數據中心電網成本管理問題正式向AEMC提交兩份《國家電力規則》（NER）修改申請，NER修訂
  將於AEMC 2026年9月會議上審議；新標準將明確規定數據中心開發商在取得接入資格前須達到
  的技術要求，South Energy應確保其班迪（Bundey）及中北部地塊已符合或領先於上述新要求。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：AEMC | https://www.aemc.gov.au/news-centre/media-releases/aemc-proposes-new-grid-standards-data-centre-connections
  資料來源：King & Wood Mallesons（2026年8月5日）| https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/big-day-for-data-centres.html

• AEMO 2026年6月數據揭示，全國目前有54億瓦（5.4GW）的數據中心容量正在推進NEM輸電接入
  排隊流程——這使得接入隊列本身成為新南威爾士州及維多利亞州運營商的系統性瓶頸；South
  Energy在南澳州的項目地塊依托EnergyConnect所建班迪330千伏變電站，完全處於這一全國接
  入隊列擁堵的體系之外，代表著在「排隊位置日益成為交易決定性因素」的市場環境下，具備
  預先清關、可立即履約的接入容量。【高度相關】
  資料來源：Certified Strategic / 澳大利亞數據中心指數（2026年6月）| https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/aemo-5-4gw-australian-data-centre-pipeline-june-2026


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• AEMC 2026年8月5日一攬子方案中，包含強制性監管建議，要求數據中心通過投資新增可再生
  能源發電並提供有保障的容量，以全額抵銷其用電需求——這一強制性義務在力度上遠超2026年
  3月發布的僅具諮詢性質的「國家期望框架」；一旦通過9月NER修訂正式生效，這一規則將把
  數據中心從被動的可再生能源購電方轉變為新增可再生能源供應的主動承購方，直接提升能夠
  整合發電和儲能的南澳州地塊的價值，使South Energy的「地塊＋發電」一體化模式成為買家
  實現法規合規的直接解決方案。【高度相關】
  資料來源：King & Wood Mallesons（2026年8月5日）| https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/big-day-for-data-centres.html
  資料來源：Pinsent Masons | https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/australia-new-data-centre-rules-mean-planning-environment

• 澳大利亞全國電池儲能裝機規模實現大幅躍升：自2026年第一季度以來，新增4.4GW電池儲能
  投入運行，日負荷轉移能力提升至原來的三倍（AEMO數據）——這一系統性轉變進一步鞏固了
  南澳州在人均電池儲能方面的全國領先地位，並使有保障的可再生能源供電對於South Energy
  地塊所服務的全天候AI算力負載而言愈加可靠、具備成本競爭力。
  資料來源：Energy Storage News / AEMO（2026年）| https://www.energy-storage.news/australias-battery-storage-fleet-triples-daily-load-shifting-as-4-4gw-comes-online-in-q1-2026-says-aemo/


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• 澳大利亞聯邦銀行（CommBank）於2026年8月發布分析報告，將澳大利亞數據中心投資機遇定
  規模為1,500億澳元——這是迄今最具權威性的本土市場規模測算，也是首份來自主要金融機構
  而非行業遊說組織的數據；CommBank報告明確點名南澳州氣候較涼（降低製冷能耗）、可再生
  能源比例高及土地和電網成本較低等結構性優勢，認為這些因素正在將資本從悉尼、墨爾本等
  供應緊張市場引向南澳州；South Energy應在買家路演材料及投資委員會文件中引用這一CBA
  分析，作為具公信力的獨立第三方佐證。【高度相關】
  資料來源：CommBank新聞室（2026年8月）| https://www.commbank.com.au/articles/newsroom/2026/08/australias-data-centre-boom.html

• 全球超大規模雲服務商AI資本開支在2026年預計達約7,250億美元——亞馬遜（2,000億）、
  Alphabet/谷歌（1,850億）、Meta（1,250億）、微軟（1,200億）——較2025年同比增長77%；
  AWS和微軟兩家已合計向澳大利亞數字基礎設施承諾約450億澳元投入，印證了買家對大規模地
  塊收購的財務能力；這種資本投放的緊迫性，使已具備開發條件的南澳州地塊相比在擁擠市場
  中尋找未開發土地更具吸引力。
  資料來源：Data Center Knowledge / Futurum（2026年）| https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/hyperscalers/hyperscalers-in-2026-what-s-next-for-the-world-s-largest-data-center-operators-


📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 南澳州已宣布擬制定專門的數據中心立法——使其成為澳大利亞首個就AI數據中心尋求制定行業
  專項監管框架的州份；專項立法將簡化規劃審批流程、明確電網接入義務，並向開發商和買家
  傳遞政府對該行業的主權承諾；對South Energy而言，這是繼《氫能與可再生能源法》以來南
  澳州層面最重大的政策進展，有望通過法定時間表提供規劃和審批確定性，實質降低項目持有
  期風險。【高度相關】
  資料來源：W.Media | https://w.media/south-australia-proposes-dedicated-data-centre-legislation/

• AEMC 2026年8月5日一攬子方案是將阿爾巴尼斯總理7月15日自願性框架轉化為具有約束力法規
  的正式執行機制：鮑文部長提交兩份NER修改申請——一份針對強制性可再生能源抵銷義務，一
  份針對輸電接入成本回收——AEMC已同意推進兩項修改並排定2026年9月審議；Frontier
  Economics另於2026年6月12日通過澳大利亞數據中心協會（ADCA）發布成本回收詳細報告，
  直接為AEMC規則制定提供參考；2026年3月發布的自願性「國家期望」框架正在60至90天的監
  管時間表內被具有約束力的規則所取代。
  資料來源：King & Wood Mallesons | https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/big-day-for-data-centres.html
  資料來源：Frontier Economics / ADCA（2026年6月12日）| https://datacentres.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-06_Frontier-Electricity-Cost-Recovery-from-Data-Centres.pdf


🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

• 據ABC新聞（2026年6月19日）報道，南澳州一個具體的擬建數據中心項目在當地引發意見分歧
  ——居民支持其帶來的就業機會，但對水資源需求表示擔憂；這是首次有文件記錄的、針對具體
  南澳州數據中心項目的社區公開異議，表明用水量和對當地的影響將成為南澳州規劃審批流程
  中的持續性議題；South Energy的地塊開發方案和買家簡報材料應主動提出水資源管理策略
  （密閉循環冷卻、雨水收集、不佔用市政供水）作為規劃風險緩釋和社會許可的差異化競爭
  優勢，融入所有開發申請文件。【高度相關】
  資料來源：ABC新聞（2026年6月19日）| https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-06-19/proposed-data-centre-in-south-australia-dividing-locals/106810776

• RenewMap南澳州數據中心管線追蹤系統（2026年8月）顯示，南澳州目前有7個項目在建設開
  發中，12個已投入運營，最大運營設施僅為10MW（ADL02）——IREN班迪800兆瓦園區代表著相
  比現有任何南澳州運營設施約80倍的規模躍升；這一數據凸顯了南澳州現有大規模落地先例的
  稀缺性，使South Energy的項目準備和推進能力成為買家洽談中具有實質意義的差異化優勢，
  尤其在買家面臨大規模南澳州交付參考案例有限的情況下。
  資料來源：RenewMap（2026年8月）| https://renewmap.com.au/resources/projects/sa/datacentre/
