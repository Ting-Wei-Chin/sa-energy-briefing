🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES
──────────────────────────────────────

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] IREN secured a transmission connection agreement with ElectraNet for four 330kV feeder exits at Bundey substation, supporting up to 800MW of AI data centre capacity with no network upgrade required — the single largest grid-connected load commitment ever announced in South Australia.
  Source: GlobeNewswire (3 Jun 2026) – https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305925/0/en/IREN-Announces-First-Australian-Data-Center-Campus-800MW-in-South-Australia.html

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] ElectraNet's 2026 Transmission Annual Planning Report (TAPR) discloses over 75 prospective connection projects from 41 proponents across Greater Adelaide, the Mid North and South East, driven by data centres, defence, mining and hydrogen — and forecasts SA peak demand doubling from 3.3 GW to 6.5 GW by 2040.
  Source: PV Magazine Australia (31 Mar 2026) – https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/
  Also: ESD News – https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] ElectraNet's TAPR identifies three priority transmission expansion projects: Northern Transmission (Greater Adelaide/Mid North), Eyre Peninsula Upgrade (Cultana–Yadnarie lines), and South East Expansion — all targeting regions with strong data centre and renewable energy developer interest.
  Source: ElectraNet New Customer Connection Guide (30 Mar 2026) – https://electranet.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/New-Customer-Connection-Guide-V1.pdf

• Project EnergyConnect, the 900km SA–NSW interconnector, is targeting full 800MW transfer capability by mid-2026 following Stage 2 construction completion in New South Wales by Transgrid — materially expanding South Australia's exportable renewable energy capacity.
  Source: Utility Magazine – https://utilitymagazine.com.au/project-energyconnect-successfully-delivering-electranets-largest-transmission-project/

• AEMO's June 2026 data centre pipeline disclosure shows 5.4 GW of prospective national load in the connection queue, with South Australia accounting for a significant and growing share as developers favour the state's 100% net renewable grid profile.
  Source: Australia Data Centre Index (Jun 2026) – https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/aemo-5-4gw-australian-data-centre-pipeline-june-2026
  Also: Kalkine (2026) – https://kalkine.com.au/news/general-news/energyconnect-nears-the-finish-line-as-data-centres-push-australias-grid-to-its-limits

• AMPYR Australia has received AEMO's 5.3.4 technical letter for its 270MW grid-forming battery near Port Augusta (Northern Battery), targeting financial close and construction commencement in Q4 2026 — adding critical system strength to the SA network ahead of major new industrial loads.
  Source: Energy Storage News – https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)
───────────────────────────────────────────

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] On 15 July 2026, the Australian Government announced its intention to legislate mandatory AI Standards for large-scale data centres, moving beyond the voluntary Data Centre Expectations published on 23 March 2026 — National Cabinet is expected to consider the legislative approach in August 2026, with legislation expected in early 2027.
  Source: Gilbert + Tobin Law (2026) – https://www.gtlaw.com.au/insights/australian-government-announces-mandatory-ai-standards-for-large-scale-data-centres-and-new-office-of-ai
  Also: White & Case – https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/australian-ai-update-pms-ai-and-data-centre-speech-key-takeaways-data-centre
  Also: Norton Rose Fulbright – https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/746b2689/new-expectations-for-data-centre-operators-and-ai-infrastructure-developers

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] A cornerstone of the federal framework is the "net generator" requirement: large AI data centre operators will be legally required to put at least as much energy into the national grid as they take out — this fundamentally reshapes the business model for data centre land acquisition and positions renewable-adjacent sites (like those in SA's Gawler Ranges and Spencer Gulf regions) as premium assets.
  Source: Pinsent Masons – https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/australia-data-centres-net-generators-proposed-reforms
  Also: Clayton Utz (Mar 2026) – https://www.claytonutz.com/insights/2026/march/great-expectations-what-the-australian-government-requires-of-data-centres-and-ai-infrastructure

• The federal government established a centralised Office of AI within the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet to fast-track data centre project approvals — a positive signal that nationally significant data centre projects will face reduced regulatory friction at the federal level.
  Source: Grafa – https://grafa.com/en/news/australia/why-an-aussie-centralised-policy-fast-tracking-triggers-a-local-data-hub-pivot
  Also: Holding Redlich – https://www.holdingredlich.com/new-national-interest-framework-for-data-centres-and-ai-infrastructure-in-australia

• South Australia's Technical Regulator issued Guidelines (February 2026) requiring all data centres of 100 MW or more to obtain certification covering power system reliability, security and stability, including mandatory backup energy systems and compliance obligations during system stress events — project developers must factor certification lead time into planning timelines.
  Source: Ashurst (Apr 2026) – https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-april-2026-update/

• South Australia's Minimum MW Offer (MMO) mechanism was implemented from July 2026, with the industry-led contract co-design process formalising outcomes into National Electricity Law — ESEM (Electricity Spot Exchange Model) detailed policy design is underway, with the first tender targeted for end of 2027.
  Source: AER (T-3 Reliability) – https://www.aer.gov.au/industry/retail/reliability-obligation/south-australia-december-2026-february-2027/t-3
  Also: Ashurst (Feb 2026) – https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/energy-alert-australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-february-2026-update/

• Malleson's published guidance on new renewable energy, grid connection, and cost recovery rules specifically addressing data centres, indicating that the legal framework for data centre grid integration is actively evolving in Australia.
  Source: Mallesons (2026) – https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/big-day-for-data-centres.html

  Source: SA Government Planning – https://dit.sa.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0008/1580066/South-Australian-Planning-and-Design-Code-Version-2026-1-15-January-2026.pdf

  Source: GlobeNewswire (3 Jun 2026) – https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305925/0/en/IREN-Announces-First-Australian-Data-Center-Campus-800MW-in-South-Australia.html

🔌 1. 電網接入機會
──────────────────

• 【高度相關】IREN公司與ElectraNet簽署輸電連接協議，獲得Bundey變電站四條330kV出線走廊，最高支持800MW人工智能數據中心負載，且無需任何電網升級——這是南澳州有史以來規模最大的單一電網負載承諾，直接驗證南澳州電網接入可行性。

  資料來源：PV Magazine Australia（2026年3月31日）– https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/
  另見：ESD News – https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/

• 【高度相關】ElectraNet TAPR確定三個優先輸電擴展項目：北部輸電項目（大阿德萊德及中北部地區）、埃爾半島升級項目（Cultana–Yadnarie線路）以及東南擴展項目——三個走廊均與數據中心及可再生能源開發商高度感興趣的地區重疊，是南能公司（South Energy）選址評估的關鍵參考依據。
  資料來源：ElectraNet新客戶連接指南（2026年3月30日）– https://electranet.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/New-Customer-Connection-Guide-V1.pdf

• 連接南澳州與新南威爾士州的900公里跨州輸電互聯項目EnergyConnect，預計在Transgrid完成新州段第二期施工後於2026年中旬實現完整800MW輸電能力——將大幅提升南澳州可出口可再生能源容量，強化其作為數據中心選址的商業吸引力。
  資料來源：Utility Magazine – https://utilitymagazine.com.au/project-energyconnect-successfully-delivering-electranets-largest-transmission-project/

• AEMO於2026年6月披露，全澳數據中心電網接入申請管道達5.4GW；南澳州憑借100%淨可再生能源電網特性，在申請項目中佔比持續上升，成為全國增長最快的數據中心接入市場之一。
  資料來源：Australia Data Centre Index（2026年6月）– https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/aemo-5-4gw-australian-data-centre-pipeline-june-2026

  資料來源：Energy Storage News – https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/
