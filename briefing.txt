📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 22 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• ElectraNet's 2026 Transmission Annual Planning Report (TAPR) proposes a $3.5 billion
  Northern Transmission Project (NTx) — a new high-voltage corridor in two sections: NTx
  South running from the Bundey substation to Greater Adelaide, and NTx North running from
  Bundey to Whyalla/Cultana East — which would create a 2,000MW+ north-south electricity
  highway and provide a geographically distinct, bushfire-resilient pathway into Adelaide;
  AEMO published a Project Assessment Draft Report (PADR) in April 2026 confirming the
  project has entered formal assessment; for South Energy, this upgrade would directly
  expand transfer capacity from Bundey-connected sites into the primary Adelaide consumption
  centre, materially increasing the commercial scale a buyer could absorb from a
  Bundey-anchored project. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ElectraNet 2026 TAPR | https://electranet.com.au/resource/2026-tapr-transmission-annual-planning-report/
  Source: AEMO NTx PADR (April 2026) | https://www.aemo.com.au/-/media/files/stakeholder_consultation/consultations/nsp_consultations/2026/ntx-padr.pdf
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/
  Source: NTx Project | https://ntxproject.com.au/

• AMPYR Australia received AEMO's 5.3.4 technical compliance letter on 5 August 2026 for
  its 270MW / 1,080MWh Northern Battery grid-forming BESS near Port Augusta — the final
  major regulatory approval before financial close and construction commencement, which
  AMPYR is targeting for Q4 2026; the project will be built on the site of the former
  Northern Power Station coal plant using existing substation infrastructure, and is
  designed to deliver up to eight hours of storage to reinforce South Australian grid
  strength and system reliability north of Adelaide.
  Source: Energy Storage News (August 2026) | https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/
  Source: IndexBox | https://www.indexbox.io/blog/ampyrs-270mw-northern-battery-secures-key-aemo-grid-connection-approval/


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• South Australia's electricity demand is forecast to double to 50TWh by 2040 — driven by
  data centres, green steel, and hydrogen production — according to analysis based on
  ElectraNet's TAPR demand forecasting published by PV Tech and Energy Magazine; this
  trajectory directly validates the investment case for additional transmission and
  generation capacity across SA's Mid North and Spencer Gulf corridors, which correspond
  precisely to South Energy's land portfolio locations, and is likely to trigger accelerated
  approvals for new generation and storage connecting into those zones. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: PV Tech | https://www.pv-tech.org/green-steel-data-centres-and-hydrogen-push-south-australias-electricity-demand-toward-50twh-by-2040/
  Source: Energy Magazine | https://www.energymagazine.com.au/sa-electricity-demand-is-set-to-double-but-is-the-grid-ready

• AEMO's finalised 2026 Integrated System Plan (ISP) confirms that Australia's least-cost
  energy pathway is renewable energy supported by storage, connected by new transmission
  and backed up by flexible gas — structurally aligning with South Australia's grid
  architecture and reinforcing the state's position as the lowest-risk location for buyers
  seeking NEM-compliant, firmed renewable supply for always-on AI compute workloads.
  Source: ESD News / AEMO 2026 ISP | https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• South Australian thermal energy storage company 1414 Degrees announced on 3 August 2026
  that its subsidiary SiliconAurora has signed a Heads of Agreement to develop up to 1GW
  of AI data centre infrastructure at its Aurora Energy Precinct in South Australia,
  anchored by firmed renewable generation and storage assets; this is the second large-scale
  SA data centre commitment confirmed in 2026 alongside the 800MW IREN/Bundey campus, and
  signals that SA-based integrated energy-plus-compute precincts are actively attracting AI
  data centre partnerships — validating South Energy's model with prospective buyers who
  are evaluating SA delivery capability and precedent. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News (3 August 2026) | https://esdnews.com.au/1414-degrees-inks-major-data-centre-deal-in-south-australia/

• No new hyperscaler announcements specific to Australia were identified beyond yesterday's
  coverage of $725 billion in global AI capex and ~A$45 billion in confirmed Australian
  digital infrastructure commitments from AWS and Microsoft.


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• The Australian Government's mandatory AI standards framework — announced by PM Albanese
  on 15 July 2026 — is scheduled for National Cabinet consideration in August 2026, meaning
  a decision on the mandatory AI data centre legislative framework could come this week;
  once National Cabinet endorses the approach, formal legislation is expected in early 2027,
  converting the existing voluntary National Expectations into legal obligations with
  compliance timelines that will sharpen buyer urgency for sites already structurally
  compliant with grid and renewable requirements. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: GT Law (July 2026) | https://www.gtlaw.com.au/insights/australian-government-announces-mandatory-ai-standards-for-large-scale-data-centres-and-new-office-of-ai
  Source: White & Case | https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/australian-ai-update-pms-ai-and-data-centre-speech-key-takeaways-data-centre

• South Australia is implementing new Market Making Obligations (MMO) for its electricity
  market from July 2026 as part of a broader NEM regulatory reform, directly shaping how
  large loads such as data centres interact with the SA spot market and affect the
  electricity cost exposure that buyers will model when evaluating SA site acquisitions.
  Source: Ashurst (April 2026 Update) | https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-april-2026-update/


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• The SA Government has confirmed that a planned $5 billion water pipeline — primarily
  designed to supply BHP's Far North SA mining expansion — could also serve new data centres
  in South Australia; this government-funded water infrastructure directly resolves the
  principal community and planning-approval risk identified in recent SA data centre
  opposition (water demand), and strengthens the site development case for the Mid North and
  Far North SA corridors where South Energy's portfolio is concentrated; South Energy should
  reference this infrastructure commitment in development applications and buyer materials
  as a government-backed water supply solution. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ABC News (23 June 2026) | https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-06-23/sa-push-for-ai-data-centres-in-sa-in-new-strategy/106830536

• With two projects now anchored at 800MW (IREN/Bundey) and 1GW (SiliconAurora/Aurora
  Precinct), South Australia's pipeline above 100MW stands at just these two commitments
  — confirming a structural mid-market gap (100–500MW projects) in which South Energy's
  development-ready sites face no direct SA competition and are positioned as the primary
  option for buyers seeking sub-gigawatt, near-term deliverable capacity in the state.
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/1414-degrees-inks-major-data-centre-deal-in-south-australia/
  Source: RenewMap (August 2026) | https://renewmap.com.au/resources/projects/sa/datacentre/

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年8月22日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• ElectraNet 2026年《輸電年度規劃報告》（TAPR）提出總估算造價35億澳元的「北部輸電項目」
  （NTx）——計劃分兩期建設全新高壓輸電廊道：NTx南段由班迪（Bundey）變電站延伸至大阿德萊德
  地區，NTx北段由班迪延伸至懷阿拉（Whyalla）/卡爾塔納東（Cultana East）——建成後將形成一條
  逾2,000兆瓦的南北「電力高速公路」，並為阿德萊德提供具備地理差異性、可防範叢林火災風險的輸
  電替代通道；AEMO已於2026年4月發布NTx《項目評估草案報告》（PADR），確認項目已正式進入評估
  程序；對South Energy而言，這一輸電升級將直接擴大班迪接入地塊向主要消費中心阿德萊德輸送電力
  的容量，大幅提升買家可從班迪錨定項目中消納的商業規模。【高度相關】
  資料來源：ElectraNet 2026 TAPR | https://electranet.com.au/resource/2026-tapr-transmission-annual-planning-report/
  資料來源：AEMO NTx PADR（2026年4月）| https://www.aemo.com.au/-/media/files/stakeholder_consultation/consultations/nsp_consultations/2026/ntx-padr.pdf
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/

• AMPYR澳大利亞於2026年8月5日獲得AEMO頒發5.3.4技術達標函，對象為其位於波特奧古斯塔
  （Port Augusta）附近的270兆瓦/1,080兆瓦時「北部電池」電網型儲能系統——這是金融交割及
  開工前最後一項重大監管批准，AMPYR目標在2026年第四季度完成；項目將建設在舊北部燃煤電廠
  舊址，利用現有變電站基礎設施，設計提供最長八小時儲能，以強化阿德萊德以北南澳州電網的
  系統強度與可靠性。
  資料來源：Energy Storage News（2026年8月）| https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/
  資料來源：IndexBox | https://www.indexbox.io/blog/ampyrs-270mw-northern-battery-secures-key-aemo-grid-connection-approval/


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• 根據PV Tech及《能源雜誌》援引ElectraNet TAPR需求預測發布的分析，南澳州電力需求預計將翻
  倍，至2040年達到50太瓦時（TWh），驅動力為數據中心、綠色鋼鐵及制氫生產；這一需求軌跡從
  根本上驗證了在南澳州中北部及斯賓塞灣走廊追加輸電和發電容量的投資邏輯——而這正是South
  Energy土地組合的所在區域，並可能促使相關地帶的新發電和儲能接入申請獲得加快批准。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：PV Tech | https://www.pv-tech.org/green-steel-data-centres-and-hydrogen-push-south-australias-electricity-demand-toward-50twh-by-2040/
  資料來源：Energy Magazine | https://www.energymagazine.com.au/sa-electricity-demand-is-set-to-double-but-is-the-grid-ready

• AEMO最終版2026年《綜合系統規劃》（ISP）確認，澳大利亞成本最低的能源發展路徑為：以可再生
  能源為核心、以電池儲能為支撐、以新增輸電為聯結、以靈活天然氣為備用——與南澳州電網架構高
  度契合，進一步印證南澳州是尋求NEM合規、有保障可再生能源供電的AI算力買家的最低風險選址。
  資料來源：ESD News / AEMO 2026 ISP | https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• 南澳州熱能儲能企業1414 Degrees於2026年8月3日宣布，其子公司SiliconAurora已就在南澳州
  Aurora能源產業園開發最多1吉瓦（GW）AI數據中心基礎設施簽署意向協議（HoA），以有保障的
  可再生發電和儲能資產為支撐；這是繼IREN班迪800兆瓦園區後，2026年確認的第二個南澳州規模
  化數據中心承諾，印證以南澳州為基地的能源與算力一體化產業園正在主動吸引AI數據中心合作，
  進一步驗證了South Energy項目模式對評估南澳州交付能力的潛在買家的吸引力。【高度相關】
  資料來源：ESD News（2026年8月3日）| https://esdnews.com.au/1414-degrees-inks-major-data-centre-deal-in-south-australia/

• 過去48小時內，未發現澳大利亞超大規模雲服務商的新公告，超出昨日簡報範圍（7,250億美元全
  球AI資本開支及AWS、微軟約450億澳元澳大利亞數字基礎設施承諾）。


📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 澳大利亞聯邦政府由阿爾巴尼斯總理於2026年7月15日宣布的強制性AI標準框架——涵蓋大型數據
  中心及新設「AI辦公室」——將於2026年8月提交全國內閣審議，意味著全國內閣可能在本周內就
  強制性AI數據中心立法框架作出決定；全國內閣一旦批准，正式立法預計於2027年初落地，將現
  行自願性「國家期望」轉化為具合規時限的法律義務，將加劇買家對已具備電網及可再生能源結
  構合規條件地塊的迫切需求。【高度相關】
  資料來源：GT Law（2026年7月）| https://www.gtlaw.com.au/insights/australian-government-announces-mandatory-ai-standards-for-large-scale-data-centres-and-new-office-of-ai
  資料來源：White & Case | https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/australian-ai-update-pms-ai-and-data-centre-speech-key-takeaways-data-centre

• 南澳州自2026年7月起推行新的電力市場「造市義務」（MMO），作為更廣泛NEM監管改革的組成部
  分，直接影響數據中心等大型用電戶參與南澳州現貨市場的方式，對買家評估南澳州地塊的電費
  成本風險敞口和管理策略具有直接參考意義。
  資料來源：Ashurst（2026年4月市場更新）| https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-april-2026-update/


🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

• 南澳州政府已確認，一條主要為服務必和必拓（BHP）遠北礦業擴張而規劃的50億澳元輸水管道
  工程，亦可用於向南澳州新建數據中心供水；這一政府出資的水利基礎設施，直接化解了近期社
  區反對意見和規劃審批中最核心的風險——用水需求問題——並進一步強化在South Energy土地組
  合集中所在的中北部及遠北南澳州走廊布局數據中心的可行性；South Energy在開發申請及買家
  材料中應將此基礎設施承諾列為政府背書的供水解決方案加以引用。【高度相關】
  資料來源：ABC News（2026年6月23日）| https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-06-23/sa-push-for-ai-data-centres-in-sa-in-new-strategy/106830536

• 隨著IREN班迪（800兆瓦）和SiliconAurora/Aurora產業園（1吉瓦）兩個項目相繼確立，南澳州
  百兆瓦以上規模的開發管線目前僅有這兩個超大規模承諾——印證了市場在中型規模（100至500兆
  瓦）開發就緒型項目上存在結構性缺口；South Energy的地塊在此細分市場在南澳州尚無直接競
  爭對手，是尋求短期內可交付的次吉瓦規模容量的買家的首選選項。
