🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• AEMO Issues Call for Non-Network Options on Three Newly Actionable 2026 ISP
  Projects — Submissions Close 17 September 2026 [HIGH RELEVANCE]: AEMO has
  issued a call for non-network options to address three newly actionable
  projects identified in the final 2026 Integrated System Plan (published
  25 June 2026), with submissions due by Thursday, 17 September 2026; project
  proponents and site owners with assets that could help address identified grid
  needs without new transmission infrastructure should evaluate whether their
  project qualifies; for South Energy, sites with secured transmission
  connections and integrated renewable firming may represent viable
  non-network responses to AEMO's stated grid needs, creating an opportunity
  to position projects ahead of the formal connection queue before next week's
  deadline.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: AEMO 2026 ISP |
  https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/major-publications/integrated-system-plan-isp/2026-integrated-system-plan-isp

  [MONITORING] ElectraNet's 2026 TAPR forecasts increased connection demand
  from Greater Adelaide, the Mid North, and South East SA driven by data
  centres, AI growth, and defence industry expansion; three new transmission
  projects are proposed, including the $3.5B Northern Transmission Project
  spanning northern and eastern SA, and the Eyre Peninsula Upgrade for the
  Upper Spencer Gulf region; sites near proposed Northern Transmission Project
  routes (Bundey area) gain long-term transmission capacity upside beyond the
  current four 330 kV feeder exits already securing IREN's campus.
  Source: ESD News |
  https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 meeting on NER changes mandating data
  centre renewable and firming offsets — outcome not yet published as of
  9 September. The rule changes, once adopted, will define binding obligations
  for all new connection applicants before the 2027 legislative framework takes
  full effect. Watch for the official communiqué from energy.gov.au.


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] Goyder Battery Stage 1 (200 MW / 800 MWh) construction
  commenced at ElectraNet's Bundey substation in August 2026, co-located with
  IREN's 800 MW data centre campus transmission connection (four 330 kV feeder
  exits). The co-location of large-scale BESS firming infrastructure with the
  highest-capacity grid connection node in regional SA (Bundey) is a developing
  site-quality signal — projects able to demonstrate proximity to Bundey-class
  grid connection and co-located BESS firming will match the specific criteria
  hyperscalers have named (renewable power access, firming, land availability).


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

  No new updates today. Data Center World Australia 2026 (September 7–9,
  Melbourne) concluded on Day 3 today; no specific new announcements from the
  closing day have been confirmed at time of publication. The conference and its

  [MONITORING] US tech firms including Google, Apple, and Meta have shown

  window; Deloitte's August 2026 project tracker counts 33 Australian data
  centre projects worth a combined A$104 billion.


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• South Australia Releases Data Centre Strategy and Plans Dedicated Data
  Centre and AI Infrastructure Act — Crown Sponsorship Pathway and Coordinator
  General Designation Powers Now Active [HIGH RELEVANCE]: The South Australian
  government released a new dedicated data centre strategy on Tuesday
  (8 September 2026) and announced plans to introduce the Data Centre and AI
  Infrastructure Act; the Act will designate data centres as essential
  infrastructure, enabling a crown sponsorship scheme that bypasses standard
  local council assessment and replaces it with state-level oversight; the SA
  Coordinator General has already been granted the power to designate and
  accelerate individual data centre developments on a case-by-case basis; the
  Act will require all developers to "match demand growth with new, firmed
  supply" — the government's "new energy for new demand" standard — ensuring
  no adverse impact on consumer power bills; additional obligations include
  transparency on energy use, compliance with clear technical standards, grid
  stability support through demand flexibility, and fair allocation of network
  costs; for South Energy, these two provisions are material: (1) the crown
  sponsorship and Coordinator General designation powers compress planning
  approval timelines and eliminate council-level approval risk, directly
  reducing development risk on land acquisitions; (2) the "new energy for new
  demand" statutory obligation validates the additionality-compliant renewable
  and BESS firming structure already embedded in South Energy's project model,
  ensuring that projects built to this standard are fully pre-compliant with
  the Act on day one.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: CapitalBrief |
  https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/new-energy-for-new-demand-sa-plans-data-centre-and-ai-infrastructure-act-47dfa4af-f755-494c-981a-121fed28eb67/
  Source: Australia Data Centre Index (certifiedstrategic.com) |
  https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/south-australia-data-centre-strategy-new-energy-for-new-demand

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 NER decision (data centre renewable and
  firming mandatory offset requirement) — formal communiqué pending. Federal
  draft legislation targeting early 2027. Minister Bowen confirmed "no
  carve-outs" on 28 August 2026. National Cabinet (26 August 2026) endorsed
  developing nationally consistent mandatory standards covering energy, water,
  land-use, and skills/training. The SA Data Centre Strategy released
  yesterday signals SA intends to legislate ahead of, and in alignment with,
  the federal floor standard.


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] Key background context: Deloitte's project tracker
  (August 2026) counted 33 Australian data centre projects worth a combined
  A$104 billion. Of the approximately 9 GW of proposed data centre capacity
  seeking transmission connections on Australia's main grid, approximately 84%
  remained at the application stage as of June 2026, confirming a structural
  bottleneck that favours sites with completed transmission connection
  agreements. NSW and Victoria continue to absorb the majority of speculative
  project applications; projects able to demonstrate a secured SA Bundey-class
  transmission connection, renewable firming, and cleared planning due
  diligence occupy a demonstrably scarcer position in the national pipeline.
  NOTE: With SA now introducing a dedicated Data Centre and AI Infrastructure
  Act (see Category 4), the planning pathway for SA projects is further
  differentiated from NSW and VIC where no equivalent state-level fast-track
  mechanism exists.

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年9月9日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• AEMO就2026年綜合系統規劃三項新可行動項目發出非電網解決方案
  徵集——截止日期2026年9月17日【高度相關】：澳大利亞能源市場
  運營商（AEMO）就最終版2026年綜合系統規劃（2026年6月25日發布）
  中三項新可行動項目發出非電網解決方案徵集，截止日期為2026年9月
  17日（星期四）；持有能夠在不新建輸電基礎設施前提下滿足電網需求
  資產的項目推動方及場址業主，應評估其項目是否符合條件；對於
  South Energy而言，已獲得輸電連接協議並集成可再生能源調峰的
  場址，或可作為AEMO所述電網需求的可行非電網解決方案，
  在下週截止日期前搶先佔據正式連接排隊的有利位置。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：AEMO 2026年綜合系統規劃 |
  https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/major-publications/integrated-system-plan-isp/2026-integrated-system-plan-isp

  【持續監測】ElectraNet 2026年輸電充足性規劃報告（TAPR）預測，
  受數據中心、人工智能增長及國防產業擴張驅動，大阿德萊德、
  北部中部及南澳州東南部的電網接入需求將顯著上升；報告提議三項新
  輸電項目，包括橫跨南澳北部及東部的35億澳元北部輸電項目（NTx），
  以及針對上斯賓塞灣的埃爾半島升級工程；毗鄰北部輸電項目擬議線路
  的場址（如邦迪地區）在IREN園區現有四個330千伏饋線出口的基礎上
  進一步獲得長期輸電容量升值空間。
  資料來源：ESD News |
  https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/

  【持續監測】能源與氣候變化部長理事會（ECMC）2026年9月會議
  關於強制要求數據中心完成可再生能源及調峰補償的《國家電力規則》
  修訂案——截至9月9日尚未發布正式公報。相關規則修訂一旦通過，
  將界定所有新連接申請人在2027年立法框架全面生效前須承擔的
  強制義務。請關注energy.gov.au發布的官方公報。


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】Neoen高德爾電池儲能項目一期（200兆瓦/800兆瓦時）
  已於2026年8月在ElectraNet邦迪變電站啟動建設，與IREN 800兆瓦
  數據中心園區的輸電連接協議（四個330千伏饋線出口）同址共建。

  （邦迪）的同址集中，是一個正在形成的選址質量信號——能夠證明
  毗鄰邦迪級電網連接和同址電池儲能調峰的項目，將精確匹配超大規模
  雲端客戶明確列出的選址標準（可再生能源獲取、調峰能力、土地可用性）。

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 南澳州發布數據中心戰略並計劃推出專項《數據中心與人工智能
  基礎設施法》——皇家擔保規劃通道及總督察員指定授權正式啟動
  【高度相關】：南澳州政府於本週二（2026年9月8日）發布新版
  專項數據中心戰略，並宣布計劃引入《數據中心與人工智能基礎設施法》；
  該法案將把數據中心列為基本基礎設施，啟用「皇家擔保方案」，
  使數據中心開發可繞過標準地方議會審批程序，改由州政府層面
  直接監管；南澳州總督察員已獲授權，可針對個別數據中心開發項目
  逐案指定並加速審批；法案將要求所有開發商「以新的、有調峰支持的
  供應匹配需求增長」——即政府提出的「新需求配新能源」標準——
  確保不對消費者電費產生負面影響；其他義務包括：能源使用透明度、
  符合明確技術標準、通過需求靈活性支持電網穩定，以及公平分攤
  網絡費用；對South Energy而言，以下兩點具有重大實質意義：
  （一）皇家擔保方案及總督察員指定授權大幅壓縮規劃審批時間表，
  消除地方議會層面的審批風險，直接降低土地獲取項目的開發風險；
  （二）「新需求配新能源」法定義務驗證了South Energy項目模型中
  已嵌入的附加性可再生能源及電池儲能調峰架構，確保按此標準
  構建的項目在法案生效首日即完全預合規。【高度相關】
  資料來源：CapitalBrief |
  https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/new-energy-for-new-demand-sa-plans-data-centre-and-ai-infrastructure-act-47dfa4af-f755-494c-981a-121fed28eb67/
  資料來源：澳大利亞數據中心指數（certifiedstrategic.com）|
  https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/south-australia-data-centre-strategy-new-energy-for-new-demand

  【持續監測】ECMC 2026年9月《國家電力規則》決策（數據中心
  可再生能源及調峰強制補償要求）——正式公報尚未發布。聯邦法律
  草案目標於2027年初提交議會。博文部長於2026年8月28日確認
  「無豁免條款」。全國內閣（2026年8月26日）已背書制定覆蓋能源、
  用水、土地使用及技能/培訓的全國一致性強制標準。昨日發布的
  南澳州數據中心戰略表明，南澳州將在與聯邦底線標準協調一致的

🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】關鍵背景：德勤項目追蹤數據（2026年8月）顯示，
  澳大利亞已有33個數據中心項目、總投資額達1,040億澳元。在申請
  接入澳大利亞主幹電網的約9千兆瓦擬建數據中心容量中，截至2026年
  6月約84%仍處於申請階段，印證了「已完成輸電連接協議的場址」
  的結構性瓶頸溢價邏輯。新南威爾士州和維多利亞州繼續吸收大多數
  投機性項目申請；凡能夠證明已獲得南澳州邦迪級輸電連接、
  可再生能源調峰安排及規劃盡職調查已完成的項目，在全國項目管道
  中佔據可證明的稀缺地位。備注：隨著南澳州即將推出專項《數據中心
  與人工智能基礎設施法》（詳見第4類），南澳州項目的規劃通道
  與新南威爾士州及維多利亞州（目前尚無同等州級快速審批機制）
  進一步形成差異化優勢。
