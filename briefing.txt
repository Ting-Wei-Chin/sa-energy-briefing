📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 11 September 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• NVIDIA's 8 Australian AI Factory Partners Face Grid Queue Reality Check —
  Pre-Secured Transmission Connections Are the Gating Asset [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  Post-announcement analysis published on 10 September 2026 reveals that
  NVIDIA's declared 2GW Australian buildout by 2027 is constrained by a
  structural grid connection bottleneck: as of June 2026, 66.9 GW of
  prospective data centre and large-load projects have submitted connection
  requests across the National Electricity Market, with a 40% historical
  dropout rate and AEMO's construction capacity lagging well behind declared
  demand; analysis concludes that most of NVIDIA's 8 named Australian
  partners — including several without secured transmission connection
  agreements — cannot realistically be energised before the 2027 target;
  for South Energy, this is the most direct external validation yet that
  a pre-secured transmission connection agreement (the Bundey 330 kV
  four-feeder benchmark being the current market reference) is the actual
  gating asset in Australian AI infrastructure development — not capital,
  GPU supply, land area, or partner relationships alone — and that
  projects with completed connection agreements command a structural premium
  over the overwhelming majority of the declared pipeline.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: TechTimes |
  https://www.techtimes.com/articles/327170/20260910/nvidia-calls-australia-2gw-sovereign-ai-hub-grid-queues-wont-supply-all-partners-time.htm

  [MONITORING] AEMO's call for non-network options for three newly
  actionable 2026 ISP projects remains open — submissions close Thursday
  17 September 2026. Sites with secured transmission connections and
  integrated renewable firming should assess eligibility.

  [MONITORING] ElectraNet's 2026 TAPR forecasts increased connection
  demand from Greater Adelaide, the Mid North, and South East SA driven
  by data centres, AI growth, and defence industry expansion; three new
  transmission projects are proposed, including the $3.5B Northern
  Transmission Project spanning northern and eastern SA.

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 meeting on NER changes mandating data
  centre renewable and firming offsets — outcome not yet published as of
  11 September. Six jurisdictions backed the framework; QLD and NT
  dissented at the 28 July meeting. Federal legislation targeted for
  early 2027. Watch for the official communiqué from energy.gov.au.


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] Goyder Battery Stage 1 (200 MW / 800 MWh) construction
  commenced at ElectraNet's Bundey substation in August 2026, co-located
  with IREN's 800 MW data centre campus transmission connection (four
  330 kV feeder exits). Co-location of large-scale BESS firming with
  the highest-capacity grid connection node in regional SA continues to
  be the leading site-quality signal for hyperscale buyer criteria.


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] NVIDIA announced on 9 September 2026 a collaboration with
  eight Australian AI infrastructure partners — including IREN, Firmus,
  NEXTDC, AirTrunk, CDC, Sharon AI, ResetData, and Megaport — targeting
  a 2 GW AI factory buildout by 2027; IREN's 800 MW Bundey campus (SA)
  and Firmus's Project Southgate sites near Tailem Bend and Port Augusta
  (SA) are explicitly named. See 10 September briefing for full detail.
  Today's grid queue analysis (Category 1) confirms that SA projects with
  secured connections are among the small minority of this coalition that
  can actually deliver on the 2027 timeline.

  [MONITORING] US tech firms including Google, Apple, and Meta have shown
  stronger-than-expected interest in Australia, drawn by its green grid
  and proximity to Asia (Bloomberg, 7 September 2026); hyperscalers have
  committed approximately A$45 billion to Australian digital infrastructure
  in a 22-month window.


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• Federal Government's Mandatory Data Centre Energy and Water Rules Will
  NOT Be Retrospective — Dozens of Approved Projects Escape New Standards;
  Two Parliamentary Inquiries to Report in 2026 [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  ABC News analysis published 11 September 2026 reveals that dozens of
  already-approved but not-yet-built AI data centre projects will escape
  the federal government's proposed mandatory restrictions on energy and
  water use because the new rules are not expected to apply retrospectively;
  ABC review of planning records and company documents shows this new
  generation of data centres will be significantly larger and more
  power-hungry than existing facilities; Prime Minister Albanese has
  committed to passing mandatory standards legislation by early 2027 —
  meaning projects that secure planning approval before that date may
  qualify for grandfathering; separately, two federal parliamentary
  inquiries (into data centres and into AI respectively) are currently
  examining the sector and will report back later in 2026; for South
  Energy, the non-retrospective intent creates a clear strategic window:
  projects that obtain SA Coordinator General approval under the new
  Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act in 2026 will have been assessed
  under the current (non-mandatory) framework and may lock in pre-
  legislation status — directly increasing the premium of South Energy's
  project pipeline for buyers seeking to avoid the mandatory standards
  applicable to new approvals from 2027 onward.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ABC News |
  https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-11/the-ai-data-centres-set-to-escape-planned-restrictions/107097556

  [MONITORING] South Australia released its dedicated data centre strategy
  and announced the Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act on 8 September
  2026; crown sponsorship pathway and Coordinator General designation
  powers are now active, compressing planning timelines and eliminating
  council-level approval risk. (Covered in detail in the 9 September
  briefing.)

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 NER decision (data centre renewable
  and firming mandatory offset requirement) — formal communiqué pending.
  Federal draft legislation targeting early 2027. QLD and NT dissented
  at the 28 July ECMC meeting; six remaining jurisdictions backed the
  nationally consistent framework. Minister Bowen confirmed "no
  carve-outs" on 28 August 2026.

  [MONITORING] AEMC draft rule on new technical grid standards for data
  centres (30 MW+ threshold; facilities must ride through grid faults
  rather than disconnect) — submissions closed 7 May 2026; final rule
  expected to be considered at the ECMC September 2026 meeting. This is
  a separate regulatory stream from the renewable/firming offset NER
  changes; both are on the ECMC September agenda. Outcome pending.


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] Firmus Technologies' Project Southgate (confirmed active
  as of the NVIDIA 9 September announcement) names Tailem Bend and Port
  Augusta as SA AI factory sites, indicating active site-selection
  activity in both the Murray Mallee corridor and Upper Spencer Gulf.

  [MONITORING] Key background context: Deloitte's project tracker
  (August 2026) counted 33 Australian data centre projects worth a
  combined A$104 billion. Of the approximately 9 GW of proposed data
  centre capacity seeking transmission connections on Australia's main
  grid, approximately 84% remained at the application stage as of
  June 2026, confirming a structural bottleneck that favours sites with
  completed transmission connection agreements. With SA now introducing
  a dedicated Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act (Coordinator General
  fast-track active), SA projects are further differentiated from NSW
  and VIC where no equivalent state-level mechanism exists.

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年9月11日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• 英偉達澳大利亞8家人工智能工廠合作夥伴面臨電網排隊現實考驗——
  預先確保輸電連接協議才是核心稀缺資產【高度相關】：
  2026年9月10日發布的英偉達公告後續分析揭示，英偉達宣稱的
  「2027年前澳大利亞2吉瓦建設目標」受到結構性電網接入瓶頸的
  嚴重制約：截至2026年6月，全國電力市場（NEM）已接獲合計
  66.9吉瓦的擬建數據中心及大型負荷接入申請，歷史淘汰率高達
  40%，且AEMO確認電網建設產能遠遠落後於申報需求；分析得出
  結論：英偉達8家澳大利亞命名合作夥伴中，多數尚未持有已確認
  的輸電連接協議，在現實條件下無法在2027年目標前完成通電接入；
  對South Energy而言，這是迄今為止來自外部的最有力驗證——
  已確認的輸電連接協議（以邦迪330千伏四饋線基準為現行市場
  參考）才是澳大利亞人工智能基礎設施開發的真正稀缺門控資產，
  而非僅憑資本、GPU供應、土地面積或合作關係單一要素；在
  絕大多數申報項目仍停留在排隊階段的市場格局下，持有已完成
  連接協議的項目擁有結構性溢價。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：TechTimes |
  https://www.techtimes.com/articles/327170/20260910/nvidia-calls-australia-2gw-sovereign-ai-hub-grid-queues-wont-supply-all-partners-time.htm

  【持續監測】AEMO就2026年綜合系統規劃三項新可行動項目發出
  非電網解決方案徵集——截止日期仍為2026年9月17日（星期四）。
  已獲輸電連接協議並集成可再生能源調峰的場址應評估申請資格。

  【持續監測】ElectraNet 2026年輸電充足性規劃報告（TAPR）預測，
  受數據中心、人工智能增長及國防產業擴張驅動，大阿德萊德、
  北部中部及南澳州東南部的電網接入需求將顯著上升；報告提議
  三項新輸電項目，包括橫跨南澳北部及東部的35億澳元北部輸電項目。

  【持續監測】能源與氣候變化部長理事會（ECMC）2026年9月會議
  關於強制要求數據中心完成可再生能源及調峰補償的《國家電力
  規則》修訂案——截至9月11日正式公報尚未發布。六個司法管轄區
  支持全國統一框架，昆士蘭州和北領地在7月28日會議上持異議立場。
  聯邦立法目標於2027年初提交議會。請關注energy.gov.au官方公報。


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】Neoen高德爾電池儲能項目一期（200兆瓦/800兆瓦時）
  已於2026年8月在ElectraNet邦迪變電站啟動建設，與IREN 800兆瓦
  數據中心園區的輸電連接協議（四個330千伏饋線出口）同址共建。
  大規模電池儲能調峰基礎設施與南澳州區域最高容量電網接入節點
  的同址集中，持續作為超大規模雲端買家選址標準的領先質量信號。


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】英偉達於2026年9月9日宣布與澳大利亞8家人工智能
  基礎設施合作夥伴——包括IREN、Firmus、NEXTDC、AirTrunk、CDC、
  Sharon AI、ResetData及Megaport——合作，目標於2027年前建設
  最高達2吉瓦的人工智能工廠容量；南澳州IREN邦迪800兆瓦園區
  及Firmus「南門計劃」塔萊姆本德和阿古斯塔港附近場址已明確
  點名。詳情見9月10日簡報。今日電網排隊分析（第一類）進一步
  確認，在上述聯盟中，已持有南澳州輸電連接協議的項目是極少數
  真正具備2027年如期交付能力的項目之一。

  【持續監測】包括谷歌、蘋果及Meta在內的美國科技巨頭對澳大利亞
  展現出超出預期的濃厚興趣，受其清潔電網及亞太地理優勢吸引
  （彭博社，2026年9月7日）；超大規模雲端客戶在22個月內已向
  澳大利亞數字基礎設施承諾約450億澳元投資。


📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 聯邦政府強制性數據中心能源和用水標準將不具追溯效力——
  數十個已獲批項目得以豁免；兩項議會調查將於2026年底報告
  【高度相關】：ABC新聞於2026年9月11日發布的分析揭示，
  數十個已獲規劃審批但尚未開工建設的人工智能數據中心項目，
  將因新規不具追溯效力而豁免於聯邦政府擬議的強制性能源和
  用水限制；ABC對規劃文件和企業材料的審查顯示，這一新世代
  數據中心的規模和能耗將遠超現有設施；總理阿爾巴尼斯承諾
  於2027年初前通過強制性標準立法——這意味著在該日期前獲得
  規劃審批的項目可能取得豁免資格；另外，聯邦兩個議會調查
  委員會（分別針對數據中心和人工智能議題）目前正在審查該
  行業，並將於2026年底前提交報告；對South Energy而言，
  「不追溯」意圖創造了清晰的戰略時間窗口：通過南澳州
  《數據中心與人工智能基礎設施法》總督察員審批通道在2026年
  取得規劃許可的項目，將在現行（非強制）框架下完成審批，
  可能鎖定立法前身份——直接提升South Energy項目組合對尋求
  規避2027年後新審批項目適用之強制標準的買家而言的溢價。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：ABC新聞 |
  https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-11/the-ai-data-centres-set-to-escape-planned-restrictions/107097556

  【持續監測】南澳州於2026年9月8日發布專項數據中心戰略並宣布
  《數據中心與人工智能基礎設施法》；皇家擔保規劃通道及總督察員
  指定授權已正式啟動，大幅壓縮規劃審批時間表、消除地方議會
  審批風險。（詳見9月9日簡報。）

  【持續監測】ECMC 2026年9月《國家電力規則》決策（數據中心
  可再生能源及調峰強制補償要求）——正式公報尚未發布。聯邦法律
  草案目標於2027年初提交議會。昆士蘭州和北領地在7月28日ECMC
  會議上持異議立場，其餘六個司法管轄區支持全國統一框架。
  博文部長於2026年8月28日確認「無豁免條款」。

  【持續監測】澳大利亞能源市場委員會（AEMC）就30兆瓦以上數據
  中心電網接入技術標準（要求設施在電網故障時保持接入而非斷開）
  提出的規則修訂草案——諮詢期於2026年5月7日截止，最終規則
  預計由ECMC在2026年9月會議上審議；此規則修訂流程與可再生
  能源/調峰補償NER修訂為不同的獨立監管流程，但兩者均列入
  ECMC九月議程。結果待公布。


🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】Firmus科技「南門計劃」（根據英偉達9月9日公告
  確認仍在積極推進）點名塔萊姆本德及阿古斯塔港作為南澳州
  人工智能工廠場址，表明在莫雷馬利走廊和上斯賓塞灣地區均存在
  積極的場址選擇活動。

  【持續監測】關鍵背景：德勤項目追蹤數據（2026年8月）顯示，
  澳大利亞已有33個數據中心項目、總投資額達1,040億澳元。在申請
  接入澳大利亞主幹電網的約9千兆瓦擬建數據中心容量中，截至
  2026年6月約84%仍處於申請階段，印證了「已完成輸電連接協議
  場址」的結構性瓶頸溢價邏輯。隨著南澳州推出《數據中心與
  人工智能基礎設施法》（總督察員快速審批通道已啟動），南澳州
  項目與目前尚無同等州級機制的新南威爾士州和維多利亞州之間的
  差異化優勢進一步拉大。
