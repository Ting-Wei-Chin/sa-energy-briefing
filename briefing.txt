📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 17 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• Australian networks are now proposing to charge data centres for their full reserved
  connection capacity whether they use it or not — a "pay for what you reserve" model
  that makes idle grid reservations permanently expensive, driving up the ongoing cost
  of late-mover connection strategies; locking in connection terms before this cost model
  is formalised by regulators is increasingly critical for South Energy's SA pipeline sites.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/network-seeks-to-charge-data-centres-for-full-grid-connection-capacity-whether-they-use-it-or-not/

• Australia now has 9 gigawatts of data centre projects seeking grid connection — even as
  existing data centres are already delivering a measurable benefit by absorbing SA's midday
  solar surplus and reducing the "duck curve" — confirming that the connection queue is
  building rapidly and that early movers who secure network access ahead of this wave enjoy
  a structural scarcity advantage that latecomers will find very difficult to replicate.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-are-already-helping-flatten-solar-duck-curve-and-there-are-9-gigawatts-more-seeking-grid-connection/

• AEMO's latest reliability assessment confirms the reliability outlook for South Australia
  is improving: a previously flagged 390MW gap caused by the forecast retirement of Torrens
  Island B Power Station (800MW) in 2026–27 could be eliminated if AGL's in-principle
  agreement with the SA Government to extend the gas peaker by two years is formalised —
  a positive development for the system strength underpinning data centre uptime commitments
  in SA.
  Source: AEMO | https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/media-release/reliability-outlook-improves-timely-investment-delivery-essential


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• A new RenewEconomy analysis flags that Australia currently has no national target for
  long-duration energy storage (LDES) — despite urgently needing it to firm the growing
  renewable generation that SA already delivers at the 100%-net level; the LDES policy
  vacuum creates uncertainty for hyperscale buyers seeking long-dated, firmed PPA structures
  anchored to SA sites, and South Energy should proactively incorporate LDES project
  structuring into its land pipeline to pre-solve this concern before buyers raise it.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/australia-urgently-needs-long-duration-energy-storage-but-first-we-need-a-target/


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• A strategic analysis in RenewEconomy argues it is the five major hyperscalers — Amazon,
  Microsoft, Google, Apple, and Meta — not the renewable energy sector or federal policy,
  that will ultimately decide Australia's data centre footprint; the analysis concludes that
  South Energy's most effective path to project exit is direct business development with
  hyperscaler land and energy procurement teams rather than waiting for policy-driven demand
  to arrive in SA. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/hyperscalers-will-decide-australias-data-centre-future-not-the-renewable-energy-sector/

• Moody's has revised its global hyperscaler capital expenditure forecast upward by US$85
  billion — the seventh consecutive upward revision — with total hyperscaler capex now
  forecast to approach US$1 trillion by 2027; this confirms AI infrastructure investment
  continues to exceed consensus estimates and that the window for presenting hyperscale-ready
  SA land positions to buyers remains wide open.
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/moodys-hyperscaler-capex-forecasts-marked-up-by-85bn-to-close-in-on-1trn-by-2027/

• Singapore-based Keppel has secured the rights to a 720MW data centre campus outside
  Melbourne and is in active discussions with hyperscalers and neo-cloud operators for
  capacity commitments — demonstrating that the largest buyers are already transacting on
  gigawatt-scale Australian land positions; South Energy should present SA's superior
  renewable credentials and EnergyConnect interconnection capacity to this same buyer pool
  before Melbourne sites capture the pipeline. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/keppel-secures-rights-to-720mw-data-center-campus-outside-melbourne-australia/


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• Federal Energy Minister Chris Bowen has reframed Australia's data centre and AI
  infrastructure policy as making Australia the world's preferred "Earth-based data
  superpower" — directly contrasting Australia's 100%-renewable, high-reliability grid
  with orbital or offshore alternatives — a policy narrative that strengthens the premium
  on SA's clean-grid advantage and signals the federal government will actively market SA's
  renewable credentials to the global hyperscaler community. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/as-musk-plans-to-send-ai-into-orbit-bowen-positions-australia-to-be-an-earth-based-data-superpower/

• The AEMC is proposing new technical standards requiring data centres — as inverter-based
  loads — to remain connected and "ride through" grid fault events rather than automatically
  disconnecting; under a draft determination open until October 2026, these rules would
  reduce the risk of cascading blackouts from mass data centre trips, but also impose new
  compliance requirements on operators; SA projects designed to these standards from the
  outset avoid costly retrofits.
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-told-to-ride-through-grid-faults-not-trip-off-under-proposed-new-rules-to-lessen-risk-of-blackouts/

• AEMO has lodged a rule change request for real-time grid visibility of large loads
  (including data centres), with Minister Bowen lodging a complementary rule change to
  ensure data centres pay the full cost of network upgrades their connections trigger —
  both changes tightening the financial and technical compliance environment in ways that
  advantage SA projects already structured to these standards.
  Source: AEMO | https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/news-updates/digital-demand-surge


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• Switzerland's Partners Group has acquired Australian data centre operator GreenSquareDC
  — a significant institutional capital event confirming continued premium valuations for
  operational and development-stage Australian data centre assets, and validating the
  project-exit thesis central to South Energy's model: global private equity infrastructure
  funds are actively seeking to acquire development-stage land-plus-project assets in SA.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/partners-group-acquires-australian-data-center-firm-greensquaredc/

• Macquarie Data Centres has announced plans for a new 150MW data centre campus in Sydney
  at an AU$240 million land acquisition price — equivalent to AU$1.6M per MW of capacity —
  targeting hyperscale, AI, and government workloads; this is a useful comparable transaction
  benchmark for valuing South Energy's SA projects and a further signal that institutional
  buyers are actively transacting on large-scale land positions across the east coast.
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/australias-macquarie-data-centres-plans-new-150mw-campus-in-sydney/

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年8月17日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• 澳大利亞輸電網絡營運商正提議對數據中心的全量預留接入容量收費，無論實際使用量多
  少——即「按預留量付費」模式；此舉將令閒置電網容量預留長期成本大幅攀升，顯著加劇
  遲入場接入策略的財務負擔。在監管機構正式落實這一收費機制之前，South Energy應盡快
  鎖定南澳州管線地塊的接入條款。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/network-seeks-to-charge-data-centres-for-full-grid-connection-capacity-whether-they-use-it-or-not/

• 澳大利亞目前已有9吉瓦的數據中心項目正在爭奪電網接入資格——儘管現有數據中心已在消
  化南澳州正午太陽能盈餘、緩解「鴨曲線」方面發揮了可見的效益——這充分說明接入排隊
  隊伍正在快速壯大，先行者在確保電網接入後將享有後來者極難複製的結構性稀缺優勢。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-are-already-helping-flatten-solar-duck-curve-and-there-are-9-gigawatts-more-seeking-grid-connection/

• AEMO最新可靠性評估確認南澳州的電力供應可靠性前景正在改善：此前預計Torrens Island B
  電廠（800兆瓦）於2026至27年退役時將產生390兆瓦的可靠性缺口，但若AGL與南澳州政府
  就延長該氣電廠運行兩年的原則協議得以正式確立，此缺口將完全消除——對支撐南澳州數
  據中心正常運行時間承諾的電力系統穩定性而言是積極訊號。
  資料來源：AEMO | https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/media-release/reliability-outlook-improves-timely-investment-delivery-essential


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• RenewEconomy最新分析指出，儘管澳大利亞迫切需要長時儲能（LDES）來為南澳州已實現的
  100%淨可再生能源發電提供穩定調峰，但目前全國仍無長時儲能國家目標；這一政策空白使
  希望依託南澳州地塊簽訂長期有保障購電協議的超大規模服務商面臨不確定性。South Energy
  應主動將長時儲能項目結構納入土地管線提案，在買家提出此顧慮前先行解決。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/australia-urgently-needs-long-duration-energy-storage-but-first-we-need-a-target/


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• RenewEconomy的戰略分析認為，真正決定澳大利亞數據中心發展版圖的是五大超大規模服務
  商——亞馬遜、微軟、谷歌、蘋果和Meta——而非可再生能源行業或聯邦政策；分析結論指出，
  South Energy推動項目退出最有效的路徑，是直接對接超大規模服務商的土地與能源採購團隊，
  而非被動等待政策驅動需求自然流入南澳州。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/hyperscalers-will-decide-australias-data-centre-future-not-the-renewable-energy-sector/

• 穆迪已將全球超大規模服務商的資本支出預測再次上調850億美元——這已是連續第七次上調
  ——2027年預測總額趨近1兆美元；這進一步印證人工智能基礎設施投資持續超越市場共識，
  南澳州超大規模就緒地塊的市場窗口依然廣闊。
  資料來源：Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/moodys-hyperscaler-capex-forecasts-marked-up-by-85bn-to-close-in-on-1trn-by-2027/

• 新加坡吉寶集團（Keppel）已鎖定墨爾本郊外一處720兆瓦數據中心園區的開發權，並正積極
  與超大規模服務商及新型雲服務商洽談容量承諾——表明最大規模買家已在澳大利亞積極布局
  吉瓦級土地。South Energy亟需在南澳州更優越的可再生能源優勢和EnergyConnect互聯容量
  被同一買家群體轉向墨爾本地塊之前，向其呈現南澳州的差異化方案。【高度相關】
  資料來源：Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/keppel-secures-rights-to-720mw-data-center-campus-outside-melbourne-australia/


📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 聯邦能源部長克里斯·鮑文（Chris Bowen）將澳大利亞的數據中心及人工智能基礎設施政策
  重新定位為打造全球首選「地球基地數據超級大國」，直接以澳大利亞的100%可再生能源高
  可靠電網對抗軌道或離岸替代方案的可行性；這一政策敘事強化了南澳州清潔電網優勢的溢
  價，並表明聯邦政府有意向全球超大規模服務商社群主動推介南澳州的可再生能源地位。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/as-musk-plans-to-send-ai-into-orbit-bowen-positions-australia-to-be-an-earth-based-data-superpower/

• 澳大利亞能源市場委員會（AEMC）正提議制定新技術標準，要求數據中心（作為逆變器型
  負荷）在電網故障事件期間保持接入並「穿越故障」，而非自動斷開；相關草案徵詢將持續
  至2026年10月，最終確定後將直接降低大規模數據中心脫網引發連鎖停電的風險，同時對運
  營商提出新的合規要求；從一開始即依此標準設計的南澳州項目可避免代價高昂的後期改造。
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-told-to-ride-through-grid-faults-not-trip-off-under-proposed-new-rules-to-lessen-risk-of-blackouts/

• AEMO已提交規則修改申請，要求對大型負荷（包括數據中心）進行實時電網可視化追蹤；
  鮑文部長同時提交配套規則修改申請，確保數據中心全額承擔其接入所觸發的電網升級費用
  ——兩項修改均進一步強化財務和技術合規要求，令已按此標準構建的南澳州項目從中受益。
  資料來源：AEMO | https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/news-updates/digital-demand-surge


🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

• 瑞士私募基金Partners Group已收購澳大利亞數據中心運營商GreenSquareDC——這一重大機
  構資本事件確認了澳大利亞運營期及開發期數據中心資產的持續高溢價，並驗證了South
  Energy商業模式的核心——項目退出論據：全球私募基礎設施基金正積極尋求收購南澳州土地
  加項目型開發資產。【高度相關】
  資料來源：Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/partners-group-acquires-australian-data-center-firm-greensquaredc/

• 澳大利亞麥格理數據中心（Macquarie Data Centres）宣佈計劃在悉尼開發一處150兆瓦新數
  據中心園區，土地收購價格為2.4億澳元（相當於每兆瓦容量約160萬澳元），主要面向超大規
  模、AI及政府工作負荷——為評估South Energy南澳州項目價值提供了有用的可比交易基準，
  並進一步印證機構買家正在澳大利亞東部沿岸積極布局大規模土地。
  資料來源：Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/australias-macquarie-data-centres-plans-new-150mw-campus-in-sydney/
