🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] AEMO call for non-network options for three newly actionable
  2026 ISP projects — SUBMISSIONS CLOSE THURSDAY 17 SEPTEMBER 2026 (4 days).
  Sites with secured transmission connections and integrated renewable firming
  should assess eligibility now.

  [MONITORING] Transgrid's formal declaration that western Sydney's grid will
  be capacity-constrained by 2033 (RenewEconomy, 10 September) continues to
  redirect hyperscale site-selection activity to regional markets. SA's Bundey
  330 kV campus (four secured feeder exits, 800 MW committed) remains the only
  pre-secured, large-scale transmission node in any Australian regional market
  capable of absorbing hyperscale demand on the 2026–2028 timeline.

  [MONITORING] ElectraNet's 2026 TAPR proposes the $3.1B Northern Transmission
  Project (NTx), linking Adelaide to the Mid North. PADR confirmed Stage 1
  ($1.4B, Adelaide–Mid North) delivers clear net market benefits; AER has
  approved Stage 1b early works funding (land acquisition, environmental
  approvals, procurement). PACR due December 2026.

  [MONITORING] Project EnergyConnect's full 800 MW bi-directional transfer
  capability between SA and NSW expected to be released by AEMO in Q4 2026.

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 meeting on NER changes mandating data centre
  renewable and firming offsets — formal communiqué not yet published as of
  13 September 2026. Six jurisdictions backed the framework; QLD and NT
  dissented at the 28 July meeting. Federal legislation targeted early 2027.


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• Goyder Battery at Bundey Substation Doubled to 454 MW / 1,814 MWh;
  Stage 2 Site Works Under Way [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  Neoen has executed Stage 2 contracts with Tesla (Megapack 3 / Megablock
  technology) and UGL (balance of plant), doubling the Goyder Battery from
  the Stage 1 capacity previously monitored (227 MW / 907 MWh) to a combined
  454 MW / 1,814 MWh; the project connects directly to ElectraNet's Bundey
  330 kV substation — the same node anchoring IREN's 800 MW data centre campus
  (four feeder exits); site works for Stage 2 commenced August 2026 with the
  full battery expected operational in 2028; Goyder Battery will be the first
  project in the world to deploy Tesla's Megablock product, which combines
  four Megapack 3 units into a 20 MWh deployable block, reducing construction
  costs by up to 40%; the Bundey node will consequently carry both the
  highest-capacity regional grid connection in Australia and the largest
  confirmed co-located battery firming asset in SA, meeting the "firm,
  clean energy" co-location requirement being hardwired into the incoming REGO
  offset framework — a material enhancement to site quality signals for
  hyperscale buyers.

  projects without committed offtake face accelerating cost-allocation exposure
  under the proposed "full capacity charging" rule change before AEMC.

  committed approximately A$45 billion to Australian digital infrastructure
  over a 22-month window. US hyperscaler capex forecast to top $700 billion
  globally in 2026 — a 62% increase on 2025. Australian public cloud spending
  reached A$33.6 billion in FY26, up 17.9% year-on-year.

  [MONITORING] Anthropic is in active discussions for 300–500 MW of Australian
  data centre capacity, with CDC Data Centres reported as the front-runner
  and AirTrunk, NEXTDC and Firmus Technologies also in process. No signed
  lease has been confirmed as of this briefing.


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• NSW Grid Access Consultation Closes TOMORROW — 14 September 2026 (5:00 PM
  AEST) [MONITORING — ACTION ITEM]:
  The public consultation on NSW's proposed Electricity Infrastructure
  Investment Amendment Bill 2026 — which would give the NSW Energy Minister
  the power to control, restrict or prohibit data centre access to specified
  transmission infrastructure (threshold: 5 MW+ connections) — closes
  tomorrow; once closed, the volume and nature of industry submissions will
  begin to signal whether NSW's ministerial gatekeeping approach is politically
  durable or will be softened; the regulatory contrast with SA's single-pathway
  Coordinator General fast-track under the Data Centre and AI Infrastructure
  Act remains the primary SA buyer-conversion argument in competitor comparison
  discussions.
  Source: NSW Government / Energy NSW |
  https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/nsw-plans-and-progress/regulation-and-policy/public-consultations/data-centres

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 NER decision (mandatory data centre
  renewable and firming offset requirement) — formal communiqué still pending.
  Federal draft legislation targets early 2027. SA's mandatory offset
  obligation framework under discussion: data centres must surrender new REGO
  certificates to offset demand and demonstrate new firm capacity. Minister
  Bowen confirmed "no carve-outs" on 28 August 2026.

  [MONITORING] Federal mandatory data centre energy and water standards:
  non-retrospective intent means projects obtaining SA Coordinator General
  approval in 2026 may lock in pre-legislation status — directly increasing
  pipeline value for buyers seeking to avoid mandatory standards applicable to
  post-2027 approvals.

  [MONITORING] AEMC draft rule on new technical grid standards for data
  centres (30 MW+ threshold; ride-through on grid faults) — final rule
  expected to have been reviewed at the ECMC September 2026 meeting. Outcome
  pending.

  [MONITORING] AEMC proposed rule: networks seeking to charge data centres for
  their full contracted grid connection capacity regardless of utilisation
  (rule change submitted to AEMC, July 2026). Proponents argue this reduces
  transmission costs for all consumers by up to 15%. Implication for SA:
  projects with firm committed offtake (such as the Bundey 800 MW campus with
  contracted hyperscale anchor) face lower speculative cost exposure than
  uncontracted connection holders under the proposed framework.


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] AER has approved ElectraNet's Stage 1b early works contingent
  project application for the Northern Transmission Project (NTx), enabling
  land use planning and acquisition, environmental approvals, and procurement
  for the $3.1B Adelaide–Mid North transmission project to proceed. The NTx
  land corridor (Mid North to Northern SA) is entering its planning and
  acquisition phase — creating co-location and adjacency opportunities for
  projects situated along or adjacent to the NTx development corridor.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]

  [MONITORING] Transgrid's formal declaration that western Sydney's grid will
  be capacity-constrained by 2033 structurally redirects hyperscale site-
  selection activity away from NSW and toward regional markets. SA's completed
  800 MW transmission connection node at Bundey (the only connection of this
  scale in any Australian regional market with four 330 kV feeder exits) is
  the primary beneficiary of this displacement effect. Land adjacent to or
  within the Bundey catchment area is the direct land acquisition target
  aligned with this structural shift.

  [MONITORING] Firmus Technologies' Project Southgate (Tailem Bend and Port
  Augusta as SA AI factory sites) confirms active site-selection activity in
  the Murray Mallee corridor and Upper Spencer Gulf. Both corridors represent
  land acquisition target zones for South Energy.

  [MONITORING] Deloitte August 2026 tracker: 33 Australian data centre
  projects, combined A$104 billion. Of approximately 9 GW of proposed capacity
  seeking NEM transmission connections, approximately 84% remained at the
  application stage as of June 2026 — confirming the structural premium of
  completed connection agreements. The proposed AEMC "full capacity charging"
  rule will further widen the cost differential between speculative and
  confirmed connections.
