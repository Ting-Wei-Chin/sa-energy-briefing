📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 12 September 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• Transgrid Declares Sydney Grid "Effectively Full" for Data Centres — Operators
  Redirected to Regional Markets [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  RenewEconomy reported on 10 September 2026 that Australia's largest transmission
  company, Transgrid, has received over 20 GW of data centre connection inquiries
  in the past two years — twice NSW's total peak demand — with more than 10 GW
  concentrated within a 12 km radius of Sydney West alone; Transgrid has signed
  1.5 GW of new data centre connection agreements but states that existing
  transmission capacity within the Sydney metropolitan region is now largely
  committed, and its network in western Sydney will be full by 2033; Transgrid
  is formally directing developers to look at the Hunter Valley and Riverina
  regions for spare capacity; for South Energy, this is a structural demand
  displacement event — the Australian market's largest connectivity hub is
  saturated, and SA's Bundey 330 kV campus (four secured feeder exits, 800 MW
  committed capacity) is the only pre-secured, large-scale transmission node in
  any Australian region that can absorb hyperscale demand on the 2026–2028
  timeline without network upgrades or AEMO queue delay; this development
  materially strengthens the investment case for SA projects with completed
  transmission connection agreements.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy |
  https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-told-to-pack-their-bags-for-the-regions-after-house-full-sign-goes-up-in-sydney/

  [MONITORING] AEMO's call for non-network options for three actionable 2026
  ISP projects remains open — submissions close Thursday 17 September 2026.
  Sites with secured transmission connections and integrated renewable firming
  should assess eligibility.

  [MONITORING] ElectraNet's 2026 TAPR proposes three new transmission projects
  for SA, including the $3.5B Northern Transmission Project, driven by data
  centre and AI growth demand in Greater Adelaide, the Mid North, and South East
  SA.

  [MONITORING] Project EnergyConnect's full 800 MW bi-directional transfer
  capability between SA and NSW is expected to be released by AEMO in Q4 2026,
  following completion of the NSW section by Transgrid. This will further
  improve import and export flexibility at the Bundey 330 kV substation — the
  SA entry point of the interconnector.

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 meeting on NER changes mandating data centre
  renewable and firming offsets — formal communiqué has not yet been published
  as of 12 September. Six jurisdictions backed the framework; QLD and NT
  dissented at the 28 July meeting. Federal legislation targeted for early 2027.


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] Goyder Battery Stage 1 (200 MW / 800 MWh) construction is
  underway at ElectraNet's Bundey substation, co-located with IREN's 800 MW
  data centre campus transmission connection (four 330 kV feeder exits).
  Large-scale BESS firming co-located with the highest-capacity regional grid
  node in SA remains the leading site-quality indicator for hyperscale buyers.

  [MONITORING] South Australia's $32.5 billion pipeline of large-scale
  renewable energy projects (under construction, under assessment, or
  development-approved as at mid-2026) provides deep co-location and
  Power Purchase Agreement optionality for buyers seeking to meet the
  forthcoming REGO-backed offset obligations under the AEMC's data centre
  energy obligation framework.


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] The Data Center Investment Summit Australia (DCIS) is scheduled
  for 16 September 2026 at ICC Sydney — a capital-markets summit bringing
  together investors, hyperscalers, operators, developers, and policymakers
  to examine Australia's digital infrastructure investment landscape. AI-driven
  demand, power access, and grid connection timelines are the headline themes.
  Any announcements from this event next week are watch-list items.

  [MONITORING] NVIDIA's 9 September 2026 announcement of a 2 GW AI factory
  buildout with eight Australian partners (IREN, Firmus, NEXTDC, AirTrunk,
  CDC, Sharon AI, ResetData, Megaport) by 2027 — IREN's 800 MW Bundey SA
  campus and Firmus's Project Southgate (Tailem Bend and Port Augusta) are
  SA-named sites. Grid queue analysis published 10 September confirms that
  SA projects with secured transmission agreements are among the small minority
  of this coalition that can realistically energise within the 2027 target.

  [MONITORING] US hyperscalers (Google, Apple, Meta, AWS, Microsoft) have
  committed approximately A$45 billion to Australian digital infrastructure
  over a 22-month window; AWS alone plans A$20 billion by 2029. The Big Four
  have budgeted up to $630 billion globally in 2026 capex — a 62% increase on
  2025. Australian public cloud spending reached A$33.6 billion in 2026, up
  17.9% year-on-year.


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING — CLOSES 14 SEPTEMBER] NSW mandatory grid access framework:
  The NSW Government's Electricity Infrastructure Investment Amendment Bill
  2026, introduced August 2026, gives the NSW Energy Minister the power to
  control, restrict, or prohibit data centre access to specified transmission
  infrastructure (threshold: facilities with 5 MW+ connections), and introduces
  a cost-allocation framework ensuring network upgrade costs are not borne by
  ordinary consumers. Public consultation on 10 proposed reforms closes 14
  September 2026. For SA projects, this regulatory contrast is a live buyer
  talking point: NSW is moving toward ministerial gatekeeping and cost-
  allocation hurdles; SA's Coordinator General fast-track under the Data
  Centre and AI Infrastructure Act provides a pro-investment, single-pathway
  approval route that NSW does not offer.
  Source: NSW Government / Mallesons |
  https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/another-big-day-for-data-centres-in-nsw-a-new-framework-for-connection.html

  [MONITORING] Federal mandatory data centre energy and water standards: the
  non-retrospective intent (ABC News, 11 September 2026) means projects
  obtaining SA Coordinator General approval in 2026 may lock in pre-legislation
  status — directly increasing pipeline value for buyers seeking to avoid
  mandatory standards applicable to post-2027 approvals.

  [MONITORING] ECMC September 2026 NER decision (mandatory data centre
  renewable and firming offset requirement) — formal communiqué pending.
  Federal draft legislation targets early 2027. SA's mandatory offset
  obligation framework: data centres must surrender new REGO certificates
  to offset demand and demonstrate new firm capacity. Minister Bowen
  confirmed "no carve-outs" on 28 August 2026.

  [MONITORING] AEMC draft rule on new technical grid standards for data
  centres (30 MW+ threshold; ride-through on grid faults) — final rule
  expected to have been reviewed at the ECMC September 2026 meeting. Outcome
  pending.


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

  No new updates today.

  [MONITORING] Transgrid's formal declaration that western Sydney's grid
  will be capacity-constrained by 2033 (see Category 1) structurally redirects
  hyperscale site-selection activity away from NSW and toward regional markets.
  SA, with a completed 800 MW transmission connection node at Bundey (the only
  connection of this scale in any Australian regional market with four 330 kV
  feeder exits), is the primary beneficiary of this displacement effect.
  Land adjacent to or within the Bundey catchment area is the direct land
  acquisition target aligned with this structural shift.

  [MONITORING] Firmus Technologies' Project Southgate (Tailem Bend and Port
  Augusta as SA AI factory sites) confirms active site-selection activity in
  the Murray Mallee corridor and Upper Spencer Gulf. Both corridors represent
  land acquisition target zones for South Energy.

  [MONITORING] Deloitte's August 2026 tracker: 33 Australian data centre
  projects, combined A$104 billion. Of approximately 9 GW of proposed capacity
  seeking NEM transmission connections, approximately 84% remained at the
  application stage as of June 2026 — confirming the structural premium of
  completed connection agreements.

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年9月12日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• Transgrid宣布悉尼電網對數據中心「實際上已飽和」——
  運營商被正式引導轉向區域市場 【高度相關】：
  再生能源經濟（RenewEconomy）於2026年9月10日報道，澳大利亞最大
  輸電企業Transgrid過去兩年已收到超過20吉瓦的數據中心接入詢盤
  ——相當於新南威爾士州全州峰值需求的兩倍——其中逾10吉瓦集中於
  悉尼西區半徑12公里範圍內；Transgrid已簽署1.5吉瓦的新數據中心
  連接協議，但現已明確表示悉尼大都市區的現有輸電容量已基本用盡，
  西悉尼電網預計於2033年前達到承載上限；Transgrid正式引導開發商
  轉向獵人谷和里弗里納地區尋找剩餘容量；對South Energy而言，
  這是一次結構性需求位移事件——澳大利亞市場最大連接樞紐已趨於
  飽和，而南澳州邦迪330千伏園區（持有四個已確認饋線出口、800兆瓦
  已承諾容量）是全澳任何區域市場中唯一能夠在2026至2028年時間窗口
  內、無需電網升級或AEMO排隊等待即可承接超大規模數據中心需求的
  預先確認大型輸電節點；此一動態從根本上強化了持有南澳州已完成
  輸電連接協議項目的投資邏輯。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy |
  https://reneweconomy.com.au/data-centres-told-to-pack-their-bags-for-the-regions-after-house-full-sign-goes-up-in-sydney/

  【持續監測】AEMO就2026年綜合系統規劃三項新可行動項目發出
  非電網解決方案徵集——截止日期為2026年9月17日（星期四）。
  已獲輸電連接協議並集成可再生能源調峰的場址應評估申請資格。

  【持續監測】ElectraNet 2026年輸電充足性規劃報告（TAPR）提議
  三項新南澳州輸電項目，包括35億澳元北部輸電項目，受數據中心
  及人工智能增長需求驅動——覆蓋大阿德萊德、北部中部及東南部地區。

  【持續監測】項目EnergyConnect的全800兆瓦雙向輸電能力（南澳至
  新南威爾士州）預計由AEMO於2026年第四季度發布，待Transgrid完成
  新南威爾士州路段工程後。此舉將進一步提升邦迪330千伏變電站——
  互聯電纜南澳州入口節點——的進出口靈活性。

  【持續監測】能源與氣候變化部長理事會（ECMC）2026年9月關於
  強制數據中心完成可再生能源及調峰補償的《國家電力規則》修訂
  會議——截至9月12日正式公報尚未發布。六個司法管轄區支持，
  昆士蘭州和北領地在7月28日持異議。聯邦立法目標於2027年初。


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】Neoen高德爾電池儲能項目一期（200兆瓦/800兆瓦時）
  正在ElectraNet邦迪變電站建設中，與IREN 800兆瓦數據中心園區
  輸電連接協議（四個330千伏饋線出口）同址共建。大規模電池儲能
  調峰設施與南澳州區域最高容量電網接入節點的同址集中，仍是
  超大規模買家選址標準的核心質量信號。

  【持續監測】南澳州325億澳元大型可再生能源項目管道（截至2026年
  中期，在建、在評或已獲開發批准）為買家提供深度共址選項及
  購電協議（PPA）對接可能，有助滿足AEMC數據中心能源義務框架下
  即將生效的可再生能源保證原產地（REGO）抵消要求。


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】澳大利亞數據中心投資峰會（DCIS Australia 2026）
  定於2026年9月16日在悉尼國際會議中心舉行——這是一場以資本市場
  為核心的峰會，匯聚投資者、超大規模雲端商、運營商、開發商及
  政策制定者，聚焦討論澳大利亞數字基礎設施投資格局。人工智能
  驅動需求、電力接入及電網連接時間窗口為核心議題。下周峰會
  期間發布的任何公告均為重點關注事項。

  【持續監測】英偉達2026年9月9日宣布與8家澳大利亞合作夥伴
  （IREN、Firmus、NEXTDC、AirTrunk、CDC、Sharon AI、ResetData、
  Megaport）聯合推進2027年前2吉瓦人工智能工廠建設——南澳州
  IREN邦迪800兆瓦園區及Firmus「南門計劃」塔萊姆本德及阿古斯塔港
  場址明確點名。9月10日電網排隊分析確認，持有南澳州已確認輸電
  連接協議的項目屬於該聯盟中少數真正能夠在2027年目標前通電的項目。

  【持續監測】美國超大規模雲端商（谷歌、蘋果、Meta、AWS、微軟）
  在22個月內已向澳大利亞數字基礎設施承諾約450億澳元；AWS單獨
  計劃至2029年前投入200億澳元。全球四大超大規模雲端商2026年
  資本開支預算合計高達6,300億美元，較2025年增長62%。澳大利亞
  公有雲支出2026年達336億澳元，同比增長17.9%。


📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測——諮詢9月14日截止】新南威爾士州強制電網接入框架：
  新州政府2026年8月提交《電力基礎設施投資修訂法案2026》，
  授權新州能源部長對5兆瓦以上連接容量的數據中心設施控制、
  限制或禁止其接入指定輸電基礎設施，並引入成本分攤框架以防止
  電網升級費用轉嫁至普通用戶；就10項擬議改革的公開諮詢
  於2026年9月14日截止。對於南澳州項目，這一監管對比是買家
  採購決策中的現成論點：新州正走向部長審批門控和成本分攤障礙；
  南澳州《數據中心與人工智能基礎設施法》下的總督察員快速審批
  通道提供親商投資、單一路徑審批方式——這是新州無法提供的。
  資料來源：新州政府 / Mallesons |
  https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/another-big-day-for-data-centres-in-nsw-a-new-framework-for-connection.html

  【持續監測】聯邦強制性數據中心能源和用水標準的不追溯效力
  （ABC新聞，2026年9月11日）意味著在2026年取得南澳州總督察員
  規劃批准的項目，可能鎖定立法前身份——直接提升管道項目對
  尋求規避2027年後新審批項目適用之強制標準的買家的溢價。

  【持續監測】ECMC 2026年9月《國家電力規則》決策（數據中心
  可再生能源及調峰強制抵消要求）——正式公報尚未發布。聯邦法律
  草案目標2027年初提交議會。博文部長2026年8月28日確認「無豁免條款」。

  【持續監測】AEMC關於30兆瓦以上數據中心電網接入技術標準
  （電網故障時保持接入要求）的規則修訂草案——最終規則預計由

🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

  今日無新動態。

  【持續監測】Transgrid正式宣布西悉尼電網將於2033年前達到容量
  上限（詳見第一類），結構性地將超大規模選址活動從新南威爾士州
  引向區域市場。南澳州邦迪800兆瓦輸電連接節點（四個330千伏饋線
  出口——澳大利亞任何區域市場規模最大的已確認連接節點）是這一
  位移效應的主要受益者。毗鄰或位於邦迪輻射範圍內的土地是
  直接對齊這一結構性轉移的土地獲取目標。

  【持續監測】Firmus科技「南門計劃」（塔萊姆本德及阿古斯塔港
  南澳州場址）確認莫雷馬利走廊和上斯賓塞灣地區存在積極選址
  活動，均為South Energy的土地獲取目標地帶。

  【持續監測】德勤2026年8月項目追蹤：33個澳大利亞數據中心項目
  合計1,040億澳元。在申請接入澳大利亞主幹電網的約9千兆瓦擬建
  容量中，截至2026年6月約84%仍處於申請階段，印證已完成輸電
  連接協議場址的結構性稀缺溢價邏輯。
