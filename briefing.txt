🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

  29 October 2026 [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Twelve days remain to the AEMC's binding technical standard for 30 MW+ data centres
  (disturbance ride-through, system strength self-remediation or charge, alignment with
  Texas/Ireland/Finland benchmarks). Pre-secured firm transmission agreements at Bundey and
  the NTx corridor are specifically structured to satisfy these standards from day one of the
  determination's commencement. Projects still in the connection queue face the highest
  compliance exposure post-determination.

• [NEW] AEMO Q2 2026: SA Battery Fleet Sets New Price-Setting Record; LOR 3 Managed Without
  Load Shedding [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  AEMO's Q2 2026 Quarterly Energy Dynamics report confirms South Australia recorded the
  highest cap return across the NEM at A$38/MWh, with battery discharge contributing to
  price-setting in more than half of all high-priced intervals. Separately, a PD PASA LOR 3
  condition forecast for South Australia was managed entirely through post-contingent actions

  renewable penetration and storage-led reliability are now proven operational conditions,
  not aspirational targets.
