🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• The Australian Energy Market Commission (AEMC) is advancing its final technical grid
  standards for large data centre connections, with a final rule expected mid-to-late 2026
  — the framework introduces mandatory "ride-through" disturbance requirements (data centres
  must remain connected and restore load within defined timeframes during voltage/frequency
  events, rather than trip off), a three-tier classification system (Tier 1 <30MW; Tier 2
  30–100MW; Tier 3 100MW+), and stronger system security standards for inverter-based loads;
  AEMC chair Anna Collyer has warned that the cost of managing grid security cannot be passed
  to electricity consumers, meaning developers must fund compliance upfront. SA connection
  applications at Tier 3 scale (100MW+) should be engineered to meet these standards now,
  ahead of the final rule. [HIGH RELEVANCE]

🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• (13 August 2026) RenewEconomy analysis confirms that global AI infrastructure
  competition is intensifying in Australia's favour: Microsoft has locked in a confirmed
  A$25 billion three-year digital infrastructure commitment, Google is reportedly weighing
  a A$20 billion investment, and Amazon has pledged A$20 billion to expand Australian data
  centre capacity — all three have publicly tied their Australian ambitions to renewable
  energy, and South Australia's grid (currently averaging 75% variable renewables, targeting
  100% net renewable by end-2027) is the only Australian state capable of meeting hyperscaler
  net-zero procurement requirements without qualifying regulatory risk, making SA sites the
  highest-value assets for South Energy's pipeline. [HIGH RELEVANCE]

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• Federal Energy Minister Chris Bowen confirmed the Albanese government will legislate
  to override Queensland and the Northern Territory's rejection of the July 28 Ministerial
  Council agreement, mandating that all new large data centres must invest in additional
  renewable generation and firming capacity to fully offset their electricity demand ("BYO
  renewables") — AEMC National Electricity Rule change requests are on track for
  consideration in September 2026; the federal override creates a definitive two-speed
  regulatory environment in which South Australia (already renewable-first, already advancing
  its Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act) is positioned as the only immediately compliant,
  low-regulatory-risk destination for hyperscale AI data centre investment in Australia,
  directly advantaging South Energy's SA-based project pipeline. [HIGH RELEVANCE]

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年8月14日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• 澳大利亞能源市場委員會（AEMC）正推進針對大型數據中心電網接入的最終技術標準，
  預計於2026年中至下半年完成最終規則制定——新框架引入強制性「故障穿越」要求
  （數據中心在電壓或頻率擾動期間必須保持並網、並在規定時限內恢復負荷，不得自動
  斷網），採用三層分類體系（第一層：低於30兆瓦；第二層：30至100兆瓦；第三層：
  100兆瓦及以上），並對逆變器類負荷設立更嚴格的系統安全標準；AEMC主席科利爾
  （Anna Collyer）警告，電網安全管理成本不得轉嫁電力消費者，即開發商必須預先自行
  承擔合規費用。在南澳州申請第三層規模（100兆瓦以上）電網接入的項目，應立即按
  上述新標準完成工程設計，先行布局，以免最終規則生效後被動應對。【高度相關】

🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• （2026年8月13日）RenewEconomy分析確認，全球AI基礎設施競賽正向澳大利亞傾斜：
  微軟已落實3年期250億澳元數字基礎設施承諾，谷歌據報正評估200億澳元投資，
  亞馬遜則承諾追加200億澳元擴大澳大利亞數據中心容量——三家企業均公開將澳大利亞

  超大規模租戶淨零採購標準的州份，令South Energy在南澳的項目管線具備最高資產

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 聯邦能源部長鮑文（Chris Bowen）確認，阿爾巴尼斯政府將立法推翻昆士蘭州及北部
  地區拒絕接受7月28日部長級理事會協議的立場，強制規定所有新建大型數據中心必須
  自行投資建設額外可再生能源發電及調峰容量（「自帶可再生能源」規則），以充分抵消
  其電力需求——澳大利亞能源市場委員會的《國家電力規則》修訂申請預計於2026年9月
  提交審議；聯邦強制立法造就了明確的「雙速」監管格局：南澳州（已實施可再生能源
  優先政策、已推進《數據中心與人工智能基礎設施法》立法）成為澳大利亞唯一能立即
  合規、監管風險最低的超大規模AI數據中心投資目的地，直接令South Energy在南澳的
  項目管線受益。【高度相關】
