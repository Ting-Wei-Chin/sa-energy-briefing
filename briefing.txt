📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 13 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• The Australian Energy Regulator (AER) approved ElectraNet's $29.5 million Stage 1b early
  works funding for the Northern Transmission Project (NTx), covering land use planning and
  acquisition, development and environmental approvals, and procurement activities — a key
  regulatory milestone that de-risks the $3.5 billion project connecting the Bundey substation
  corridor to the Adelaide metropolitan load centre and directly expands grid capacity for
  SA data centre projects. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ElectraNet | https://electranet.com.au/news/australian-energy-regulator-approve-electranets-early-works-funding-ntx/
  Source: AER | https://www.aer.gov.au/industry/networks/contingent-projects/electranet-northern-transmission-project-stage-1b-early-works-contingent-project
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/electranet-gets-go-ahead-for-renewable-energy-transmission-link/


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• The Australian Government announced on 5 August 2026 it will expand the Small-scale
  Renewable Energy Scheme (SRES) to cover solar PV installations up to 1MW (from the current
  100kW cap), cutting commercial and industrial rooftop solar costs by approximately 20%,
  effective 1 October 2026 — data centre developers can now access STC financial incentives
  for on-site solar at meaningful scale, reducing facility operating costs and supporting
  renewable power commitments to future hyperscale tenants.
  Source: pv-magazine | https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/08/05/expansion-of-rooftop-solar-rebate-scheme-to-unlock-australias-missing-middle/
  Source: Clean Energy Regulator | https://cer.gov.au/news-and-media/news/2026/august/expansion-solar-photovoltaic-pv-eligibility-under-small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/businesses-score-in-australias-rooftop-solar-scheme-expansion/


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• Anthropic sent requests for proposal to five Australian data centre operators (CDC Data
  Centres, AirTrunk, NEXTDC, IREN, and Stack Infrastructure) seeking at least 1.4GW of AI
  compute capacity in a deal valued at up to A$21.6 billion — IREN's Bundey campus in SA is
  among the shortlisted contenders, Anthropic's goal is to bring at least 1GW online by end
  of 2027, and a final procurement decision is expected imminently, with CDC reportedly in
  line for the largest share (~500MW). [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: AFR (via Investing.com) | https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/anthropic-plans-to-buy-14gw-australian-data-centre-capacity-afr-reports-4775509
  Source: Australia Data Centre Index | https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/anthropic-1-4gw-australian-data-centre-tender
  Source: Blockspace | https://blockspace.media/short/anthropic-hunts-for-1-4-gw-in-australia-eyes-iren-for-colocation/

• ASX-listed 1414 Degrees, through subsidiary SiliconAurora, signed a Heads of Agreement on
  27 July 2026 for up to 1GW of AI data centre infrastructure at the Aurora Energy Precinct,
  SA — the deal covers a 1,580-hectare site with 900MW of solar generation potential, a
  pre-approved 140MW/280MWh BESS, and high-voltage connection infrastructure, to be
  developed in three stages from a 17MW starter campus to 1GW full build-out. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/1414-degrees-signs-deal-for-up-to-1gw-ai-campus-at-aurora-energy-precinct-in-south-australia/
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/1414-degrees-inks-major-data-centre-deal-in-south-australia/
  Source: Energy Storage News | https://www.energy-storage.news/thermal-energy-storage-company-1414-degrees-secures-1gw-data-centre-deal-in-south-australia/

• CommBank's August 2026 analysis estimates Australia's data centre build-out will total
  approximately A$150 billion by 2030, contributing ~6 percentage points to business
  investment growth in 2026 and ~5 percentage points in 2027, with AI data centres already
  representing 17% of Australia's total private investment — confirming data centres as the
  dominant new capital formation driver in the Australian economy.
  Source: CommBank | https://www.commbank.com.au/articles/newsroom/2026/08/australias-data-centre-boom.html
  Source: TechRepublic | https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-australia-ai-data-center-investment-boom-apac/


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• National Cabinet agreed in August 2026 on the framework for mandatory Australian Standards
  for AI covering large-scale data centres — core obligations confirmed include net energy
  generation (putting at least as much power into the grid as consumed), new renewable energy
  underwriting, full grid connection cost recovery (not passed to consumers), and water use
  minimisation; legislation expected in Parliament in early 2027, making Australia the world's
  first country with a nationally co-ordinated mandatory data centre energy and AI
  framework. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: White & Case | https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/australian-ai-update-australia-changes-course
  Source: Digital Watch Observatory | https://dig.watch/updates/australia-national-ai-standards-and-new-ai-office

• SA Premier Malinauskas unveiled a dedicated Data Centre Strategy in June 2026, proposing a
  new Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act — the legislation will designate data centres as
  essential infrastructure, create a streamlined and dedicated planning pathway, empower the
  Coordinator-General to fast-track major project decisions, and establish industry
  partnerships — materially reducing approval timelines and regulatory risk for South Energy
  project integration in SA. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: w.media | https://w.media/south-australia-proposes-dedicated-data-centre-legislation/
  Source: Capital Brief | https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/new-energy-for-new-demand-sa-plans-data-centre-and-ai-infrastructure-act-47dfa4af-f755-494c-981a-121fed28eb67/
  Source: InDaily | https://www.indailysa.com.au/news/just-in/2026/06/23/we-need-data-centres-government-all-in-on-ai-revolution


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• The SiliconAurora/1414 Degrees Aurora Energy Precinct deal establishes a second major SA
  data centre land zone alongside Bundey — the 1,580-hectare site features a three-stage
  build-out proposition (17MW starter → 200MW anchor → 1GW full scale) anchored by 900MW
  of solar potential, a pre-approved 140MW BESS, and a dedicated 275kV HV transmission
  connection; adjacent parcels within the Aurora precinct vicinity should be assessed for
  acquisition now, before the site footprint is fully locked up by the current operator. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/1414-degrees-signs-deal-for-up-to-1gw-ai-campus-at-aurora-energy-precinct-in-south-australia/
  Source: Kalkine | https://kalkine.com.au/news/announcements/1414-degrees-lands-heads-of-agreement-for-up-to-1-gw-ai-data-centre-deployment-at-aurora-energy-precinct

• With Anthropic's A$21.6B procurement decision imminent and IREN's Bundey campus shortlisted,
  the Bundey/Mid North corridor faces accelerating demand pressure — other SA operators
  developing grid-ready sites within reach of Bundey substation's 330kV infrastructure are
  well-positioned as overflow or alternative sites for the Anthropic allocation and
  subsequent rounds of hyperscale AI demand. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: AFR (via Investing.com) | https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/anthropic-plans-to-buy-14gw-australian-data-centre-capacity-afr-reports-4775509
  Source: The Silicon Review | https://thesiliconreview.com/2026/07/anthropic-150-billion-data-centre-plan-australia-14gw-ai-compute

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年8月13日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• 澳大利亞能源監管機構（AER）批准了ElectraNet北部輸電項目（NTx）第1b階段2,950萬
  澳元的早期工程資金，涵蓋土地利用規劃與收購、開發及環境審批、採購活動——此舉標
  誌著這項35億澳元項目達成關鍵監管里程碑，有助降低連接Bundey變電站走廊至阿德萊德
  都市負荷中心的輸電工程風險，並直接擴大南澳數據中心項目的電網接入容量。【高度相關】
  資料來源：ElectraNet | https://electranet.com.au/news/australian-energy-regulator-approve-electranets-early-works-funding-ntx/

  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/electranet-gets-go-ahead-for-renewable-energy-transmission-link/


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• 澳大利亞聯邦政府於2026年8月5日宣布，將把小型可再生能源計劃（SRES）的資助上限由
  現行100千瓦擴大至1兆瓦（提升10倍），令商業及工業屋頂太陽能光伏成本降低約20%，
  自2026年10月1日起生效——數據中心開發商現可就規模化現場太陽能系統申領小型技術
  證書（STC）補貼，有助降低設施運營成本，同時強化對潛在超大規模租戶的可再生能源
  承諾。
  資料來源：pv-magazine | https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/08/05/expansion-of-rooftop-solar-rebate-scheme-to-unlock-australias-missing-middle/
  資料來源：澳大利亞清潔能源監管機構 | https://cer.gov.au/news-and-media/news/2026/august/expansion-solar-photovoltaic-pv-eligibility-under-small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/businesses-score-in-australias-rooftop-solar-scheme-expansion/


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• 人工智能公司Anthropic已向五家澳大利亞數據中心運營商（CDC Data Centres、AirTrunk、
  NEXTDC、IREN及Stack Infrastructure）發出建議書邀請，尋求至少1.4吉瓦的AI算力容量，
  交易估值高達216億澳元——IREN在南澳Bundey的園區已列入入圍名單，Anthropic目標是
  在2027年底前上線至少1吉瓦容量，最終採購決定預計即將公布，CDC據報將獲得最大份
  額（約500兆瓦）。【高度相關】

• 澳交所上市公司1414 Degrees透過旗下子公司SiliconAurora，於2026年7月27日簽署意向
  協議書，擬在南澳Aurora Energy Precinct開發高達1吉瓦的AI數據中心基礎設施——協議
  涵蓋約1,580公頃土地（含初期40公頃獨家開發地塊），配備900兆瓦太陽能發電潛力、
  已獲批的140兆瓦/280兆瓦時電池儲能系統及高壓輸電接入設施，按三階段開發（17兆瓦
  起步園區→200兆瓦錨定園區→1吉瓦全規模）推進。【高度相關】

  總投資額到2030年將達約1,500億澳元，2026年為商業投資增長貢獻約6個百分點、2027年
  貢獻約5個百分點，人工智能數據中心已佔澳大利亞全部私人投資的17%——確認數據中心
  已成為澳大利亞經濟資本形成的主要新驅動力。

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 全國內閣於2026年8月就涵蓋大型數據中心的強制性《澳大利亞人工智能標準》框架達成
  協議——確認的核心義務包括：淨能源發電（向電網輸入的電量不低於消耗量）、承銷新
  增可再生能源供應、承擔全部電網接入費用（不得轉嫁消費者）及最小化用水量；相關
  立法預計於2027年初提交議會，澳大利亞成為全球首個建立全國統一強制性數據中心能源
  與AI框架的國家。【高度相關】
  資料來源：White & Case | https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/australian-ai-update-australia-changes-course
  資料來源：Digital Watch Observatory | https://dig.watch/updates/australia-national-ai-standards-and-new-ai-office

• 南澳州長馬利諾斯卡斯（Malinauskas）於2026年6月宣布推出專項《數據中心策略》，
  提議制定《數據中心與人工智能基礎設施法》——立法將把數據中心列為必要基礎設施，
  設立簡化的專屬規劃審批路徑，授權總協調員就重大項目行使快速決策職能，並建立行業
  合作機制——顯著縮短審批時間並降低South Energy在南澳項目整合的監管風險。【高度相關】

  資料來源：InDaily | https://www.indailysa.com.au/news/just-in/2026/06/23/we-need-data-centres-government-all-in-on-ai-revolution


🗺️ 5. 土地、規劃與市場動態

• SiliconAurora/1414 Degrees的Aurora Energy Precinct交易在Bundey之外確立了南澳第二
  個主要數據中心土地區域——1,580公頃選址具備完整「電力+土地」一體化方案（900兆瓦
  太陽能潛力、已獲批的140兆瓦電池儲能及專用275千伏高壓輸電接入），按三階段推進至
  1吉瓦；應立即評估Aurora Precinct周邊的鄰近及競爭性地塊的收購可行性，以免現有運
  營商完全鎖定選址範圍。【高度相關】

• 在Anthropic 216億澳元採購決定即將公布、IREN的Bundey園區已列入入圍名單的背景下，
  Bundey/中北部走廊面臨需求加速的壓力——其他在Bundey變電站330千伏基礎設施範圍內
  開發電網就緒選址的南澳州運營商，可作為Anthropic分配及後續超大規模AI需求浪潮的
  溢出或替代選址，具備強勁競爭定位。【高度相關】
