📋 SA DATA CENTER LAND BRIEFING - 20 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Land Acquisition & Project Integration
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Note: Today's searches surfaced several significant stories not covered in yesterday's briefing;
original publication dates are cited where applicable.

🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• IREN (NASDAQ: IREN), an AI computing company, has signed a formal transmission connection
  agreement with ElectraNet to support an 800MW data center campus at Bundey, South Australia
  — securing four 330kV feeder exits at the Bundey substation created by the EnergyConnect
  interconnector, reportedly with no network upgrades required; this is the largest single
  data centre grid-connection agreement in South Australian history and directly validates
  the "no-queue" grid access advantage South Energy has been positioning for the Bundey
  corridor; South Energy should immediately assess whether IREN's Bundey presence competes
  with or complements its own Bundey site, and whether IREN represents a priority engagement
  target as a project buyer. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: GlobeNewsWire (3 June 2026) | https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305925/0/en/IREN-Announces-First-Australian-Data-Center-Campus-800MW-in-South-Australia.html
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/

• ElectraNet's 2026 Transmission Annual Planning Report (TAPR) explicitly forecasts increased
  connection demand from Greater Adelaide, the Mid North, and the South East of SA driven by
  data centres and the AI boom; the TAPR proposes three new transmission projects — the
  Northern Transmission Project (northern and eastern SA), the Eyre Peninsula Upgrade
  (Cultana–Yadnarie line expansion), and the South East Expansion — all of which would
  materially strengthen grid access for industrial land in those corridors and create new
  connection opportunity windows ahead of infrastructure delivery. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/
  Source: PV Magazine Australia (31 March 2026) | https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/

• AMPYR Australia has received a 5.3.4 connection letter from AEMO for a 270MW grid-forming
  battery energy storage system near Port Augusta in South Australia — a key milestone
  confirming the project's path through the NEM connection process; the Port Augusta region
  is a core Mid North location where grid-firmed renewable supply directly supports data centre
  power security, and AMPYR's battery adds to the firmed capacity stack at a strategically
  significant node for South Energy's northern SA land positions.
  Source: Energy Storage News | https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• The South Australian Government has released more than 11,000 sq km of land across two new
  priority areas — Gawler Ranges East (~5,200 sq km on the Upper Eyre Peninsula) and Whyalla
  West (~6,500 sq km in the Upper Spencer Gulf region) — for large-scale solar, wind, and
  storage investment under Australia's first state-based renewable energy land tendering
  framework; combined, the areas are estimated to support projects sufficient to power more
  than 500,000 homes; both corridors carry strong solar and wind resources directly relevant
  to data centre site development, and developers with land in or adjacent to these areas gain
  preferential positioning within the state's renewable energy supply pipeline. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/south-australia-opens-new-areas-for-renewables-investment/
  Source: SA Government (7 April 2026) | https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/south-australia-open-for-more-renewable-energy-investment
  Source: PV Magazine (7 April 2026) | https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/04/07/south-australia-opens-up-new-areas-for-renewable-energy-opportunities/


🏢 3. TECH COMPANY DEMAND - POTENTIAL BUYERS

• IREN explicitly cited South Australia's status as "Australia's first 100% (net) renewable
  electricity grid" as the primary reason for selecting SA over all other Australian states
  for its 800MW flagship data centre campus — the most direct and public third-party
  endorsement yet of the SA renewable-grid differentiation thesis South Energy has been
  advancing in buyer conversations; as the most high-profile AI-native data centre entrant
  in the Australian market to date, IREN's SA commitment signals to all other AI-compute
  buyers that SA is the bankable renewable location, and South Energy should use this as
  explicit third-party validation in every active pipeline discussion. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/

• Partners Group has acquired Australian data centre operator GreenSquareDC, securing land
  options across strategic sites in Sydney, Melbourne, and Perth; GreenSquareDC has no South
  Australian presence, and Partners Group's entry as a well-capitalised, newly active operator
  represents a credible buyer opportunity for South Energy's SA project pipeline — particularly
  given Partners Group's likely mandate to diversify GreenSquareDC's footprint beyond the
  eastern seaboard tri-city cluster.
  Source: Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/partners-group-acquires-australian-data-center-firm-greensquaredc/


📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• Prime Minister Albanese delivered a significant policy speech on 15 July 2026 announcing
  that large-scale AI data centres in Australia will be legally required to be "net generators"
  of power — defined as putting at least as much energy into the national grid as they take
  out — going materially further than the March 2026 National Expectations framework which
  was advisory only; proposals closely aligned with this requirement will be fast-tracked
  through Commonwealth regulatory processes; South Energy's SA sites with co-located
  generation and storage assets are structurally positioned to help operators meet the
  net-generator obligation, transforming what was a marketing advantage into a legal
  compliance enabler. [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: White & Case (July 2026) | https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/australian-ai-update-pms-ai-and-data-centre-speech-key-takeaways-data-centre
  Source: Department of Industry | https://www.industry.gov.au/NationalDataCentreExpectations

• The Australian Government has separately announced mandatory AI standards for large-scale
  data centres and the establishment of a new Office of AI, creating a permanent regulatory
  body to oversee AI infrastructure compliance; the Office of AI will be the gateway through
  which data centre proposals are assessed against national interest, energy, and local
  capability expectations — making engagement with this framework a prerequisite for operators
  seeking Commonwealth-tier approvals; South Energy should monitor the documents and
  disclosures required to satisfy Office of AI review to ensure its project pipeline is
  positioned to assist buyers through this process.
  Source: GT Law Australia | https://www.gtlaw.com.au/insights/australian-government-announces-mandatory-ai-standards-for-large-scale-data-centres-and-new-office-of-ai

• AEMO's 2026 Integrated System Plan (ISP) has been finalised, confirming that Australia's
  least-cost energy transition path is renewable energy supported by storage and transmission,
  with gas retained as dispatchable backup; the 2026 ISP elevates firmed renewable supply as
  the primary system planning framework, directly validating the technology stack underpinning
  South Energy's SA site strategy.
  Source: ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/


🗺️ 5. LAND, PLANNING & MARKET MOVES

• No new SA-specific land transactions or planning approvals were identified in today's
  searches. The Partners Group / GreenSquareDC acquisition (noted in Category 3) is the
  primary market move with potential SA implications — South Energy should consider initiating
  direct outreach to Partners Group's infrastructure team regarding SA site options as a
  first-mover contact ahead of any organised market process.

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年8月20日

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• AI算力公司IREN（納斯達克股票代碼：IREN）已與ElectraNet正式簽署輸電接入協議，計劃在南
  澳州班迪（Bundey）建設一個800兆瓦數據中心園區——協議確保了EnergyConnect互聯工程所建班
  迪變電站四個330千伏出線間隔的使用權，據悉無需進行電網升級；這是南澳州歷史上最大規模的
  單一數據中心電網接入協議，直接印證了South Energy一直向買家傳遞的班迪走廊「無需排隊等
  候接入」的電網優勢；South Energy應立即評估IREN在班迪的佈局與自身班迪地塊之間的競爭或
  互補關係，並研究將IREN作為潛在項目買家優先接洽的可行性。【高度相關】
  資料來源：GlobeNewsWire（2026年6月3日）| https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305925/0/en/IREN-Announces-First-Australian-Data-Center-Campus-800MW-in-South-Australia.html
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/

• ElectraNet《2026年輸電年度規劃報告》（TAPR）明確預測，受數據中心和AI熱潮驅動，大阿德
  萊德地區、中北部及南澳州東南部的電網接入需求將大幅增加；TAPR提出三個新增輸電工程項目：
  北部輸電工程（覆蓋北部及東部SA地區）、艾爾半島升級工程（Cultana–Yadnarie線路擴建）及
  東南部擴建工程——上述工程均將實質性提升相關走廊工業用地的電網接入條件，並在基礎設施建
  設完成前形成新的搶先接入機會窗口。【高度相關】
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/
  資料來源：PV Magazine Australia（2026年3月31日）| https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/

• AMPYR Australia已獲AEMO就其南澳州奧古斯塔港附近一個270兆瓦網格化電池儲能系統發出的
  5.3.4接入函——這是確認該項目在NEM接入流程中正式推進的重要里程碑；奧古斯塔港地區是中
  北部的核心節點，電網支撐的可再生能源供電直接保障數據中心的電力安全，AMPYR電池的投入
  進一步完善South Energy北部南澳州地塊所在關鍵節點的有保障容量池。
  資料來源：Energy Storage News | https://www.energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/


☀️ 2. 可再生能源與選址實況

• 南澳州政府已釋放逾11,000平方公里土地，設立兩個新優先開發區——高勒山脈東部（Gawler
  Ranges East，約5,200平方公里，位於上艾爾半島）和懷阿拉西部（Whyalla West，約6,500
  平方公里，位於斯賓塞灣上游地區）——面向大型太陽能、風電及儲能項目招商引資，這也是澳
  大利亞首個以州為主體的可再生能源土地公開招標框架；兩個區域的合計規模估計足以支撐為
  50萬戶以上家庭供電的可再生能源裝機；兩地均具備優越的太陽能及風能資源，與數據中心選
  址開發高度契合，在上述走廊擁有土地的開發商將在南澳州可再生能源供應鏈中占據優先地位。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/south-australia-opens-new-areas-for-renewables-investment/
  資料來源：南澳州政府（2026年4月7日）| https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/south-australia-open-for-more-renewable-energy-investment
  資料來源：PV Magazine（2026年4月7日）| https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/04/07/south-australia-opens-up-new-areas-for-renewable-energy-opportunities/


🏢 3. 科技公司需求（潛在買家）

• IREN明確將南澳州「澳大利亞首個100%（淨）可再生能源電網」的定位，列為其在所有澳大利亞
  州份中優先選擇南澳州建設800兆瓦旗艦數據中心園區的首要理由——這是迄今業界對South Energy
  一直向買家傳達的「南澳州可再生電網差異化優勢」論點最直接、最具公信力的第三方公開背書；
  作為澳大利亞市場迄今最具知名度的AI原生數據中心新進入者，IREN的南澳州承諾向其他AI算力
  買家發出清晰信號——南澳州是可信賴的可再生能源首選地，South Energy應在所有進行中的項目
  洽談中積極引用這一案例作為第三方佐證。【高度相關】
  資料來源：RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/

• 夥伴集團（Partners Group）已收購澳大利亞數據中心運營商GreenSquareDC，並鎖定了悉尼、墨
  爾本及珀斯戰略地塊的選址優先權；GreenSquareDC目前在南澳州尚無業務佈局，Partners Group
  作為資本實力雄厚的新晉活躍運營商，是South Energy南澳州項目管線的潛在買家——尤其考慮到
  Partners Group拓展GreenSquareDC在東海岸三城叢集以外地域版圖的戰略需求。
  資料來源：Data Center Dynamics | https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/partners-group-acquires-australian-data-center-firm-greensquaredc/


📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 澳大利亞總理阿爾巴尼斯（Anthony Albanese）於2026年7月15日發表重要政策演講，宣布大型AI
  數據中心將被依法要求成為電網的「淨發電方」——即輸入電網的電量不得少於其從電網取用的電
  量——遠超2026年3月發布的僅具指引性質的「國家期望框架」的力度；高度符合上述要求的項目
  申請將在聯邦監管審批中獲得優先處理；配備共置發電和儲能資產的South Energy南澳州地塊，
  在結構上已具備幫助運營商實現「淨發電方」合規要求的能力，使其從單純的市場定位優勢升級
  為法律合規的核心使能者。【高度相關】
  資料來源：White & Case（2026年7月）| https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/australian-ai-update-pms-ai-and-data-centre-speech-key-takeaways-data-centre
  資料來源：澳大利亞工業部 | https://www.industry.gov.au/NationalDataCentreExpectations

• 澳大利亞政府另行宣布針對大型數據中心的強制性AI標準，並設立新的AI辦公室，作為長期監管
  機構負責審查AI基礎設施的合規情況；AI辦公室將成為數據中心項目接受國家利益、能源及本地
  能力建設要求評估的核心窗口——尋求聯邦層面審批優先的運營商必須充分對接這一框架；South
  Energy應密切跟進AI辦公室對項目審查所需文件及披露要求的最新動態，確保自身項目管線能夠
  協助買家順利通過審批流程。
  資料來源：GT Law Australia | https://www.gtlaw.com.au/insights/australian-government-announces-mandatory-ai-standards-for-large-scale-data-centres-and-new-office-of-ai

• AEMO《2026年整合系統規劃》（ISP）已完成定稿，確認澳大利亞能源轉型最低成本路徑為可再
  生能源配合儲能和輸電基礎設施，天然氣作為可調度備用電源予以保留；2026年ISP將有保障的
  可再生電力供應確立為系統規劃的首要框架，直接驗證了South Energy南澳州地塊戰略所依托的
  技術方案組合的正確性。
  資料來源：ESD News | https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/
