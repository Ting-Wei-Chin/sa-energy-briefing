📋 SA ENERGY DAILY BRIEFING — 05 August 2026
Prepared for: South Energy | Data Center Land Acquisition

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. GRID & INFRASTRUCTURE

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] ElectraNet's 2026 Transmission Annual Planning Report (TAPR) reveals 75+ prospective projects across 41 proponents seeking transmission connection in South Australia, with peak demand forecast to double from 3.3 GW today to 6.5 GW within 15 years — driven by data centres, AI, green steel and hydrogen. (Source: PV Magazine Australia / ESD News — https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/ | https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/)

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] ElectraNet's $3.5 billion Northern Transmission Project (NTx) is in early planning to increase transfer capacity between Bundey and Adelaide, and between Bundey and the anticipated Cultana load centre — a critical pathway for large-load connections in the Mid North. (Source: PV Magazine Australia — https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/)

• The Australian Government allocated AU$30 million for 'smarter grid' projects including electromobility infrastructure (announced 27 July 2026), which may support distribution upgrades across SA. (Source: Electrive — https://www.electrive.com/2026/07/27/australia-allocates-au30-million-for-smarter-grid-projects-including-electromobility/)

• AEMO's South Australian Grid Reference Transitional Service tender is underway following expiry of NSCAS contracts; 4.8 GW of battery projects — 80% of all SA connections in progress — are in the pipeline, all early-stage SA batteries are grid-forming. (Source: AEMO — https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/electricity/national-electricity-market-nem/nem-forecasting-and-planning/transition-planning/transitional-services---type-1-services/south-australian-grid-reference-transitional-service)

☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY PROJECTS

• Construction of the Goyder Battery and Goyder North Wind Farm is scheduled to commence mid-2026, adding significant dispatchable renewable capacity to the SA grid. (Source: Goyder Energy — https://goyderenergy.com.au/)

• South Australia awarded six battery energy projects Firm Energy Reliability Mechanism (FERM) agreements in the state's inaugural tender, totalling 517 MW / 4,136 MWh of dispatchable storage. (Source: SA Dept of Energy & Mining — https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/new-energy-projects-to-underpin-south-australias-capacity)

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] Genaspi Energy has won a contract to build the 300 MW Bundey Solar Farm alongside a 300 MW / 1,200 MWh battery to support the IREN 800 MW data centre campus — a combined renewable-plus-storage solution co-located with a major new load. (Source: RenewEconomy — https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/)

• The SA Government opened more than 11,000 sq km across Gawler Ranges East and Whyalla West zones for renewable energy feasibility licences (tender open until 28 June 2026), with strong combined wind and solar resources. (Source: SA Dept of Energy & Mining / ESD News — https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/south-australia-open-for-more-renewable-energy-investment | https://esdnews.com.au/south-australia-opens-new-areas-for-renewables-investment/)

📜 3. ENERGY POLICY & REGULATION

• AEMO released the final 2026 Integrated System Plan (ISP) on 25 June 2026, reaffirming that renewables firmed with storage, backed up by gas, is the least-cost path to reliable electricity supply to 2050. (Source: AEMO / ESD News — https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/)

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] The Australian Energy Market Commission (AEMC) has proposed a draft rule introducing new grid connection technical standards for large inverter-based loads including hyperscale data centres; stakeholder submissions closed 7 May 2026 and a final rule is expected mid-to-late 2026 — critical for any SA data centre planning grid connection. (Source: AEMC — https://www.aemc.gov.au/news-centre/media-releases/aemc-proposes-new-grid-standards-data-centre-connections)

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] South Australia's Technical Regulator Guidelines (introduced February 2026) require data centres of 100 MW or more to obtain certification covering power system reliability, security and stability, including mandatory backup energy systems. (Source: W.Media — https://w.media/australia-moves-to-tighten-grid-rules-for-data-centres/)

• The Mandatory Matching Obligation (MMO) for South Australia is being implemented from July 2026, with industry-led contract co-design commenced Q2 2026; outcomes will be formalised into the National Electricity Law and Rules during 2026. (Source: Ashurst — https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-april-2026-update/)

💰 4. ENERGY PRICING & SUPPLY

• Renewable generation reached 40.0% of total NEM supply in June 2026 — a quarterly high — with battery discharge more than doubling compared to June 2025, demonstrating storage's growing role in SA's supply mix. (Source: Leading Edge Energy — https://leadingedgeenergy.com.au/blog/electricity-market-review-latest/)

• SA wholesale prices averaged ~$144/MWh in Q1 2026 (vs $50/MWh in Victoria) but fell to $37/MWh — the lowest of any state — in Q4 2025 when wind, solar and battery output were strong, highlighting the volatility of SA's market. (Source: Ashurst / SA Premier — https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-april-2026-update/ | https://premier.sa.gov.au/media-releases/news-archive/sa-wholesale-power-prices-lowest-in-nation)

• AEMO confirmed the previously forecast SA reliability gap for December 2026–February 2027 is now closed, following AGL's in-principle agreement with the SA Government to potentially extend Torrens Island B Power Station by two years. (Source: AER — https://www.aer.gov.au/industry/retail/reliability-obligation/south-australia-december-2026-february-2027/t-3)

🏗️ 5. INDUSTRY MOVES (Data Centres & Large Energy Users)

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] IREN has signed a transmission connection agreement with ElectraNet for an 800 MW data centre campus at Bundey, SA (~125 km north-east of Adelaide), secured via four 330 kV feeder exits without requiring network upgrades; energisation is on track from 2028. This is one of the largest data centre campuses announced in the Asia-Pacific region. (Source: Data Centre News Asia / RenewEconomy — https://datacenternews.asia/story/iren-signs-deal-for-800mw-south-australia-data-centre | https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/)

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] AEMO disclosed a 5.4 GW Australian data centre pipeline as of June 2026, with South Australia capturing a significant share given its 100% net renewable energy target by 2027 and available transmission capacity at Bundey. (Source: Certified Strategic / AEMO — https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/aemo-5-4gw-australian-data-centre-pipeline-june-2026)

• SA Power Networks CEO publicly affirmed that South Australia's world-leading renewable energy grid makes the state attractive for AI data centres, with the company actively supporting the state's data centre connection strategy. (Source: SA Power Networks — https://www.sapowernetworks.com.au/data/326025/sa-power-networks-supports-data-centre-strategy/)

• ElectraNet's TAPR forecasts increased connection demand from Greater Adelaide, Mid North and South East regions driven by the data centre and AI boom alongside defence industry development — signalling growing competition for available connection slots. (Source: ESD News — https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/)

📋 南澳洲能源每日簡報 — 2026年8月5日
製備單位：South Energy | 資料中心土地取得部門

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔌 1. 電網與基礎設施

• 【高度相關】ElectraNet於2026年《輸電年度規劃報告》（TAPR）中揭露，目前有超過41個申請方、75個以上的潛在專案正在洽談南澳洲輸電網路接線事宜；南澳電力尖峰需求預計在未來15年內從現有的3.3 GW倍增至6.5 GW，主因為資料中心、人工智慧、綠鋼及氫能產業的崛起。（資料來源：PV Magazine Australia / ESD News — https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/ | https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/）

• 【高度相關】ElectraNet宣布造價35億澳幣的「北部輸電計畫」（NTx）進入早期規劃階段，目標提升Bundey至阿德雷德、以及Bundey至預期的Cultana負載中心之間的輸電容量——此計畫對Mid North地區大型負載接線至關重要。（資料來源：PV Magazine Australia — https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/）

• 澳洲聯邦政府於2026年7月27日宣布投入3000萬澳幣推動「智慧電網」計畫，涵蓋電動交通基礎設施，預計將支持南澳配電網升級。（資料來源：Electrive — https://www.electrive.com/2026/07/27/australia-allocates-au30-million-for-smarter-grid-projects-including-electromobility/）

• AEMO南澳電網參考過渡服務招標正在進行中（NSCAS合約到期後）；目前進入接線流程的電池專案達4.8 GW，佔南澳所有在辦接線容量的80%，且早期階段的南澳電池全部採用電網成形技術。（資料來源：AEMO — https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/electricity/national-electricity-market-nem/nem-forecasting-and-planning/transition-planning/transitional-services---type-1-services/south-australian-grid-reference-transitional-service）

☀️ 2. 再生能源專案

• Goyder電池儲能系統與Goyder北部風場的建設工程預計於2026年中開工，將為南澳電網增添大量可調度的再生能源容量。（資料來源：Goyder Energy — https://goyderenergy.com.au/）

• 南澳洲政府在首次招標中向六個電池能源專案頒發「固定能源可靠性機制」（FERM）協議，總規模達517 MW / 4,136 MWh的可調度儲能，有助穩定電網並填補化石燃料機組退役後的容量缺口。（資料來源：南澳州能源與礦業部 — https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/new-energy-projects-to-underpin-south-australias-capacity）

• 【高度相關】Genaspi Energy贏得合約，將在Bundey興建300 MW太陽能發電場及300 MW / 1,200 MWh的電池儲能系統，以支持IREN 800 MW資料中心園區的電力需求——此模式為大型負載搭配就地再生能源的典型案例。（資料來源：RenewEconomy — https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/）

• 南澳洲政府向Gawler Ranges East及Whyalla West兩大新區域開放超過11,000平方公里土地申請再生能源可行性許可（招標截止至2026年6月28日），兩處均具備優異的風能與太陽能資源組合。（資料來源：南澳州能源與礦業部 / ESD News — https://www.energymining.sa.gov.au/home/news/latest/south-australia-open-for-more-renewable-energy-investment | https://esdnews.com.au/south-australia-opens-new-areas-for-renewables-investment/）

📜 3. 能源政策與法規

• AEMO於2026年6月25日發布最終版《2026年整合系統計畫》（ISP），再度確認「再生能源搭配儲能、以天然氣備用」為2050年前最具成本效益的供電路徑，為南澳長期投資提供政策確定性。（資料來源：AEMO / ESD News — https://esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/）

• 【高度相關】澳洲能源市場委員會（AEMC）提出草案規則，為包括超大型資料中心在內的大型逆變型負載制定新的電網接線技術標準；各方意見提交截止至2026年5月7日，最終規則預計於2026年中至下半年頒布——此舉對南澳資料中心接線規劃具有直接影響。（資料來源：AEMC — https://www.aemc.gov.au/news-centre/media-releases/aemc-proposes-new-grid-standards-data-centre-connections）

• 【高度相關】南澳洲技術監管機構於2026年2月頒布指引，要求100 MW以上的資料中心取得電力系統可靠性、安全性與穩定性認證，包括強制備用電力系統及系統壓力事件期間的合規義務。（資料來源：W.Media — https://w.media/australia-moves-to-tighten-grid-rules-for-data-centres/）

• 南澳洲《強制配對義務》（MMO）自2026年7月起實施，業界主導的合約共同設計程序已於2026年第二季啟動，相關結果將在2026年內正式納入《國家電力法》及規則。（資料來源：Ashurst — https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-april-2026-update/）

💰 4. 電力定價與供應

• 2026年6月，再生能源發電量達到NEM總供電量的40.0%，創下季度新高；電池放電量亦較2025年6月增加一倍以上，顯示儲能在南澳電力供給中的角色日益重要。（資料來源：Leading Edge Energy — https://leadingedgeenergy.com.au/blog/electricity-market-review-latest/）

• 2026年第一季南澳批發電價平均約為每MWh $144澳幣，高於維多利亞州的$50澳幣；惟在風力、太陽能及電池輸出強勁的2025年第四季，南澳批發電價曾降至全澳最低的每MWh $37澳幣，顯示南澳電力市場的波動特性。（資料來源：Ashurst / SA省長辦公室 — https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/australian-electricity-and-gas-markets-april-2026-update/ | https://premier.sa.gov.au/media-releases/news-archive/sa-wholesale-power-prices-lowest-in-nation）

• AEMO已確認原先預測的南澳2026年12月至2027年2月供電可靠性缺口已獲彌補，主因為AGL與南澳洲政府達成原則性協議，Torrens Island B電廠可能延役兩年。（資料來源：AER — https://www.aer.gov.au/industry/retail/reliability-obligation/south-australia-december-2026-february-2027/t-3）

🏗️ 5. 產業動態（資料中心與大型用電戶）

• 【高度相關】IREN已與ElectraNet簽署輸電接線協議，將在南澳Bundey（阿德雷德東北約125公里）興建800 MW資料中心園區，透過四條330 kV饋線出口接入電網，無需進行電網升級；預計2028年起通電，為亞太地區規模最大的資料中心園區之一。（資料來源：Data Centre News Asia / RenewEconomy — https://datacenternews.asia/story/iren-signs-deal-for-800mw-south-australia-data-centre | https://reneweconomy.com.au/ai-giant-choose-australias-first-100-pct-net-renewable-grid-to-build-countrys-biggest-data-centre/）

• 【高度相關】AEMO於2026年6月披露澳洲資料中心接線管線已達5.4 GW，南澳佔相當大比重——主因南澳2027年100%淨再生能源目標及Bundey（Project EnergyConnect輸電線入口）的可用輸電容量。（資料來源：Certified Strategic / AEMO — https://certifiedstrategic.com/insights/aemo-5-4gw-australian-data-centre-pipeline-june-2026）

• SA Power Networks執行長公開表示，南澳世界領先的再生能源電網對AI資料中心極具吸引力，且公司正積極支持全州資料中心接線策略。（資料來源：SA Power Networks — https://www.sapowernetworks.com.au/data/326025/sa-power-networks-supports-data-centre-strategy/）

• ElectraNet《輸電年度規劃報告》預測大阿德雷德、Mid North及South East地區的接線需求將因資料中心、AI產業熱潮及國防工業發展而大幅增加，顯示可用接線槽位的競爭日趨激烈。（資料來源：ESD News — https://esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/）
