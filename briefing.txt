🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] AEMC publishes data centre energy framework — 5 Aug 2026
  The Australian Energy Market Commission publicly released its advice on 5 August,
  recommending data centres must bring their own clean, firmed energy: new renewable
  energy contracted via the REGO scheme (with additionality to close the existing-plant
  loophole) plus firming capacity. National Electricity Rule changes go to the Energy and
  Climate Change Ministerial Council (ECMC) in September 2026, with federal legislation
  targeted for early 2027. States may impose stronger requirements but cannot weaken
  the national floor.
  Source: AEMC (aemc.gov.au) | ESD News (esdnews.com.au/aemc-says-data-centres-must-bring-their-own-clean-firmed-energy/) |
          PV Tech (pv-tech.org/australias-data-centre-energy-rules-take-shape-as-aemc-publishes-framework-and-nsw-moves-to-control-grid-access/)

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] ElectraNet 2026 TAPR: $3.5B Northern Transmission project for data centre growth — 31 Mar 2026
  ElectraNet's 2026 Transmission Annual Planning Report forecasts rising connection demand
  from data centres and AI across Greater Adelaide, the Mid North, and South East, and
  proposes a new Northern Transmission project costing ~$3.5B plus increased Bundey-to-
  Adelaide transfer capacity. A new Customer Connection Guide was also published on
  30 March 2026.
  Source: ESD News (esdnews.com.au/tapr-highlights-vital-transmission-projects-for-sas-growth/) |
          PV Magazine Australia (pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/03/31/electranet-unveils-transmission-plans-to-support-demand-growth/) |
          ElectraNet (electranet.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/New-Customer-Connection-Guide-V1.pdf)

• Project EnergyConnect approaches full 800MW capability — mid-2026
  The 900km NSW–SA interconnector is nearing its full 800MW transfer capacity, with
  SA's new Bundey 330kV substation commissioned and ready to serve large industrial
  and data centre loads without requiring additional network upgrades.
  Source: Project EnergyConnect (projectenergyconnect.com.au) | Kalkine (kalkine.com.au)

• AMPYR 270MW grid-forming BESS approved for Port Augusta, SA — Q4 2026 target
  AMPYR Australia has received AEMO's 5.3.4 letter for a 270MW Northern Battery near
  Port Augusta. Financial close and construction start are targeted for Q4 2026, adding
  critical firming capacity in northern SA — directly relevant to new large-load sites
  in the region.
  Source: Energy-Storage.News (energy-storage.news/ampyr-australia-bags-5-3-4-letter-for-270mw-grid-forming-battery-storage-system/)

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] National Cabinet to consider mandatory data centre energy laws — August 2026
  PM Albanese is seeking agreement from state and territory leaders at National Cabinet
  (meeting scheduled August 2026) to mandate that large data centres put at least as
  much energy into the grid as they consume, minimise water use, and fund any additional
  water or grid infrastructure they require. Federal legislation is targeted for early
  2027. Non-compliant proposals will not receive Commonwealth regulatory priority.
  Source: Department of Industry (industry.gov.au/NationalDataCentreExpectations) |
          White & Case (whitecase.com/insight-alert/australian-ai-update-pms-ai-and-data-centre-speech-key-takeaways-data-centre) |

          Norton Rose Fulbright (nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/746b2689/new-expectations-for-data-centre-operators-and-ai-infrastructure-developers)

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] AEMC framework advice released 5 Aug 2026 — clean, firmed energy obligation
  AEMC delivered its four-point framework to the ECMC on 28 July; publicly released
  5 August. Data centres must: (1) contract for new renewable energy via REGO with
  additionality; (2) contract for firming capacity; (3) be flexible in demand response;
  (4) pay their fair share of grid connection costs. ECMC to consider rule changes in
  September 2026. Queensland and NT opposed a national floor; Energy Minister Bowen
  confirmed states may go further but not softer.
  Source: AEMC (aemc.gov.au/news-centre/media-releases/data-centres-bring-clean-firm-energy-be-flexible-and-pay-their-way-aemc-advises-ministers) |
          ESD News (esdnews.com.au/aemc-says-data-centres-must-bring-their-own-clean-firmed-energy/) |
          Energy-Storage.News (energy-storage.news/australia-advances-data-centre-energy-policy-on-three-fronts-as-battery-storage-emerges-as-key-compliance-tool/) |
          Mallesons (mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/big-day-for-data-centres.html)

• [HIGH RELEVANCE] SA plans dedicated Data Centre and AI Infrastructure Act
  Premier Malinauskas announced SA will introduce a specific Data Centre and AI
  Infrastructure Act requiring developers to "match demand growth with new, firmed
  supply." Planning reforms will be introduced alongside the Act to streamline and
  accelerate development approvals. SA Technical Regulator Guidelines (Feb 2026) already
  require 100MW+ data centres to obtain certification covering grid reliability, security,
  and stability.
  Source: Capital Brief (capitalbrief.com/briefing/new-energy-for-new-demand-sa-plans-data-centre-and-ai-infrastructure-act-47dfa4af-f755-494c-981a-121fed28eb67/) |
          w.media (w.media/south-australia-proposes-dedicated-data-centre-legislation/)

• AEMO 2026 Integrated System Plan — renewables + storage + gas confirmed — 25 Jun 2026
  The 2026 ISP reaffirms that the least-cost path to 2050 is renewable energy firmed
  with storage, backed by gas. SA's reliability gap for Dec 2026–Feb 2027 has been
  formally closed. SA implements Market Making Obligation (MMO) in July 2026.
  Source: ESD News (esdnews.com.au/aemo-unveils-2026-integrated-system-plan/) | REGlobal (reglobal.org)

• 【高度相關】澳洲能源市場委員會（AEMC）發布數據中心能源框架 — 2026年8月5日

  透過現行REGO計畫（附加性要求，封堵現有電廠憑證漏洞）採購新可再生能源，
  並同時簽訂儲能容量合約。全國電力法規修改申請預計於2026年9月提交能源與
  氣候變化部長理事會（ECMC）審議，聯邦立法目標於2027年初送交議會。各州
  可設定更嚴格標準，但不得低於全國底線。

  連接新南威爾士州與南澳州的900公里高壓輸電線路正接近800MW全額輸電能力，
  南澳州Bundey新建330kV變電站已竣工並可為大型工業及數據中心負荷提供服務，
  無需額外網絡升級。
  資料來源：Project EnergyConnect | Kalkine

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 【高度相關】全國內閣擬審議數據中心強制能源法規 — 2026年8月
  澳洲總理阿爾巴尼斯計劃在2026年8月的全國內閣會議上，爭取各州及地區領導人
  同意立法強制要求大型數據中心向電網輸入不少於其消耗的電量，同時最小化用水

  不符合規定的項目將不獲聯邦監管部門的審批優先支持。
