🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• AEMO 2026 ESOO Published — South Australia Faces 390MW Reliability Gap in 2026-27;
  AGL Reaches In-Principle Agreement with SA Government to Extend Torrens Island B for
  Two Years [HIGH RELEVANCE]: AEMO's 2026 Electricity Statement of Opportunities (ESOO)
  confirms that while the overall national reliability outlook has improved — supported by
  a 40GW pipeline of committed and anticipated generation and storage — South Australia
  faces a specific 390MW reliability gap in 2026-27 triggered by the forecast retirement
  of AGL's 800MW Torrens Island B gas peaker near Adelaide; critically, AEMO notes that
  AGL has reached a recent in-principle agreement with the South Australian Government to
  potentially extend Torrens Island B operations for two years, a development not yet
  captured in the ESOO modelling but one that, if finalised, would fully close the 390MW
  gap and maintain SA grid security during the period in which large data centre loads are
  targeting connection; for South Energy, the AGL extension deal confirms that the SA
  Government is actively managing grid security in close coordination with generators,
  reinforcing to hyperscaler buyers that SA's grid reliability is being proactively managed
  at the government level — not left to market forces — and reducing the single residual
  reliability objection that sophisticated investors raise against SA data centre projects.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]
  Source: AEMO |
  https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/media-release/reliability-outlook-improves-timely-investment-delivery-essential
  Source: Energy-Storage.News |

• AEMO 2026 ESOO: Australian Data Centre Energy Demand Projected to Grow Nearly Sevenfold
  by 2035-36, Reaching 34TWh and ~13% of National Consumption [HIGH RELEVANCE]: Alongside
  the reliability findings, AEMO's 2026 ESOO projects Australian data centre energy demand
  growing from approximately 5TWh in 2025-26 to 34TWh by 2035-36 — a near-sevenfold
  increase representing around 13% of total projected national operational consumption;
  this long-range demand projection, now embedded in AEMO's official planning framework,
  confirms that data centre infrastructure is a fundamental category of national electricity
  load planning and not an opportunistic or temporary demand spike; for South Energy, the
  AEMO projection provides the macro demand justification for any SA data centre project at
  a planning or regulatory level — buyers, lenders, and government approvals bodies will
  all reference AEMO's own figures, and a SA project positioned to serve the 10-year demand
  ramp embedded in the ESOO carries a structurally justified investment case that no buyer's
  internal model can dispute; projects in the Bundey corridor, already anchored by IREN's
  800MW campus, are directly aligned with AEMO's modelled demand growth and should be
  presented to buyers as assets already on the right side of AEMO's planning trajectory.
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• Post-National Cabinet: Australian Government Formally Announces Mandatory AI Standards
  for Large-Scale Data Centres and Establishes New Federal Office of AI [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  Following the August 27-28 National Cabinet meeting, the Australian Government has taken
  the additional step of formally announcing mandatory AI standards specifically targeting
  large-scale data centres and establishing a new federal body — the Office of AI — to
  provide oversight and coordinated implementation guidance for the emerging compliance
  framework; while yesterday's KWM analysis addressed the three-part regulatory structure
  (renewable energy requirements, grid connection standards, cost recovery rules), GT Law's
  post-National Cabinet briefing confirms the government has created a dedicated Office of
  AI as a single federal regulatory counterparty, providing the institutional anchor that
  hyperscalers and project developers now have a named body to engage with on compliance
  pathway questions; for South Energy, the establishment of the Office of AI is significant
  in three respects: (1) it provides a clear government counterparty for hyperscalers
  navigating the new mandatory requirements, reducing the regulatory uncertainty that delays
  investment decisions; (2) any SA project with a documented compliance pathway aligned
  with the Office of AI's guidance will be materially better positioned in a competitive
  sale process against projects that cannot demonstrate structured regulatory engagement;
  and (3) the Office of AI signals the Australian Government's intent to actively direct
  data centre development — a commitment South Energy should reference in its information
  memorandums as evidence of long-term government support for the sector alongside the SA
  Government's own proactive grid management (Torrens Island B extension, Crown Land
  release, Technical Regulator guidelines).
  [HIGH RELEVANCE]

📋 南澳州數據中心土地每日簡報 - 2026年8月31日
準備人：South Energy | 土地獲取與項目整合

🔌 1. 電網接入機會

• AEMO 2026年《電力機遇聲明》（ESOO）正式發布——南澳州2026至27財年面臨390兆瓦
  可靠性缺口；AGL就托倫斯島B電站延運兩年與南澳州政府達成原則性協議【高度相關】：
  AEMO發布的2026年《電力機遇聲明》（ESOO）確認，儘管整體全國可靠性前景因40吉瓦
  的已確定及預期發電與儲能項目管道而有所改善，南澳州仍在2026至27財年面臨特定的
  390兆瓦可靠性缺口——起因為AGL位於阿德萊德附近的800兆瓦托倫斯島B燃氣調峰電站
  預期退役；關鍵的是，AEMO指出，AGL最近已就托倫斯島B電站延續運行兩年的可能性，與
  南澳州政府達成原則性協議；此進展尚未納入ESOO建模，但若最終落實，將完全填補390兆
  瓦缺口，並在大型數據中心負荷目標接入的關鍵窗口期維護南澳州電網安全；對South
  Energy而言，AGL延運協議證明南澳州政府正與發電商緊密協作，主動管理電網安全——而
  非交由市場自行調節——這向超大規模客戶買家強調：南澳州電網可靠性正在政府層面獲得
  積極保障，從而化解精明投資者對南澳州數據中心項目最後一項殘存的可靠性顧慮。
  【高度相關】
  資料來源：AEMO |
  https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/media-release/reliability-outlook-improves-timely-investment-delivery-essential
  資料來源：Energy-Storage.News |
  https://www.energy-storage.news/reliability-outlook-improves-but-australias-nem-still-needs-system-security-investment-aemo-finds/

• AEMO 2026年ESOO：澳大利亞數據中心能源需求預計到2035至36財年增長近七倍，達34太
  瓦時，佔全國用電量約13%【高度相關】：除可靠性相關發現外，AEMO 2026年ESOO預測
  澳大利亞數據中心能源需求將從2025至26財年的約5太瓦時增長至2035至36財年的34太瓦
  時——增幅近七倍，約佔全國運營用電量預測總量的13%；這一長期需求預測已正式嵌入
  AEMO的官方規劃框架，確立了數據中心基礎設施為全國電力負荷規劃的基礎性類別，而非
  機會性或短暫性的需求高峰；對South Energy而言，AEMO的預測在規劃或監管層面為南澳
  州任何數據中心項目提供了宏觀需求依據——買家、貸款方及政府審批機構均將援引AEMO
  自身數據；定位為承接ESOO所涵蓋的十年需求增長的南澳州項目，其投資理由具有結構性
  支撐，無法被任何買家的內部模型所質疑；班迪走廊的項目已有IREN 800兆瓦園區錨定，
  直接契合AEMO建模的需求增長方向，應向買家呈現為已處於AEMO規劃軌跡正確位置的資產。
  【高度相關】

📜 4. 政策與法規（南澳州 + 全國）

• 全國內閣後續：澳大利亞政府正式宣布針對大規模數據中心的強制性AI標準，並設立聯邦
  人工智能辦公室【高度相關】：繼8月27至28日全國內閣會議之後，澳大利亞政府進一步
  正式宣布針對大規模數據中心的強制性AI標準，並設立新的聯邦機構——「人工智能辦公
  室」——為新興合規框架提供監督與統籌實施指引；昨日KWM的分析解讀了三項監管結構
  （可再生能源要求、電網接入標準、成本回收規則），今日GT Law的全國內閣後續簡報進
  一步確認，政府已設立專屬的人工智能辦公室作為單一聯邦監管對口單位，為超大規模客
  戶及項目開發商在合規路徑問題上提供明確的政府接洽主體；對South Energy而言，人工
  智能辦公室的設立具有三重重要意義：（1）為在新強制要求中尋找方向的超大規模客戶
  提供清晰的政府對口方，降低因監管不確定性而導致投資決策延遲的風險；（2）能夠提
  供與人工智能辦公室指引相契合的書面合規路徑的南澳州項目，在競爭性出售進程中將明
  顯優於無法展示結構化監管接洽的競爭項目；（3）人工智能辦公室的設立表明澳大利亞
  政府有意主動引導數據中心發展方向，South Energy應在信息備忘錄中援引這一承諾，與
  南澳州政府自身的積極電網管理行動（托倫斯島B電站延運、皇家土地開放、技術監管機
  構指引）共同作為長期政府支持的有力佐證。
