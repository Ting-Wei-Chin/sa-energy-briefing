  Proposals Now Queued (Early November 2026) [HIGH RELEVANCE]: Tech Insider, citing ElectraNet
  planning documents, reports that South Australia's surge of data centre connection proposals —
  now approaching 30 applications — has crystallised a ~A$5 billion, ~500 km transmission
  augmentation requirement for ElectraNet to fulfil by 2032 to service new entrants without

  multi-year build timeline falling on competitors entering the queue today. South Energy's
  pre-secured Bundey 330 kV firm transmission positions are structurally outside this upgrade
  cost — the A$5B expenditure represents the competitive barrier protecting existing firm
  agreements from new arrivals. Note: figures cited from a secondary source; verify against
