🔌 1. GRID & CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES

• SA Government and AGL Sign Funding Deal to Extend Torrens Island B to July 2028 [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  The South Australian Government and AGL Energy have executed a formal funding agreement to extend
  three units (600 MW combined) of the Torrens Island B gas power station from its previously
  scheduled mid-2026 retirement to 1 July 2028, after AEMO identified a potential 390 MW
  reliability gap in 2026–27 caused by delays to the Northern Transmission project. AEMO has
  confirmed the reliability gap is now fully eliminated with the extension in place, materially
  de-risking the SA grid for new large loads connecting over the next 24 months.
  Sources: RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/south-australia-and-agl-reach-funding-deal-on-torrens-island-gas-extension-due-to-transmission-delays/
  SA Premier's Office | https://www.premier.sa.gov.au/media-releases/news-archive/government-negotiates-energy-security-agreement
  [HIGH RELEVANCE — eliminating the 2026–27 reliability gap directly reduces interconnection risk
  for hyperscaler campuses advancing grid connections in the Bundey–Adelaide NTx corridor;
  sites with active connection agreements can now progress toward commissioning without the
  single-point reliability concern that previously threatened the 2027 connection window; the
  funded extension reinforces the Bundey–Bolivar/Dry Creek corridor as South Energy's
  highest-priority land acquisition zone]

  [MONITORING] AEMO 2026 Final Integrated System Plan: Northern Transmission Project (NTx —
  Bundey to Adelaide, A$3.1B) formally placed on the Optimal Development Path (ODP). AER
  full-project regulatory approval is now the next key milestone. NTx's ODP designation
  confirms national policy prioritisation of the Bundey corridor and positions the
  Bundey–Bolivar/Dry Creek land corridor as South Energy's highest-priority acquisition zone.

  [MONITORING] ElectraNet 2026 TAPR (A$3.94B pipeline): Three priority transmission expansion
  zones confirmed — Eyre Peninsula Upgrade (Yadnarie North 275/132 kV substation, ~A$350M,
  2027/28); South East Expansion (Tailem Bend–Tungkillo second 275 kV circuit, ~A$80–90M);
  NTx (Bundey–Adelaide, A$3.1B). SA peak demand projected to more than double from 3.3 GW to
  6.5 GW+ within 15 years. AER has approved Stage 1b early works.

  [MONITORING] AEMO Grid Reference Transitional Services commenced 1 July 2026 (replacing
  expired NSCAS contracts), maintaining system strength in SA. AMPYR Northern Battery (270 MW,
  Port Augusta) — first grid-forming BESS under the post-5.3.4 connection pathway — targeting
  financial close and construction commencement Q4 2026.

  [MONITORING] Project EnergyConnect (SA–NSW 900 km interconnector): Full 800 MW transfer
  capability targeted for mid-2026, following NSW Stage 2 construction completion by Transgrid.


☀️ 2. RENEWABLE ENERGY & SITE CONDITIONS

• NEM Sets New All-Time Curtailment Record on 21 September; 7-Day Rolling Renewable Mean Tops 50% for First Time [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  Australia's National Electricity Market set a new all-time renewable curtailment record of
  11,109 MW at 11:55 am on 21 September 2026 — 256 MW above the previous NEM record of
  10,854 MW set on 5 October 2025 — as grid constraints prevented absorption of available wind
  and solar output. On the same day, the NEM's rolling seven-day mean renewable energy share
  exceeded 50% for the first time in history, reaching 50.8% by 22 September. The two
  simultaneous milestones confirm an acute and structural mismatch between surging renewable
  generation capacity and the grid's current ability to absorb it — precisely the gap that
  large, co-located flexible data centre loads are designed to resolve.
  Sources: pv magazine Australia | https://www.pv-magazine-australia.com/2026/09/21/renewables-share-hits-record-high-in-nem-2/
  RenewEconomy | https://reneweconomy.com.au/renewables-hit-more-than-80-pct-of-grid-demand-for-first-time-despite-heavy-throttling-of-wind-and-solar/
  [HIGH RELEVANCE — record curtailment volumes at 11+ GW provide the quantitative commercial
  case for large data centre flexible loads co-located with stranded renewables in SA; sites
  with generation assets adjacent to the Bundey and Upper Spencer Gulf renewable corridors
  can capture direct financial value from curtailment absorption via PPA structures; back-to-back
  curtailment records materially strengthen South Energy's pitch to hyperscalers and the SA
  Government regarding the social and commercial value of large-load data centre development
  on SA land]

  [MONITORING] Australia's NEM broke the 80% renewables barrier for the first time in history
  on Saturday 19 September 2026 (80.5% at 12:25 pm), surpassing the previous record of 79.5%
  set the day before. Approximately 54.8 GWh of wind and solar was curtailed by 1:50 pm. SA
  was a key contributor. The back-to-back records confirm the net-generator compliance case for
  SA-sited hyperscaler campuses and validate the urgent need for large flexible loads to absorb
  stranded renewable generation.

  [MONITORING] Goyder Battery Stage 2 (454 MW / 1,814 MWh): Contracts executed with Tesla
  (Megapack 3 / Megablock) and UGL; site works commenced August 2026; operational 2028.
  Co-located at Bundey 330 kV node with IREN 800 MW campus.

  [MONITORING] SA $32.5 billion large-scale renewable energy pipeline (mid-2026) provides
  deep PPA optionality for hyperscalers meeting REGO offset obligations.

  [MONITORING] AEMO: 4.4 GW BESS fleet added since Q1 2026, tripling daily load-shifting —
  underpins firmed-renewable co-location value at SA sites.

  [MONITORING] Gawler Ranges East (~5,200 km²) and Whyalla West (~6,500 km²) renewable energy

📜 4. POLICY & REGULATION (SA + NATIONAL)

• Federal Consultation Opens: "Getting It Right — Building AI Infrastructure that Works for Australia" (PM&C, 18 September 2026) [HIGH RELEVANCE]:
  The Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet, through its newly established Office of AI,
  opened formal public consultation on 18 September 2026 with its paper "Getting it right:
  Building AI infrastructure that works for Australia." The paper proposes mandatory national
  standards for large data centres covering: (1) renewable energy and firming requirements;
  (2) water sustainability; (3) land-use and community impact; (4) workforce and skills
  development; and (5) conditions for AI training and support for Australian creatives.
  Submissions close 9 October 2026.
  Sources: PM&C | https://www.pmc.gov.au/resources/getting-it-right-building-ai-infrastructure-works-australia
  PM&C Consultation Page | https://www.pmc.gov.au/domestic-policy/office-ai/getting-it-right-building-ai-infrastructure-works-australia
  [HIGH RELEVANCE — the three-week submission window (closing 9 October) is a direct
  policy-shaping opportunity for South Energy to position SA's 100%-renewable grid, the
  Coordinator-General single-approvals pathway, and pre-secured transmission infrastructure
  as the national template for compliant hyperscaler development; a submission quantifying
  SA's comparative advantage across all five proposed standard dimensions would directly
  inform mandatory standards that could further disadvantage QLD and NT relative to SA;
  the consultation confirms the federal legislative framework will bind hyperscalers before
  they secure their next Australian data centre sites — favouring operators who can
  demonstrate SA compliance now]

  infrastructure expectations. AirTrunk, CDC Data Centres and Equinix welcomed the mandatory
  framework, with CDC noting that its standards largely reflect established industry practice.
  NEXTDC, while broadly supportive, called for an "execution-focused" approach rather than
  additional regulatory burden, citing its A$2.2 billion capital programme already in motion.
  Source: w.media | https://w.media/airtrunk-cdc-and-equinix-back-govt-data-centre-rules-nextdc-urges-execution-focus/

• 聯邦開放徵詢：《正確推進——建設服務澳大利亞的AI基礎設施》（總理府，2026年9月18日）【高度相關】：

  諮詢文件。文件提出針對大型數據中心的強制性國家標準草案，涵蓋：（1）可再生能源

  2026年10月9日。

  接入等優勢，將其確立為符合全國強制性標準的超大規模雲端商開發範本；從五項擬議
  標準維度全面論證南澳州相對優勢的意見書，可直接影響最終標準文本，進一步拉大南澳
  州相對昆士蘭州及北領地的合規差距；此次諮詢確認聯邦立法框架將在超大規模雲端商
  鎖定下一個澳大利亞數據中心場址之前正式約束其行為——利好現時已能展示南澳州合規
  能力的開發商】

  Data Centres及Equinix均對框架表示歡迎，其中CDC指出相關標準基本反映了行業現有
  慣例，並非額外負擔。NEXTDC雖總體支持，但呼籲採取「重在執行」的方式，避免增加
  新的監管負擔，並指出其22億澳元資本計劃已在推進中。

  South Energy的開發模式——預先鎖定輸電接入、同址配置可再生能源、南澳州總督察員

  拒絕採納強制性數據中心標準中的可再生能源捆綁要求，允許兩州數據中心繼續使用煤電
  和天然氣。南澳州憑藉100%淨可再生能源目標及全面清潔電網，默認符合聯邦能源標準，

  連接的可再生能源豐富選址，方可在接入時無需額外資本支出即全面滿足三項義務。
